_One Of Only 35 Black Female Founders To Raise $1 Million In Venture Funding, Edwards Helps Youth Of Color Heal From Trauma_ 

**NEW YORK and NEWARK, NJ – August 24, 2020 –** Committed to making mental-health support radically accessible and inclusive for communities of color, Ashley Edwards, a Forbes Under 30 lister in 2018, founded MindRight Health, providing trauma-informed and culturally responsive mental health coaching via text message. Less than a year after launching, her company, which delivers critical daily support, announced it has raised more than $1 million in venture-capital funding, making Edwards one of only 35 Black female founders in the nation to achieve such funding and the first to do so in New Jersey. The announcement comes on the heels of the [Forbes Under 30 global nominations](https://www.forbes.com/30-under-30-nominations/ "https://www.forbes.com/30-under-30-nominations/") standardization, which ensure quality and accuracy for Forbes Under 30 lists worldwide.

Notable investors in MindRight include: angel investor Esther Dyson, executive founder of Wellville; Newark (NJ) Venture Partners, an early-stage VC firm that works with leading business-to-business companies; Telosity, a fund launched by Vinaj Ventures & Innovation that invests in companies improving children’s and young adults’ mental health; Acumen America, which offers seed capital and support for mission-driven founders, and Impact America Fund, which invests in exceptional founders whose businesses create new frameworks of ownership and opportunity and generate meaningful returns. MindRight has also developed a unique partnership with the renowned RWJBarnabas Health (NJ) system to increase access to mental health care among the children of workers on the frontlines of the pandemic.

The funding comes two years after Forbes named Edwards to its prestigious [30 Under 30 List](https://www.forbes.com/profile/ashley-edwards/#67705b2515bd "https://www.forbes.com/profile/ashley-edwards/#67705b2515bd") in the social entrepreneurs category, which celebrates leaders who are leveraging business and entrepreneurship to create a more just society. “MindRight meets teens where they are by providing personalized, evidence-informed mental-health coaching over text message,” **Forbes** wrote in 2018. “Unlike crisis hotlines, MindRight’s coaches proactively check in with teens daily, reaching them on both their good and bad days, helping them develop positive coping skills.”

Shortly after her appearance on Forbes’ Under 30 list, Edwards was featured in a [Cole Haan Changemakers series](https://www.forbes.com/sites/colehaan/2018/11/06/behind-the-nonprofit-that-coaches-at-risk-students-and-heals-trauma--one-text-at-a-time/#577a3bcb5912 "https://www.forbes.com/sites/colehaan/2018/11/06/behind-the-nonprofit-that-coaches-at-risk-students-and-heals-trauma--one-text-at-a-time/#577a3bcb5912"), produced in partnership with Forbes BrandVoice. The Changemakers series detailed how Edwards turned her passion for helping teens into a successful with MindRight Foundation, a nonprofit that served as the inspiration behind her company MindRight Health. “MindRight Foundation is committed to connecting families to emergency financial assistance given the pandemic’s impact on financial instability. The non-profit expects to provide at least $10,000 in emergency funding to families in need,” Edwards said then in describing her mission.

“Today, our mission is more critical than ever, as the pandemic and the social justice movement magnify the urgent need to address and reverse centuries of systemic racism,” she said. “While working for the non-profit, I became acutely aware that trauma disproportionately affects low-income families and communities of color, and today at MindRight Health, we’ve set out to re-imagine mental health services to meet the untapped market needs of a rapidly diversifying nation.”

Recognizing the power of her platform, leading investors across the country are backing Edwards in her mission.

**Esther Dyson, Executive Founder of Wellville**: “Ashley is focused on the right things, not just quick growth, but actually making an impact on the well-being of the service’s users. She has built a service with the knowledge that many of their mental health challenges reflect of the realities they face every day.” 

**Daniel Borok, Managing Partner at Newark Venture Partners:** “We invested in MindRight Health because we believed in Ashley and her vision for providing culturally responsive mental-health coaching to underserved communities. With a focus on health-tech, Newark Venture Partners works closely with RWJ Barnabas Health, the largest healthcare system in New Jersey, and Horizon Blue Cross Blue Shield of New Jersey as investors to find promising startups that impact community health and address social determinants of health. MindRight does just that by providing a critical coaching service in a highly scalable way, the need for which has grown exponentially in 2020.”

**Eliza Golden Roady, Associate Director at Acumen America:** “Acumen America is inspired by entrepreneurs like Ashley, who are tackling the rampant disparities in our health system by building the products and services that can reimagine access to quality care for low-income communities and communities of color. We believe that healthcare services designed around the principals of inclusivity, accessibility and prevention form the backbone of a tremendous market opportunity to help health plans provide more cost-effective care for their members while substantially improving outcomes.”

**Amy B. Mansue, Executive Vice President and Chief Experience Officer at RWJBarnabas Health:** “MindRight is providing a crucial service to the children of our team members during the COVID-19 Pandemic. Our frontline staff have selflessly given of themselves during this crisis and it is our obligation to ensure that while they are caring for our patients, we are supporting them and their families by providing access to the experts at MindRight. This service allows these children to receive coaching via text, which gives their parents the peace of mind from knowing that while their teenagers may not be talking to them, they are talking to an expert to give them support during these times.”

In January, Forbes established its [Representation and Inclusion Practice](https://www.forbes.com/sites/forbespr/2020/06/23/forbes-representation--inclusion-practice-unites-marketers-with-leading-voices-to-challenge-systems--processes-for-true-equity/#6e5dbb554e5b "https://www.forbes.com/sites/forbespr/2020/06/23/forbes-representation--inclusion-practice-unites-marketers-with-leading-voices-to-challenge-systems--processes-for-true-equity/#6e5dbb554e5b"), which unites marketers with leading voices who are challenging systems for the purpose of driving meaningful systemic change for underrepresented identities in business and entrepreneurship. **Sade Muhammad**, Forbes’ Director of Representation and Inclusion Partnerships, underscored the importance of spotlighting socially driven entrepreneurs like Edwards.

“True change comes when we have the critical conversations that address centuries of racial injustice directly and simultaneously champion the leaders who are taking action to ensure a more equitable future for historically underserved communities,” Muhammad said. “All of us bear the responsibility to tell the right stories, to leverage the right voices and to compel change – and that also includes calling on the venture capital community to meaningfully fund other Black and Brown entrepreneurs so that their world-changing products and services can get off the ground and into the marketplace. Forbes is dedicated to using our megaphone across all platforms to drive systemic change in business, culture and society.”

**About MindRight**

MindRight Health meets young people where they are by providing mental health coaching over text message. Members receive a personal team of trauma-informed coaches, supervised by licensed clinicians, who proactively support them 7 days/week, 365 days/year. Designed for underserved communities, MindRight breaks historical barriers of access to care and mental health stigma by providing a continuum of culturally-responsive, nonclinical support. MindRight provides health partners with real-time community wellness data to address social determinants of health and prevent crises. Founded by Ashley Edwards in 2019, with the mission to advance health equity by making mental health care radically accessible and inclusive of communities of color and low-income families, MindRight Health is headquartered in Newark, NJ. To learn more, visit [https://www.mindright.io/](https://www.mindright.io/ "https://www.mindright.io/").
