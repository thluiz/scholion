# **T**he Future Is Graph Databases

## A conversation with the founder of Neo4J, Emil Eifrem

Emil Eifrem [@emileifrem](https://twitter.com/emileifrem)  is the Founder of Neo4j and CEO of Neo Technology**.** He is also one of the authors of [*Graph Databases*](http://shop.oreilly.com/product/0636920028246.do) **.** Recently, I had the opportunity to sit down with Emil and we talked about the current and future opportunities for graph databases**.**

Key highlights include:

- Emil explains graph databases**.** [Discussed at [0:29](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Dr0KfJXqMbs&#t=0m29s)]
- [Facebook Graph Search](https://www.facebook.com/about/graphsearch)  is a well-known example of a graph database**.** [Discussed at [3:28](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Dr0KfJXqMbs&#t=3m28s)]
- But really, graph databases can be used much more than social search**.** [Discussed at [4:50](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Dr0KfJXqMbs&#t=4m350s)]
- Neo4j is the original graph database**.** [Discussed at [5:25](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Dr0KfJXqMbs&#t=5m25s)]
- Graph databases “shape” data**.** [Discussed at [6:20](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Dr0KfJXqMbs&#t=6m20s)]
