# **F**ix for Skype Mic volume increasing when Sc2 launches**.**

Hi, I was searching the forums and noticed some people was having the same problem I was; If I launched SC2 while on skype to a friend, the mic volume would shoot to 100% and pretty much deafen the friend listening**.** After fiddling about with a few things, I found a solution, albeit temporary**.** Please follow this step by step**.**  
  
Windows Part:  
  
1. Right Click the Volume Control option in the Windows Toolbar (Unsure if this is in MAC) and select Recording devices**.**  
  
2. Locate the microphone you are using, right click the microphone and click Properties**.**  
  
3. Navigate to advanced and uncheck "Allow applications to take exclusive control of this device"**.**  
  
Skype Part:  
  
1. Open Skype and navigate to Tools>Options  
  
2**.** Click Advanced and then click Advanced Settings, Click Manage other Program's Access to Skype**.**  
  
3**.** Remove anything that is listed in the box that appears, Click OK**.** Click Save and close options**.**  
  
4. Go to Tools > Options > Audio Settings and uncheck Automatically Adjust**.**  
  
Starcraft 2 Part:  
  
1. Launch Starcraft 2 and navigate to the options**.**  
  
2. Click Voice and Enable Voice.  
  
3. Set the Microphone Volume to the same settings as Skype (For Example I have the bar on the first notch in Skype, which is 10% so I set the SC2 settings to 10% also)**.** Make sure Automatic is unticked next to Microphone volume**.**  
  
4**.** At the bottom of the Voice settings change provider to FMod, save settings**.**  
  
5**.** If you don't want people to hear you on SC2, mute the microphone next to the portrait**.** This should fix the problem as it has for me, I hope it works for everyone until Blizzard introduces a fix**.**
