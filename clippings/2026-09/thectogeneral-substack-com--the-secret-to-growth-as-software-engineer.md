As I read through my old code and reviewed my previous work, I realized something very important.

I recognized how much I had grown and how unaware I once was of writing clean code and implementing the best practices for solving technical problems.

My growth has been so significant that it’s now clear how much I’ve improved over the past years. I felt a bit embarrassed by the things I did when I was still learning.

I wrote code and built software the best way I knew how at the time (which was fine).

I finally understood what growth truly is:

*   It’s comparing your present self to your past and recognizing how much you’ve improved in what you typically do.
    
*   It’s realizing that you now know a better approach to something you once did differently.
    
*   It’s understanding the steps you should have taken to prevent a particular situation and adjusting so that you don’t encounter the same result.
    

This is the measure of growth.

There’s a popular quote that says, ‘Comparison is the thief of joy’—maybe.

Here’s the truth:

Comparing yourself to others can indeed steal your joy and leave you in a displeasing emotional state—**this isn’t growth**.

But comparing yourself to your past self shouldn’t steal your joy—**this is what growth truly is.**

Take a moment to assess your old self and measure your growth. This could bring you joy this week. 😊

Documenting your journey helps you track how much you’ve improved, giving you something to compare your current or future self to.

There’s a nostalgic feeling that comes from reflecting on how far you’ve come. 🥹

Everyone loves nostalgia—it’s one of the best feelings ever. 💫

*   **Challenge yourself to do better than you did before.**
    
*   **Strive to become the best version of yourself yet.**
    
*   **Finally, compete with yourself, not with others.**
