I use [Evernote](https://www.evernote.com/) religiously but never needed the Pro version. The free version of Evernote is more than sufficient and enough for me. However, I’ve subscribed to the Pro version for quite sometime.

I subscribe to the Pro version because I love this software. It is “love” just as [Phil Libin](https://twitter.com/plibin) have emphasized many a times.

Get Evernote, it works everywhere - Desktop, Mobile, Web. Most of you will never need the Pro version, it is perfectly OK to stay with the free version and use it to its full.
