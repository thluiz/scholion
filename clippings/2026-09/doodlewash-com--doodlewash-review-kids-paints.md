It’s been so much fun seeing everyone’s [World Watercolor Month](https://worldwatercolormonth.com/) posts the past couple of days! So exciting being a part of this with everyone!  Such a wonderful variety of art!

In honor of [World Watercolor Month](https://worldwatercolormonth.com/) and raising awareness for art education for children, this post is on watercolors/art supplies for kids.  It starts out with information about The Dreaming Zebra Foundation, then the supplies. Brands featured- Pelikan, Artists’s Loft, Reeves, Faber-Castell, Yasutomo, and Crayola. Helpful links at the end to help generate ideas for helping the children in your life express themselves artistically.  These supply posts were born out of the belief that artistic expression is extremely important, and far reaching in how it impacts our lives. Children are masters of artistic expression and the beautiful state known as [flow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flow_\(psychology\)).  When they create, they don’t sensor themselves.  They just go for it, colors, shapes, and fun!

![Pelikan watercolours, faber-castel watercolor pencils, reeves gouache, artist loft watercolors, crayola oil pastels, yosutomo](https://i0.wp.com/doodlewash.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/07/supplies.jpg?resize=4032%2C3024&ssl=1)

### MAKE A CHILD’S DREAM COME TRUE!

[![The Dream Zebra Foundation Logo - Donate art supplies to celebrate World Watercolor Month July 2016 worldwatercolor.com ](https://i0.wp.com/doodlewash.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/05/the-dreaming-zebra-foundation-logo.jpeg?resize=390%2C145&ssl=1)](http://dreamingzebra.org/)Research has shown that art education has a tremendous impact on the developmental growth of every child and has proven to help level the learning field across socioeconomic boundaries. Unfortunately, arts education programs are too often the first to be cut when cuts are necessary, so more and more children each year are missing out on the important benefits of art classes.

With your help, we can reverse this trend. To make this first ever month-long global celebration of watercolor affect real change, we’ve teamed up with [The Dreaming Zebra Foundation](http://dreamingzebra.org/), a charity providing support so that children and young adults are given an equal opportunity to explore and develop their creativity in the arts.

![The Dreaming Zebra Foundation charity for Art supplies and art education for underprivileged children](https://i0.wp.com/doodlewash.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/05/dreaming_zebra_art-for-kids.jpg?resize=216%2C194&ssl=1)

The Dreaming Zebra Foundation is unique in that they provide an art recycling program that is free to the public. Reusable art & music supplies that would otherwise be discarded, along with new or unsold materials, are donated by individuals and businesses and matched to recipients who have requested those materials for arts education purposes in communities around the world.  _Art education has a tremendous impact on the developmental growth of every child._

All of the supplies featured in this post will donated to the Dreaming Zebra Foundation, with the exception of the Crayola pastels. I’m in Southern Arizona and I think they might melt in the mail. Scorcher down here!

Artist quality  paints may contain hazardous pigments that are not suitable for children.  If purchasing for kids, supplies should state that they are non-toxic.  Most US packaging states if it conforms to [ASTM D 4236](https://www.astm.org/Standards/D4236.htm) standards, or the foreign equivalent.

![Pelikan and artist loft watercolors](https://i0.wp.com/doodlewash.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/06/pelikanartistloft.jpg?resize=3024%2C4032&ssl=1)

[Pelikan](http://www.pelikan.com/pulse/Pulsar/en_US.Store.displayStore.135829./opaque-paints-by-pelikan) is a German company that manufacturers paints for children and families. [Pelikan Opaque Watercolors 24](http://amzn.to/2ojTULf)– $20.  “These watercolors from Germany are made from high quality pigments of the finest grain size. Easily soluble, they deliver good lightfastness with brilliant, dense colors. Conforms to [DIN 5023](http://www.pelikan.com/pulse/Pulsar/en_MY.CMS.displayCMS.132619./pelikan-k12-paint-box-and-chromatics)[DIN 5023](http://www.pelikan.com/pulse/Pulsar/en_MY.CMS.displayCMS.132619./pelikan-k12-paint-box-and-chromatics).” Not for children under three because the removable pans are a choking hazard.

This double decker palette separates, the lid has deep mixing wells, and the large cake pans snap in and out.  Nice for sharing!  Big cakes to make it easier for the wee ones.  This set is a good deal and comes with a lot of paint. Also comes in a set of [12](http://amzn.to/2nRA6n7) for a little over $11.

[Reeves](http://www.reeves-art.com/in-the-beginning) makes a line of [children’s products](http://www.reeves-art.com/products/children_and_schools_collection).  This UK company has been around for a long time- since the 1700s.

[Reeves 24 pack Gouache Tube Set](http://www.reeves-art.com/products/accessories/gouache_colour_tube_sets/)– $18 these are considered student grade.

“Reeves Gouache is non-toxic and conforms to ASTM D-4236 standards. Good-quality color at an economical price, Reeves Watercolor Sets are ideal for students and beginners.  Reeves Watercolors are easily diluted with water. Brushes and other painting materials are easily cleaned with soap and water.”

Reeves is easy to find at many local art stores, craft stores and online. They also come in smaller sets of [12](http://amzn.to/2pqjkZ5) and [18](http://amzn.to/2pq6MRB), and the same sized sets in  [watercolor](http://amzn.to/2nRFrL0).

![Reeves gouache](https://i0.wp.com/doodlewash.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/06/reevesgouache.jpg?resize=3024%2C4032&ssl=1)

Reeves Gouache

[Faber-Castell](http://www.fabercastell.com/playing-and-learning/products), another company that’s been around since the 1700s, makes a lot of premium art supplies for kids.  Most of them are distinguishable from their other lines by the red packaging. They have a pretty nice [video gallery](http://www.fabercastell.com/playing-and-learning/video-gallery) featuring their line of children’s products. [These](http://amzn.to/2oANfOf) paints look fun, the interlocking pans can be made into different shapes. They also have [these](http://amzn.to/2ojRSdU)  water-soluble crayons, and a bunch of other [products](http://www.fabercastell.com/playing-and-learning/the-art-room).

Watercolor Pencils- about $23 for the set of 36 on [Amazon](http://amzn.to/2nROCLB).  This is a nice student grade set, comes in a metal tin with a hinged lid.  Also has one gold metallic and one silver metallic pencil, and a small brush. This particular set comes from Japan.

![Faber-Castell watercolor pencils set](https://i0.wp.com/doodlewash.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/06/faber-castell.jpg?resize=3024%2C4032&ssl=1)

Watercolor Pencils

I love this set of [paintbrushes](http://amzn.to/2pmaDSv) from Faber-Castell. They aren’t large sizes, biggest is 5/8,” the angular handles are larger for small hands to manage, and they won’t roll.  The jewel colors are fun.

![Faber-Castell kids watercolor brushes](https://i0.wp.com/doodlewash.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/06/faber-castellbrushes.jpg?resize=3024%2C4032&ssl=1)

[Yasutomo Niji Pearlescent Watercolor Paint Cakes 16](http://www.yasutomo.com/index.php?page=shop.product_details&flypage=flypage.tp-ecommerce.tpl&product_id=320&category_id=4&option=com_virtuemart&Itemid=53)– [$6.71](http://amzn.to/2nRDv5m) on Amazon, [$3.74](http://www.merriartist.com/product_p/yonpwc16.htm?gclid=Cj0KEQjwj7q6BRDcxfG4pNTQ2NoBEiQAzUpuW7tY-2yOslezIhwXvTRMiJ1V54f6oQ50D1zKM87zPcQaAsex8P8HAQ) at Merri Artst.  They also come in a set of [21 colors](http://amzn.to/2ojUl8l).  These are around a few dollars for a reason. There isn’t much paint in the pans, the iridescence is weak, but for the price, fun can be had with them.  I think I got this set from my local [Michael’s](http://www.michaels.com/niji-pearlescent-watercolor-set/10138836.html#q=yosutomo&start=7) craft store. Set states non-toxic in the description and it’s not for children under three.  If you are interested in iridescent paints, see this [post](http://doodlewash.com/doodlewash-review-gold-luminous-paints/)– not all intended for use by young children.  Swatches of this set are also available in that post.
