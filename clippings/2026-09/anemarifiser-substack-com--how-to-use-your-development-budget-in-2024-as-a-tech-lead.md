9 out of 10 techies I talk to don't use their development budget. Every time I hear that, I get so frustrated! 😠 The most common reasons are:

"I don't want to upset anyone"

Using your development budget isn't just a right; it shows your commitment to growth.

You're not doing anyone a favor by letting it expire, just missing out on a growth opportunity.

"I don't know how to request it"

check your company's documentation

ask your manager about the process during your next 1-1

Very often it's simpler than you think! 😉

"Not sure how to convince my manager to approve it"

Here's how to make it as easy as possible for them to say "yes":

Be clear about the training you want: research the course and provide details - what it is, who offers it, and its relevance.

Explain the value: outline how this training will benefit not just you but also your manager and the team. Will it enhance your technical skills? Improve collaboration? Boost project efficiency?

Personal motivation: share why it matters to you personally. Perhaps it aligns with your growth goals or will help you step into a leadership role.

Cost transparency: provide the exact cost, so there are no surprises.

Impact on work: be upfront about when the training takes place and how it will affect your availability. Reassure them that it won't negatively impact key deadlines or projects.

Make it hard for them to say no by being well-prepared and leaving no room for uncertainty.

Follow Up: If they don't respond by the agreed time, follow up. Reiterate why this is important to you and how it will contribute to your work.

The less they have to think about it, the easier it is for them to say "yes." 😉
