## [](#1-arc-browser)1\. [Arc Browser](https://arc.net/)

[![Image description](https://media2.dev.to/dynamic/image/width=800%2Cheight=%2Cfit=scale-down%2Cgravity=auto%2Cformat=auto/https%3A%2F%2Fdev-to-uploads.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fuploads%2Farticles%2Ftlh4ouhuw72g0gg53z8w.png)](https://media2.dev.to/dynamic/image/width=800%2Cheight=%2Cfit=scale-down%2Cgravity=auto%2Cformat=auto/https%3A%2F%2Fdev-to-uploads.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fuploads%2Farticles%2Ftlh4ouhuw72g0gg53z8w.png)

Arc Browser is a game-changer for anyone who spends a lot of time online. This browser offers a vertical sidebar for bookmarks and tabs, making organization a breeze. With AI features like tab sorting and split view, managing your workspace has never been easier. Arc also integrates with Perplexity, an AI-powered search engine that provides quick, summarized answers to your queries, enhancing your browsing experience.

* * *

## [](#2-perplexity)2\. [Perplexity](https://www.perplexity.ai/)

[![Image description](https://media2.dev.to/dynamic/image/width=800%2Cheight=%2Cfit=scale-down%2Cgravity=auto%2Cformat=auto/https%3A%2F%2Fdev-to-uploads.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fuploads%2Farticles%2Fi00smr800q2i3e4y18du.png)](https://media2.dev.to/dynamic/image/width=800%2Cheight=%2Cfit=scale-down%2Cgravity=auto%2Cformat=auto/https%3A%2F%2Fdev-to-uploads.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fuploads%2Farticles%2Fi00smr800q2i3e4y18du.png)

Perplexity is my go-to search engine, integrated directly into Arc Browser. It uses AI to fetch and condense information from the web, presenting it in a clean, ad-free interface. This tool significantly cuts down on the time spent sifting through search results, making it an essential part of my productivity toolkit.

[![Image description](https://media2.dev.to/dynamic/image/width=800%2Cheight=%2Cfit=scale-down%2Cgravity=auto%2Cformat=auto/https%3A%2F%2Fdev-to-uploads.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fuploads%2Farticles%2Fy4ksowrze091dv1nof90.png)](https://media2.dev.to/dynamic/image/width=800%2Cheight=%2Cfit=scale-down%2Cgravity=auto%2Cformat=auto/https%3A%2F%2Fdev-to-uploads.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fuploads%2Farticles%2Fy4ksowrze091dv1nof90.png)

Notion serves as my central hub for all information storage and content creation. It's incredibly versatile, allowing me to organize notes, manage projects, and even collaborate with others. With AI features embedded throughout, Notion helps streamline my workflow, whether I'm drafting scripts, saving research, or brainstorming ideas.

## [](#4-missive)4\. [Missive](https://missiveapp.com/)

[![Image description](https://media2.dev.to/dynamic/image/width=800%2Cheight=%2Cfit=scale-down%2Cgravity=auto%2Cformat=auto/https%3A%2F%2Fdev-to-uploads.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fuploads%2Farticles%2Fu083u3bo5cqtlpshzsre.png)](https://media2.dev.to/dynamic/image/width=800%2Cheight=%2Cfit=scale-down%2Cgravity=auto%2Cformat=auto/https%3A%2F%2Fdev-to-uploads.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fuploads%2Farticles%2Fu083u3bo5cqtlpshzsre.png)

## [](#5-canva)5\. [Canva](https://www.canva.com/)

[![Image description](https://media2.dev.to/dynamic/image/width=800%2Cheight=%2Cfit=scale-down%2Cgravity=auto%2Cformat=auto/https%3A%2F%2Fdev-to-uploads.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fuploads%2Farticles%2Fgoepz0717rjgws59uoyx.png)](https://media2.dev.to/dynamic/image/width=800%2Cheight=%2Cfit=scale-down%2Cgravity=auto%2Cformat=auto/https%3A%2F%2Fdev-to-uploads.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fuploads%2Farticles%2Fgoepz0717rjgws59uoyx.png)

Canva is an incredible time-saver for creating graphics, presentations, and marketing materials. With its user-friendly interface and AI features like Magic Eraser and Magic Edit, Canva allows me to produce professional-quality designs quickly and easily, without needing extensive graphic design skills. Even the poster of this post is made on Canva itself xD.

## [](#6-decktopus)6\. [Decktopus](https://www.decktopus.com/)

[![Image description](https://media2.dev.to/dynamic/image/width=800%2Cheight=%2Cfit=scale-down%2Cgravity=auto%2Cformat=auto/https%3A%2F%2Fdev-to-uploads.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fuploads%2Farticles%2F39obheoxwbvh5ct9mqls.png)](https://media2.dev.to/dynamic/image/width=800%2Cheight=%2Cfit=scale-down%2Cgravity=auto%2Cformat=auto/https%3A%2F%2Fdev-to-uploads.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fuploads%2Farticles%2F39obheoxwbvh5ct9mqls.png)

It helps you design visually appealing slides with minimal effort, thanks to its AI-driven templates and design suggestions. Whether you need a professional presentation for work or a simple slideshow for a personal project, Decktopus makes the process seamless and efficient.

[![Image description](https://media2.dev.to/dynamic/image/width=800%2Cheight=%2Cfit=scale-down%2Cgravity=auto%2Cformat=auto/https%3A%2F%2Fdev-to-uploads.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fuploads%2Farticles%2Fn48lhff2ixxp5udcd4pa.png)](https://media2.dev.to/dynamic/image/width=800%2Cheight=%2Cfit=scale-down%2Cgravity=auto%2Cformat=auto/https%3A%2F%2Fdev-to-uploads.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fuploads%2Farticles%2Fn48lhff2ixxp5udcd4pa.png)
