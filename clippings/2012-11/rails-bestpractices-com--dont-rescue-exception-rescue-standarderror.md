In C# or Java, you can `catch (Exception)` to rescue all exception types. However, in Ruby you should almost never catch `Exception`, but only catch `StandardError`. [see more](http://rails-bestpractices.com/posts/702-don-t-rescue-exception-rescue-standarderror)
