Cat care and tech woes spark 3D modeling fun

I Constantly Try to Survive Reality

Maybe that's why I built an entire universe, including a handmade starship. I work from home, which is great in many ways, especially because my cat Pitypang has diabetes and needs regular monitoring and insulin injections.

[![ ](https://media2.dev.to/dynamic/image/width=800%2Cheight=%2Cfit=scale-down%2Cgravity=auto%2Cformat=auto/https%3A%2F%2Fdev-to-uploads.s3.us-east-2.amazonaws.com%2Fuploads%2Farticles%2Fb4bkp2w4mp9xr70gzpuj.png)](https://media2.dev.to/dynamic/image/width=800%2Cheight=%2Cfit=scale-down%2Cgravity=auto%2Cformat=auto/https%3A%2F%2Fdev-to-uploads.s3.us-east-2.amazonaws.com%2Fuploads%2Farticles%2Fb4bkp2w4mp9xr70gzpuj.png)

On Monday, when I went back upstairs to my home office, I was greeted by this screen:

[![A great moment of home office](https://media2.dev.to/dynamic/image/width=800%2Cheight=%2Cfit=scale-down%2Cgravity=auto%2Cformat=auto/https%3A%2F%2Fdev-to-uploads.s3.us-east-2.amazonaws.com%2Fuploads%2Farticles%2Fudjzdhc78lv0i5orosgd.png)](https://media2.dev.to/dynamic/image/width=800%2Cheight=%2Cfit=scale-down%2Cgravity=auto%2Cformat=auto/https%3A%2F%2Fdev-to-uploads.s3.us-east-2.amazonaws.com%2Fuploads%2Farticles%2Fudjzdhc78lv0i5orosgd.png)

_... suddenly changing company policy ..._

I think this is an office-related variation of the Blue Screen of Death. A slightly kinder version, perhaps, but the result is the same: you can't use your computer. It took me three hours to get back to work. Admittedly, I wasn't exactly rushing, because seeing that screen had completely fried my brain. I spent the rest of the day trying to get myself mentally back on track.

Have you ever had to deal with similar corporate bullshit?
