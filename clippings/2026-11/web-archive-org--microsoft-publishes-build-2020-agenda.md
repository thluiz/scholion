Last week, [Microsoft opened up registration for its Build 2020 developer conference](https://www.neowin.net/news/microsoft-is-opening-registration-for-its-digital-build-2020-conference-today), also announcing some details about its upcoming event. Now, the agenda has been published on [the Build website](https://mybuild.microsoft.com/).

The event will begin at 8am Pacific, followed by the "Empowering every developer" keynote from CEO Satya Nadella. At 8:40, it will be time for the Imagine Cup, followed by a session with Scott Hanselman and then a session with Scott Guthrie.
