Philosophers in this comic: [Jean Paul Sartre](https://existentialcomics.com/philosopher/Jean_Paul_Sartre "Jean Paul Sartre was a 20th century philosopher who wrote in the German phenomenological tradition, with such works as Being and Nothing and The Transcendence of the Ego; as well as his extremely popular fiction, such as Nausea and No Exit. However, he is best known for yelling \"RADICAL FREEDOM\" in my comics."), [Simone de Beauvoir](https://existentialcomics.com/philosopher/Simone_de_Beauvoir "Simone de Beauvoir was an existential philosopher and life long partner to Jean Paul Sartre. Although she has claimed in interviews that Sartre was the \"real philosopher\", and everything she wrote was mostly just an extention of his thought, you can sort of tell that she was the smarter of the two. Not to mention that The Second Sex has been far more influential and important than anything Sartre ever wrote. She was also taller and better looking, but who's keeping track?")
