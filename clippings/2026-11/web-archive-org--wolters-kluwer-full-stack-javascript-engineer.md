Wolters Kluwer is looking for a remote full-stack JS engineer to work on an internal startup called TaskFlow. It is a SaaS, a low-code/no-code application designer that allows non-technical users to build decision trees, interactive forms, and simple data collection workflows. Those applications are used in products across all Wolters Kluwer divisions (health, legal, tax, compliance) and outside. 

**About us:**

*   we are a remote team of ~50 engineers, designers, and product managers
*   we build our product ourselves: from an idea to implementation to support our customers in production. **You can have an impact!**
*   we started working remotely before COVID, and we are not coming back!
*   we embrace async, deep work, minimum meetings, flexible working hours, and transparent communication
*   remote team member== 1st class citizen in our team
*   we value results, transparency, and collaboration
*   our UI is rich - think of Google Docs/Miro/Visio scale
*   we release to production several times per day
*   we deploy our product globally (including AWS China)
*   we automate everything - we are crazy about testing and releasing often
*   all communication is in English

**We are looking for:**

*   great engineers who are also managers of one
*   our tech stack is JS/TS/node, Angular (we like React too!), Redux, Jest, Cypress, AWS, OpsGenie.

**We offer:**

*   competitive compensation
*   the dynamic and challenging working environment
*   you will be hired as a full-time employee via remote.com employer of record

**Interview process:**

*   Apply 
*   A screening interview with the hiring engineering manager (30 minutes)
*   Three rounds of technical interviews (30-60 minutes each, be prepare to code :))
*   Offer 
*   Ready rock!
