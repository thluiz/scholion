## Friday, December 27, 2019

### [“Fire and Ice” Background](https://gurneyjourney.blogspot.com/2019/12/backgrounds-for-fire-and-ice.html)

Here's an animation background that I painted in cel vinyl, the same paint used for painting the characters on the cels.

[![](https://blogger.googleusercontent.com/img/b/R29vZ2xl/AVvXsEhYW5oXQluehL3vGV439py3V3wFZTrSnGlVp26UPwX5vZ_TEYlQilUYKqFzxE-QxSjQCpRtejGfiCjD5ZWaxf9Y5BzKS1zwgyg2RmOuzZsSajBqkYNNGZRAw71x704hCpVL9oqLwpEoH81x/s320/7DC0B5BB-43DA-432B-BFF9-163F54E84D40.jpeg)](https://blogger.googleusercontent.com/img/b/R29vZ2xl/AVvXsEhYW5oXQluehL3vGV439py3V3wFZTrSnGlVp26UPwX5vZ_TEYlQilUYKqFzxE-QxSjQCpRtejGfiCjD5ZWaxf9Y5BzKS1zwgyg2RmOuzZsSajBqkYNNGZRAw71x704hCpVL9oqLwpEoH81x/s1600/7DC0B5BB-43DA-432B-BFF9-163F54E84D40.jpeg)

The idea was for the "subhuman" character to appear out of a layer of airbrushed mist and threaten princess Teegra in the foreground.

  

[![](https://blogger.googleusercontent.com/img/b/R29vZ2xl/AVvXsEhbAKGoMEDHgVyMtt8jrS-xiQgIABp3R9a5DAClzwZCWUjAd6PimyYATCn0cA0pCvWR6KBoQV63G0rpkUpQX0nGOn5BLulUBXcfgzkAYQ67yv3GL0VvT_g99K56eUA22YKeS122JkptW33Z/s320/9CB58E8A-0A68-48F8-8A85-C399C3D7F4CE.jpeg)](https://blogger.googleusercontent.com/img/b/R29vZ2xl/AVvXsEhbAKGoMEDHgVyMtt8jrS-xiQgIABp3R9a5DAClzwZCWUjAd6PimyYATCn0cA0pCvWR6KBoQV63G0rpkUpQX0nGOn5BLulUBXcfgzkAYQ67yv3GL0VvT_g99K56eUA22YKeS122JkptW33Z/s1600/9CB58E8A-0A68-48F8-8A85-C399C3D7F4CE.jpeg)

The background appeared in the 1983 animated feature “Fire and Ice,” co-produced by Ralph Bakshi and Frank Frazetta.
