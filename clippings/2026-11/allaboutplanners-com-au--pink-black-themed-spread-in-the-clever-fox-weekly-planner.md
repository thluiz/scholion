## After the Pen

This planner has neutral colors so you know what that means… time to add some color!

I was originally going to do a pink ombre theme with highlighters and colored pens… but highlighters tend to come in either all the same shade of that color (no matter the brand), or very bright / very pastel with shades that didn’t look that great together. So plan B was a black and pink theme.

If you did want to try an ombre pink weekly spread with pens, [see this post for a comparison I did of various pink pens](https://allaboutplanners.com.au/pen-comparison-is-there-actually-a-difference-in-ink-color-between-brands/).

![weekly spread clever fox planner pink black theme quick easy simple](https://allaboutplanners.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2019/12/weekly-spread-clever-fox-planner-pink-black-theme-quick-easy-simple-1024x768.jpg)

I prefer to do monthly habit tracking instead of weekly so covered that section with a sticky note. It was a cheap one I found at Spotlight years ago so wasn’t very sticky – hence the addition of the washi tape.

The dots in the notes section of this planner are a dark shade of grey making it a bit busy. However, Clever Fox listened to feedback and have updated this for their newer planners – the dots are now a lighter shade of grey.

![blog planning in the clever fox weekly planner ombre pink black theme 52 planners challenge](https://allaboutplanners.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2019/12/blog-planning-in-the-clever-fox-weekly-planner-ombre-pink-black-theme-52-planners-challenge-768x1024.jpg)

The planner is undated – I was going to just write the dates in with pen but stickers stand out more. Since I didn’t have a set of printed & cut stickers for all dates in pink (as I tend to do rainbow themes), I grabbed some plain date dot stickers and did a border with a marker pen.

## Would I use this planner again?

I like the weekly spread – simple + lined. The 2nd page is a bit _too_ structured for me – especially with the A5 page size – it’s not enough room to write.

I have the Clever Fox weekly planner Pro version on it’s way which has a modified layout to this one & a larger page size so I might try that layout out and see if I like it better.
