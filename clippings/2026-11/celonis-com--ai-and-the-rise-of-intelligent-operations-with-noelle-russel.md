## Responsibly scaling enterprise AI

For years, automation was about speed. Today, it’s about intelligence—systems that don’t just follow rules but understand goals and fix problems before they occur. Join us to learn how leaders can responsibly scale AI and build trust across the organization.

*   **Digital Workers:** Why we should design AI systems as digital workers with clear roles, rather than isolated tools.
*   **Self-Improving Operations:** How to design operations that provide the foresight needed to prevent failures before they materialize.
*   **The SAT Model:** How to solve for Security, Accuracy, and Trust to overcome skepticism and maintain inclusion.
