I just wanted to mention how happy I am personally that we were able to ship functionality. It allows individual authors on DEV to get on board — right through their settings! As more [Forems](https://dev.to/devteam/for-empowering-community-2k6h) pop up, platform creators and individual members may be able to share in the pie. A lot of adoption has to happen for this to work out, but it's a future we're happy to be pushing.

Web monetization is not baked into browsers yet, but it may be sometime soon. Mozilla's support for Grant for the Web is cool to see. This could be an important part of content-driven web in the future.

[Coil](https://coil.com/?ref=dev) is the company principally behind web monetization. You can support web-monetized content creators by spending $5/month through their service.
