I’ve purchased plenty of [1 page horizontal weekly + 1 page checklist weekly planners](https://allaboutplanners.com.au/category/planner-reviews/horizontal-1-page-weekly-notes/). One that had been on my shelf for quite some time but I’d yet to try was the Bluesky Noteworthy Planner!

See my review of the planner in [this post](https://allaboutplanners.com.au/blue-sky-noteworthy-planner-review-pros-cons-video-walkthrough/).

## Before the Pen

I used some whiteout tape to erase 2019 and add a 2020 sticker from one of the MAMBI sticker books. I also used some date / number stickers from a MAMBI sticker book.

![bluesky noteworthy planner spread customising colorful 4 color weekly theme simple quick easy list maker horizontal staedtler pens\_01](https://allaboutplanners.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/bluesky-noteworthy-planner-spread-customising-colorful-4-color-weekly-theme-simple-quick-easy-list-maker-horizontal-staedtler-pens_01-1024x768.jpg)

If you’re going to use stickers like these over black text, be sure to whiteout first. As you can see, I didn’t use whiteout for Monday but did for Tuesday.

## After the Pen

![bluesky noteworthy planner spread customising colorful 4 color weekly theme simple quick easy list maker horizontal staedtler pens\_05](https://allaboutplanners.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/bluesky-noteworthy-planner-spread-customising-colorful-4-color-weekly-theme-simple-quick-easy-list-maker-horizontal-staedtler-pens_05-1024x768.jpg)

Like my problem of having too many planners, I have too many pens! So wanted to use one I hadn’t used for ages – the Staedtler Twin Tip handwriting pens. These pens are certainly convenient being 2 in 1 (fine tip and a fat tip), but after you use them for a while the fine tip loses it’s point and turns into more of a marker pen too.

![bluesky noteworthy planner spread customising colorful 4 color weekly theme simple quick easy list maker horizontal staedtler pens\_09](https://allaboutplanners.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/bluesky-noteworthy-planner-spread-customising-colorful-4-color-weekly-theme-simple-quick-easy-list-maker-horizontal-staedtler-pens_09-1024x768.jpg)

I kept to my usual blog post planning in the weekly spread and categorised checklists on the second page of the spread. The top of this planner is wide enough for some washi tape but I thought it’d look a bit overdone so resisted adding it.

![bluesky noteworthy planner spread customising colorful 4 color weekly theme simple quick easy list maker horizontal staedtler pens\_08](https://allaboutplanners.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/bluesky-noteworthy-planner-spread-customising-colorful-4-color-weekly-theme-simple-quick-easy-list-maker-horizontal-staedtler-pens_08-1024x768.jpg)

![bluesky noteworthy planner spread customising colorful 4 color weekly theme simple quick easy list maker horizontal staedtler pens\_06](https://allaboutplanners.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/bluesky-noteworthy-planner-spread-customising-colorful-4-color-weekly-theme-simple-quick-easy-list-maker-horizontal-staedtler-pens_06-scaled-e1583670130871-576x768.jpg)

## Would I use this planner again?

Yes. This planner has my favorite weekly layout (horizontal weekly + checklist). I do wish the checklist columns were a little wider – they’re quite narrow due to the page size – perhaps 3 sections horizontally would fit more tasks on the page.
