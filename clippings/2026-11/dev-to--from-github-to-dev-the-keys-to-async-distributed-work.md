I want to call attention to the second episode of the DevDiscuss podcast.

In this episode, we welcomed [@sophiedebenedetto](https://dev.to/sophiedebenedetto), an engineer from GitHub, and [@maestromac](https://dev.to/maestromac), a DEV team member who's been with us pretty much since the very beginning.

Jess and I really enjoyed riffing on a topic we all feel like we've gained a certain expertise in (with a lot to learn!!). As the world has shifted remote-first in a way none of of us could have predicted, this is a prescient topic for all of us.
