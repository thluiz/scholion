## [](#a-little-about-my-sleep-schedule-history)A little about my sleep schedule history

Ever since I was a little kid 👶,  
I used to go to bed early and wake up early in the morning. (9pm-6am)🌅

Even in my school life I was able to maintain my sleeping habits  
(10pm-6am)🌅

When I joined my engineering college, I started sleeping a little late. 🌃

But I had to wake up early to go to college. (11pm/12pm - 6am)🌅

Eventually, in second year my schedule became 11pm - 5am and in third year it became 11pm - 4:00am/4:30am. 🌄

Then, as the third year ended and burnout ruined my sleep schedule. 😔

Recently, I managed to recover from the burnout and now my sleep schedule is back to 11pm-4am. 🌄

## [](#why-should-you-wake-up-early)

Why should you wake up early?

### [](#1-you-get-more-time-in-the-day)

1\. You get more time in the day

[![Time Stonks meme](https://media2.dev.to/dynamic/image/width=800%2Cheight=%2Cfit=scale-down%2Cgravity=auto%2Cformat=auto/https%3A%2F%2Fdev-to-uploads.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fi%2Fwzmqscygg77dho6kfeo2.png)](https://media2.dev.to/dynamic/image/width=800%2Cheight=%2Cfit=scale-down%2Cgravity=auto%2Cformat=auto/https%3A%2F%2Fdev-to-uploads.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fi%2Fwzmqscygg77dho6kfeo2.png)

Goddamit the day feels very long. 🙄 (Which is really nice, but it feels weird sometimes) When you wake up at 4am 🌄, you are basically done with most of your daily tasks by 9am 🌤. You practically have a minimum of 12 productive hours in a day.

\---------------------------- ###

2\. You get a sense of achievement

!\[Obama Meme\](https://dev-to-uploads.s3.amazonaws.com/i/zfhth0r3t1p8irtdvi4m.jpg)

This feeling of accomplishment is unparalleled.

\------------------------- ###

3\. 😌Silence😌

It is usually very silent in the early morning which makes it easier to concentrate on your tasks.

\----------------------------- ###

4\. You get a proud feeling that while most of the world is sleeping, you are working. This feeling is really good for your morale💯

!\[Satisfied seal meme\](https://dev-to-uploads.s3.amazonaws.com/i/5s6yaoszr5frcfoo5rh2.jpg) -------------------------- ###

5\. 🌸You'll feel better about yourself🌸

You'll have a positive attitude which will help you increase your output (or at-least make you happier) --------------------- ###

6\. You’ll Have a Better Chance at Completing your tasks💯

\--------------------- ###

7\. You can also just take it slow

!\[relaxed bart meme\](https://dev-to-uploads.s3.amazonaws.com/i/7ax3gmnzfb2j2ssd1c3c.jpg) ----------- ###

8\. 💝Some health benefits 💝

!\[Helth Meme\](https://dev-to-uploads.s3.amazonaws.com/i/tmax2z7vvtyd0xx3vdcp.png) - It improves your quality of sleep. - It gives you more time to exercise. - Not only you've more time to exercise but exercising in the morning gives maximum benefits. - It helps you to sustain a healthier diet. - It makes your skin look healthier(I don't have any proof though) ---------------- ###

9\. 😆Some funny (and possibly sarcastic) reasons 😆

\- You can talk about it on social media. - Internet speed is usually faster in the morning. - Your parents will become more happier for you. - You can brag about waking up early. ------------------------------------------------------- #

How to sleep early ?

\- Screens and Caffeine are your worst enemies. - Don't drink coffee within 6 hours of your sleep time. - Don't sleep for long during the day. - Stop using computers/mobile phones after 9 pm (Which is pretty unrealistic I know, but try to stop using them after 10 pm) - Go to bed at 10 \*\*AT ANY COST\*\* The real challenge begins now.

\*\*DON'T TOUCH YOUR MOBILE PHONES NOW\*\* 📱❌ (\_Unless of-course it's an emergency\_) I KNOW ✅ IT WILL BE VERY TEMPTING 🤤 THE CONSTANT FEELING OF CHECKING NOTIFICATIONS 😫 THE CONSTANT FEELING OF CHECKING IF YOU GOT ANY NEW MESSAGE BUT NO ❌ YOU ARE STRONG 😤 YOU HAVE TO BE STRONG 😓 YOU HAVE TO FIGHT THIS FEELING 😤 YOU CAN DO IT 😎 (May be. I mean I don't really know you :P )

*   Now, you have to create this new habit of sleeping early.
*   If you are used to sleeping late chances are your body will be full of energy at night.
*   Lay down on the bed. Stay Still.
*   Don't overthink and focus on your breathing.
