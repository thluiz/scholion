_10 Years with Hayao Miyazaki_ is a 4-part documentary that's currently free to stream online. 

[![](https://1.bp.blogspot.com/-5hhMkdvsxbU/XRNEMRZY0OI/AAAAAAACuJY/8dMzmQhRZHwwPaH9cslHvbuFCaWP7FuVgCLcBGAs/s400/Miyazaki%2Bat%2BWork.jpg)](https://1.bp.blogspot.com/-5hhMkdvsxbU/XRNEMRZY0OI/AAAAAAACuJY/8dMzmQhRZHwwPaH9cslHvbuFCaWP7FuVgCLcBGAs/s1600/Miyazaki%2Bat%2BWork.jpg)

It follows the legendary animation director's creative struggles as he develops the Studio Ghibli films _Ponyo_ and _The Wind Rises_. Chain-smoking and irritable, [Miyazaki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hayao_Miyazaki) tolerates a single cameraman, who follows him through his daily routine, including his life at home and his role at studio meetings.

[![](https://ws-na.amazon-adsystem.com/widgets/q?_encoding=UTF8&MarketPlace=US&ASIN=1569317771&ServiceVersion=20070822&ID=AsinImage&WS=1&Format=_SL250_&tag=gurnjour-20)](https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/1569317771/ref=as_li_tl?ie=UTF8&camp=1789&creative=9325&creativeASIN=1569317771&linkCode=as2&tag=gurnjour-20&linkId=3bf0215623dda9d6a2a2930b5bba2205)With spare narration and a vérité spirit, the documentary doesn't shy away from the dead ends and frustrations, even for such a successful and productive artist. For Miyazaki, each film begins as a lonely and personal journey before it becomes a collective group enterprise.

"Miyazaki works in a constant cloud of doubt and anxiety," the narrator comments. When visiting London, Miyazaki is struck by [Millais'](https://www.tate.org.uk/art/artworks/millais-ophelia-n01506) _[Ophelia](https://www.tate.org.uk/art/artworks/millais-ophelia-n01506),_ and he feels his work is at a dead end. He shifts his working place to a new house and experiments with new materials to refresh his approach. In the end, the lesson is to trust your heart...and trust the process.
