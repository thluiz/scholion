## English translation

(Machine translation of the Japanese original below, produced with openai/gpt-5.4 on 2026-10-08. Source: NDL Collaborative Reference Database, record 1000029440. Question: 'I heard the source of 守破離 (shuhari) is Zeami''s Fūshikaden. Is that true?' Answer given by the library: there are various theories about the source and it could not be confirmed. The text below is the library''s research process. The tea treatise it calls 茶和抄 is the 茶話抄, Chawa-shō.)

Because it is said to be a word of **世阿弥** (Zeami; English gloss: the Noh theorist/playwright Zeami), I investigated such sources as **『世阿弥伝書用語索引』** (*Zeami densho yōgo sakuin*; *Index of Terms in Zeami’s Transmitted Writings*). There is **「序破急」** (*johakyū*; *introduction, break, rapidity*), but there is no corresponding entry.

Since **『日本国語大辞典』** (*Nihon kokugo daijiten*; *Great Dictionary of the Japanese Language*) states that it expresses the stages of training in **茶道** (*sadō*; *the Way of Tea*) and **武道** (*budō*; *the martial ways*), I broadened the scope and investigated further.

In the entry for **〈守破離〉** (*shu-ha-ri*) in **『日本国語大辞典　6』** (*Nihon kokugo daijiten 6*; *Great Dictionary of the Japanese Language, vol. 6*), it quotes from **「茶和抄」** (*Chawa-shō*, the 茶話抄 / *Notes of Tea Talks*; the database writes 和 for 話):  
“**守破離** (*shu-ha-ri*) is a term used in **軍法** (*gunpō*; *military method/strategy*); although its manner of use is, to be sure, different, if we were to speak of it as applied to **茶道** (*sadō*; *the Way of Tea*), **守** (*shu*) is the unskilled [text omitted], **破** (*ha*) is the skilled [text omitted], and **離** (*ri*) is the master.”

Included in **『茶道古典全集　１０』** (*Sadō koten zenshū 10*; *Collected Classics of the Way of Tea, vol. 10*) (Tankōsha, 1977), pp. 268–269, is **「茶和抄」** (*Chawa-shō*, the 茶話抄 / *Notes of Tea Talks*; the database writes 和 for 話). It is a tea treatise written by **横井淡所** (Yokoi Tansho; English gloss: tea author/scholar), supplemented and revised by **川上不白** (Kawakami Fuhaku; English gloss: tea master) and **羽山宗郁** (Hayama Sōiku; English gloss: tea master/editor).

According to **『剣道事典』** (*Kendō jiten*; *Encyclopedia of Kendo*) (Shimazu Shobō, 1994), it came to be used as a term describing stages of training from the time it was used in **「不白筆記」** (*Fuhaku hikki*; *Fuhaku’s Notes*) by **川上不白** (Kawakami Fuhaku; English gloss: tea master) in **寛延2年** (*Kan’en ni-nen*; *the 2nd year of Kan’en, 1749*).

In **『武道伝書を読む』** (*Budō densho o yomu*; *Reading Martial Arts Transmission Texts*) (Yuasa Akira, Nippon Budokan, 2005), pp. 16–20, under the heading **「守・破・離」** (*shu, ha, ri*), it is related to **「風姿花伝」** (*Fūshikaden*; *The Transmission of the Flower Through Style and Form*), but the word **守破離** (*shu-ha-ri*) itself does not appear.

I am reporting the above course of investigation.

Other materials already investigated are as follows.

**『利休道歌に学ぶ』** (*Rikyū dōka ni manabu*; *Learning from Rikyū’s Didactic Poems*) by **阿部宗正** (Abe Sōsei; English gloss: tea author) (Tankōsha, 2000)  
**『武道　日本史小百科』** (*Budō: Nihonshi shōhyakka*; *Budō: A Concise Encyclopedia of Japanese History*) (Tōkyōdō Shuppan, 1994)

## Original (Japanese)

世阿弥の言葉というので『世阿弥伝書用語索引』等を調査する。「序破急」はあるが該当なし。  
『日本国語大辞典』より茶道・武道の修行段階を表すとあるので範囲を広げて調べた。  
『日本国語大辞典　6』の〈守破離〉の項では「茶和抄」の「守破離問いふ事軍法用、尤用方違ひ候へ共、茶道に取て申候はば、守は下手（略）破は上手（略）離は名人」を引用している。  
『茶道古典全集　１０』（淡交社　1977）p268-269に「茶和抄」所収。横井淡所著、川上不白・羽山宗郁補訂の茶説書。  
『剣道事典』（島津書房　1994）より、「不白筆記」（川上不白　寛延2年）で用いられて以来、修行段階を述べた言葉として用いられるようになったとあり。  
『武道伝書を読む』（湯浅晃　日本武道館　2005）p16-20では「守・破・離」の見出しで「風姿花伝」と関連づけをしているが守破離の言葉はなし。  
以上の調査経過を連絡する。

その他の調査済み資料は以下のとおり。  
『角川茶道大事典　本編』（角川書店　1990）『原色茶道大辞典』（淡交社　1984）『利休道歌に学ぶ』（阿部宗正　淡交社　2000）『武道　日本史小百科』（東京堂出版　1994）
