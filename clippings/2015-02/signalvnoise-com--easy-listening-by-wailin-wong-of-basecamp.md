# Easy Listening

Yesterday we launched [The Distance podcast](https://thedistance.com/podcast/) and our show is now in iTunes. Be sure to [subscribe](https://signalvnoise.com/posts/itun.es/i6Bn8Mf)!

In honor of The Distance podcast’s debut, I asked Basecampers what podcasts they’re currently enjoying and collected the responses. In some cases, you’ll see a recommendation for a particularly good episode to check out. (Basecamp is also sponsoring several podcasts, including Nerdette and Bullseye, which are mentioned below.) As I was compiling this list, I felt like I was naming every podcast in the world. But it’s just a sign of what a long and interesting tail there is in the world of audio. Here’s to discovering new things! And as a bonus, you’ll find recommendations for podcast apps at the end. Be sure to add your suggestions for any great podcasts (or apps) we’ve missed in the comments.
  
**Business/Tech/Design/Productivity**

- [99% Invisible](http://99percentinvisible.org/): Shaun calls it “probably the best podcast about design out there.”
- [Accidental Tech Podcast](http://atp.fm/): Marco Arment, maker of the iOS podcast app Overcast (and many other things), is one of the co-hosts.
- [All About Android](http://twit.tv/show/all-about-android): News, hardware, apps, how-tos.
- [Back to Work](http://5by5.tv/b2w): Merlin Mann and Dan Benjamin talk about productivity.
- [The Broad Experience](http://www.thebroadexperience.com/): A show about women in the workplace. Joan recommends [this episode](http://www.thebroadexperience.com/listen/2014/12/1/episode-53-a-technical-problem.html) about women engineers in Silicon Valley.
- [The Critical Path](http://5by5.tv/criticalpath): Analysis of Apple and mobile technology.
- [Debug](http://www.imore.com/debug): A “conversational interview show” about software, with a focus on Apple.
- [Freakonomics Radio](http://freakonomics.com/radio/): From the authors of the best-selling book.
- [Jobs-to-be-done Radio](http://jobstobedone.org/topics/radio/): Listen to [this 2013 episode](http://jobstobedone.org/radio/ryan-singer-jtbd-radio/) featuring our very own Ryan Singer.
- [Marketplace](http://www.marketplace.org/podcasts): This well-known business news show is 25 years old!
- [Planet Money](http://www.npr.org/blogs/money/): ’s lively show about the economy.
- [Reply All](http://gimletmedia.com/show/reply-all/): Stories about Internet culture from the duo that founded on .
- [StartUp](http://gimletmedia.com/show/startup/): Public radio veteran Alex Blumberg documents his journey starting a podcast company.
- [Support Ops](http://supportops.co/): Basecamper Chase co-hosts this weekly show about what makes a great customer support pro.
- [This Week in Google](http://twit.tv/show/this-week-in-google): Hosted by Leo Laporte and Jeff Jarvis.
- [The Tim Ferriss Show](http://fourhourworkweek.com/podcast/): From the author of “The 4 -Hour Workweek.”
- : A show about Internet culture hosted by Meredith Haggerty, who took over when the show’s founders decamped for Reply All (see above).

**Comedy**

- [Baby Geniuses](http://www.maximumfun.org/shows/baby-geniuses): Shaun describes it as a “mix of real and fake facts hosted by two very funny women and great guests.”
- [The Bugle](http://thebuglepodcast.com/): John Oliver and Andy Zaltzman’s satirical take on the week’s news.
- [Do You Need A Ride?](http://allthingscomedy.com/channels/48/do-you-need-a-ride%3F): Two comedians drive another comedian to or from an airport. Joan recommends [this episode](http://allthingscomedy.com/contents/5924/48-ep21-jackie-kashian-podcast), featuring Jackie Kashian.
- [Don’t Ever Change](http://www.feralaudio.com/show/dont-ever-change/): Interviews about life in high school.
- [Girl On Guy](http://girlonguy.net/podcast/): The winsome Aisha Tyler chats with her guy friends.
- [How Was Your Week? with Julie Klausner](http://www.howwasyourweek.com/): Author and performer Julie Klausner talks to smart and interesting people. Joan recommends [this episode](http://howwasyourweek.libsyn.com/john-safran-shes-a-hurricane-ep-195), which she says “contains a rant about Annie that is particularly amazing.”
- [International Waters](http://www.maximumfun.org/shows/international-waters): Yanks and Brits face off in a comedy pop culture quiz show.
- [Jordan, Jesse GO!](http://www.maximumfun.org/shows/jordan-jesse-go): A show about life in your twenties.
- [Judge John Hodgman](http://www.maximumfun.org/shows/judge-john-hodgman): John Hodgman (“I’m a PC”) settles real-life disputes.
- [My Brother, My Brother and Me](http://www.maximumfun.org/shows/my-brother-my-brother-and-me): Ostensibly an advice show, but more of an excuse for three real-life brothers to riff on stuff.
- [Never Not Funny](http://pardcast.com/blog/): Comedian Jimmy Pardo and producer Matt Belknap chat with new guests every week.
- [Stop Podcasting Yourself](http://www.maximumfun.org/shows/stop-podcasting-yourself): Canadians being funny.
- [This Feels Terrible](http://www.feralaudio.com/show/this-feels-terrible-with-erin-mcgathy/): Comedians talk about their relationship histories.
- [You Made It Weird](http://www.nerdist.com/podcast_channel/you-made-it-weird-channel/): Comedian Pete Holmes talks to other comedians.

**Pop Culture/Arts & Entertainment**

- [The Adventure Zone](http://www.maximumfun.org/shows/adventure-zone): The McElroy brothers of My Brother, My Brother and Me (see above) play Dungeons & Dragons with their dad. Joan recommends [Episode 2](http://maximumfun.org/adventure-zone/ep-2-here-there-be-gerblins-chapter-two).
- [The Attitude Era](http://www.earwolf.com/show/analyze-phish%3E%3C/a%3EAnalyze%20Phish%3C/a%3E:%20Parks%20and%20Rec%20writer%20Harris%20Wittels%20tries%20to%20make%20fellow%20comedy%20writer%20Scott%20Aukerman%20a%20Phish%20fan.%3C/li%3E%3Cli%3E%3Ca%20href=): A look back at the World Wrestling Federation during the late 90s.
- [All Songs Considered](http://www.npr.org/blogs/allsongs/): Jason Zimdars calls this show “the best place to hear about new music if you’re no longer 25.”
- [Bullseye](http://www.maximumfun.org/shows/bullseye): A pop culture show that’s also distributed by .
- [Denzel Washington is Greatest Actor of All Time Period](http://denzelwashington.wolfpop.com/): I told you there was a long tail in podcasts.
- [Filmspotting](http://www.filmspotting.net/): Film reviews and interviews.
- [The Goosedown](http://www.maximumfun.org/shows/goosedown): Two black comedians’ perspective on pop culture.
- [How Did This Get Made?](http://www.earwolf.com/show/how-did-this-get-made/): Comedians take down bad movies.
- [I Was There Too](http://iwastheretoo.wolfpop.com/audio/playlists/3962): Interviews with actors who played minor characters in well-known movies, like the woman with the baby carriage in The Untouchables.
- [Maltin On Movies](http://maltinonmovies.wolfpop.com/audio/playlists/3945): Longtime film critic Leonard Maltin and his co-host, comedian Baron Vaughn, talk about movies.
- [The Nerdist Podcast](http://www.nerdist.com/podcast_channel/nerdist-podcast-channel/): Weekly interviews with entertainers and comedians.
- [Pop Rocket](http://omfg.wolfpop.com/audio/playlists/3964%3EOMFG%3C/a%3E:%20A%20show%20that%20explains%20what%20kids%20are%20up%20to%20these%20days.%3C/li%3E%3Cli%3E%3Ca%20href=): A fun panel discusses what’s new and interesting in entertainment. Joan recommends [Episode 2](http://maximumfun.org/pop-rocket/pop-rocket-episode-002-golden-globes-debrief).
- [Song Exploder](http://www.maximumfun.org/shows/song-exploder): Musicians take apart their songs and share the stories behind them.
- [Sound Opinions](http://www.soundopinions.org/): A talk show hosted by two veteran Chicago music critics.
- [Tiny Desk Concerts](http://www.npr.org/series/tiny-desk-concerts/): Intimate performances at the offices.
- [U Talkin’ U2 to Me?](http://www.earwolf.com/show/u-talkin-u2-to-me/): Adam Scott of Parks and Rec and Scott Aukerman of Comedy Bang! Bang! talk about U2.
- [We Hate Movies](http://www.whmpodcast.com/): James says this show is about “entertainingly terrible (but not terribly entertaining) movies torn apart by nerds.” Also, James has [his own podcast](http://www.uberlin.co.uk/tag/ask-uberlin/) about life in Berlin!
- [Wham Bam Pow](http://www.maximumfun.org/shows/wham-bam-pow): Movie reviews focused on sci-fi and action films.
- [Who Charted?](http://www.earwolf.com/show/who-charted/): The latest in music and movies, featuring Los Angeles comedians.

**Food/Fitness/Sports/Health/Wellness/Relationships**

- [Audio Dharma](https://signalvnoise.com/posts/%E2%80%9Chttp://www.audiodharma.org/%E2%80%9C): Talks from the Insight Meditation Center, a Buddhist center in Redwood City, California.
- [Barbell Shrugged](http://daily.barbellshrugged.com/): Fitness, nutrition, conditioning, lifting heavy things.
- [Chewing the Fat](http://www.wbez.org/content/chewing-fat-podcast-louisa-chu-and-monica-eng): A weekly show hosted by two food journalists, Louisa *Chu* and Monica *Eng*. Get it?!
- [The Guardian Football Weekly](http://www.theguardian.com/football/series/footballweekly): Soccer, that is.
- [Savage Lovecast](http://www.savagelovecast.com/): Love and sex advice from the inimitable Dan Savage.
- [Take It Uneasy](http://takeituneasy.com/): Elite athletes in judo, jiu jitsu and other sports tackle “challenge, fear, and happiness in all aspects of life.” Joan recommends [this episode](http://takeituneasy.com/29-annmaria-de-mars-on-raising-ronda-rousey-aggressive-judo-math-education-and-the-value-of-hard-work/) with AnnMaria De Mars, the ’s first Judo World Champion, who is also (among other things) a game developer and mother of champion Ronda Rousey.
- [Totally Beverages](http://www.totallylaime.com/podcasts/totallybeverages/): Sometimes hot sauce, too.
- [Totally Married](http://www.totallylaime.com/podcasts/totallymarried/): From the same folks that bring you Totally Beverages.

**News/Culture/Storytelling/Not Easily Categorized**

- [Criminal](http://thisiscriminal.com/): True crime stories.
- [How To Do Everything](http://howtodoeverything.org/): Ordering wine, punching people in the face, ironing pants.
- [Intelligence Squared U.S.](http://intelligencesquaredus.org/): Oxford-style debates, recorded live.
- [Longform](http://longform.org/podcast): Interviews with non-fiction writers and journalists.
- [Oh No, Ross and Carrie!](http://www.maximumfun.org/shows/oh-no-ross-and-carrie): Shaun says the hosts “infiltrate cults and fringe science groups” in a show that’s funny without looking down on its subjects.
- [On the Media](http://www.onthemedia.org/podcast/): Covering media coverage of the week’s news.
- [The Moth](http://themoth.org/about/programs/the-moth-podcast): True stories from the storytelling non-profit, which also holds live events across the country.
- [Roderick on the Line](http://www.merlinmann.com/roderick/): Musician John Roderick talks to Merlin Mann (see Back to Work in the Productivity category).
- [Serial](http://serialpodcast.org/): The show, sponsored by Mail…kimp? that got everyone talking about podcasts again (or in the first place).
- [This American Life](http://www.npr.org/programs/ted-radio-hour/?showDate=2015-01-30%3ETED%20Radio%20Hour%3C/a%3E:%20Based%20on%20TEDtalks%20from%20notable%20speakers.%3C/li%3E%3Cli%3E%3Ca%20href=): The Ira Glass Fan Cub.
- [Throwing Shade](http://www.throwingshade.com/): Funny and acerbic discussion of women’s rights, gay rights, politics and pop culture.
- [The Thom Hartmann Program](http://xray.fm/shows/thom_hartmann): The political commentator’s nationally syndicated radio show.
- [Wait…Wait…Don’t Tell Me!](http://www.npr.org/programs/wait-wait-dont-tell-me/): ’s weekly news quiz show.
- [Fireside Mystery Theatre](http://www.firesidemysterytheatre.com/): This group of actors and musicians describe their monthly performances as “an old-fashioned, live radio show with a modern horror twist.”
- [Yo, Is This Racist?](http://www.earwolf.com/show/yo-is-this-racist/): Probably. Hosted by the creator of the identically named [Tumblr](http://yoisthisracist.com/).

**Science/History**

- [Hardcore History](http://www.dancarlin.com/hardcore-history-series/): A longform, audiobook-style show that covers historical events in detail.
- [In Our Time with Melvyn Bragg](http://www.bbc.co.uk/podcasts/series/iot): A long-running radio show about the history of ideas.
- [Invisibilia](http://www.npr.org/programs/invisibilia/): ’s new show about human behavior.
- [The Memory Palace](http://thememorypalace.us/): Short stories about historical moments and figures.
- [Nature](http://www.nature.com/nature/podcast/): From the journalists behind Nature magazine.
- [Nerdette](http://nerdettepodcast.com/): Two women host this show, which provides “a safe space for nerding out” on all kinds of things.
- [Professor Blastoff](http://www.earwolf.com/show/professor-blastoff/): Stand-up comic Tig Notaro and her friends talk about science and other things.
- [Radiolab](http://www.radiolab.org/): An immensely popular show on mostly scientific topics, presented in an engaging and accessible way.
- [Sawbones](http://www.maximumfun.org/shows/sawbones): A doctor and her husband, Justin McElroy (of two other podcasts listed above) explore the weird and gross corners of medical history.
- [Stuff You Missed in History Class](http://www.missedinhistory.com/podcasts/): Uncovering hidden stories and weird facts.
- [Stuff You Should Know](http://www.stuffyoushouldknow.com/podcasts/): More interesting stuff, not just of the historical variety.
- [The Titanium Physicists Podcast](http://titaniumphysicists.brachiolopemedia.com/): Physicists explain complex topics to regular folks.
- [A Way with Words](http://www.waywordradio.org/): A fun look at language.
