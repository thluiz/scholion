# **H**ow to brainstorm your way to a solution in 10 mins

Stuck**?**

Got a Problem**?**

Looking for a solution?

[![bb4b8410be18548ba68bfcf5aed2ffc2.jpg](growahealthychurch-com--how-to-brainstorm-your-way-to-a-solution-in-10-mins/bb4b8410be18548ba68bfcf5aed2ffc2.jpg)](http://growahealthychurch.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/07/stuck.jpg)

[Michael Hyatt](http://michaelhyatt.com/how-to-train-your-brain.html)  has posted a brilliant tip on how to brainstorm your way to a solution in 10 mins**.**

Here’s how it works.

Grab a pen**.**

Blank piece of paper.

Start the clock**.**

Dump EVERY solution you can think of onto that piece of paper for exactly 10 mins**.**

Don’t think just write!

Then analyse the results & I guarantee you will be closer to the answer**.**

I’ve done this many times before but have never set myself a 10 min time limit which I think is the brilliance of this tip**.**

So stuck for sermon ideas? New ways to pray**?** Ways to increase your income? Ways to lower your expenditure**?**

Give it a go & let me how it worked for you**.**
