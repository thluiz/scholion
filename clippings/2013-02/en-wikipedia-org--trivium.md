# **T**rivium

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

This article is about the educational syllabus**.** For the band, see [Trivium (band)](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trivium_(band) "Trivium (band)") **.** For other uses, see [Trivium (disambiguation)](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trivium_(disambiguation) "Trivium (disambiguation)") **.**

In [medieval universities](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medieval_university "Medieval university") , the **trivium** comprised the three subjects that were taught first: [grammar](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammar "Grammar") , [logic](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logic "Logic") , and [rhetoric](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rhetoric "Rhetoric") **.** The word is a [Latin](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latin "Latin") term meaning "the three ways" or "the three roads" forming the foundation of a [medieval](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Middle_Ages "Middle Ages") [liberal arts](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liberal_arts "Liberal arts") education**.** This study was preparatory for the [quadrivium](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quadrivium "Quadrivium") , which consists of geometry, arithmetic, astronomy, and music**.** Combining the trivium and quadrivium results in the seven liberal arts of classical study**.** The trivium is implicit in the *De nuptiis* of [Martianus Capella](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martianus_Capella "Martianus Capella") , although the term was not used until the [Carolingian era](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carolingian_era "Carolingian era") when it was coined in imitation of the earlier [quadrivium](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quadrivium "Quadrivium") **.**[1] It was later systematized in part by [Petrus Ramus](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petrus_Ramus "Petrus Ramus") as an essential part of [Ramism](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramism "Ramism") **.**

## Description

[Grammar](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammar "Grammar") is the mechanics of a language (always Latin, at the time);

[logic](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logic "Logic") (or [dialectic](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dialectic "Dialectic") ) is the "mechanics" of [thought](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thought "Thought") and [analysis](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Analysis "Analysis") ;

[rhetoric](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rhetoric "Rhetoric") is the use of language to instruct and persuade**.** [Sister Miriam Joseph](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sister_Miriam_Joseph "Sister Miriam Joseph") described the three parts of the Trivium thus:

> Grammar is the art of inventing symbols and combining them to express thought; logic is the art of thinking; and rhetoric, the art of communicating thought from one mind to another, the adaptation of language to circumstance**.**

Another description is:

> **Grammar is concerned with the thing as-it-is-symbolized**,  
> **Logic is concerned with the thing as-it-is-known**, and  
> **Rhetoric is concerned with the thing as-it-is-communicated.**[2]

The study of grammar, logic and rhetoric was considered preparatory for the quadrivium, which was made up of [arithmetic](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arithmetic "Arithmetic") , [geometry](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geometry "Geometry") , [music](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Music "Music") , and [astronomy](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomy "Astronomy") **.** The trivium was the beginning of the [liberal arts](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liberal_arts "Liberal arts") **.** At many [medieval universities](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medieval_university "Medieval university") this would have been the principal [undergraduate](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Undergraduate "Undergraduate") course**.** However, the contrast between the simpler trivium and more difficult quadrivium gave rise to the word "[trivial](http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/trivial "wikt:trivial") "**.**[3]
