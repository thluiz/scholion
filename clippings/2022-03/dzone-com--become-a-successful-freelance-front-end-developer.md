Businesses nowadays have started using internet marketing, which will definitely be in long-term demand, and this generates the need for developers who have skills in back-end, front-end, and full-stack development. Also, if we look from the customer point of view, then the customers will not stick to your page if it has a slow loading speed, the interface is not user-friendly, the product and service offerings are noticeably unclear, and other issues. This is one of the reasons why companies are beginning to hire front-end developers — to ensure that they are consistent in the quality they offer. But in some cases, companies do not have regular work, and they divide their tasks into smaller batches for which they do not need a full-time dedicated resource. And so, they look for a front-end development services provider or freelance developers as per their requirement.

This shows that it is an apt opportunity to build a career as a freelance front-end developer, as this will give you a chance to work on different exciting projects. So, let us understand further how you can develop your career in this field and what your role is.

## What Is a Front-End Developer?

Front-end developers are the ones who develops the interface of the webpage that the customer actually sees. They develop a page that covers CSS, JavaScript, HTML, and among other code that allows the users to interact with the page. In fact, the text which is visible to you right now on the screen is all because of the efforts of some front-end development team working behind it. The stronger the foundation of the front-end developer, the better their output will be. The front-end developers should have knowledge of the common frameworks like Backbone, Angular JS, Bootstrap, Ember.js, and also learn the jQuery-like libraries. Now once you have a sturdy base, you can definitely have your own set of clients if you follow the steps religiously.

## Benefits of Being a Freelancer

Being a freelancer has its own advantage. Let's learn about in detail:

- Being a freelancer helps you to maintain a better work-life balance as you can work while spending time with family or while you are on vacation, and this opens you to a world of opportunities.
- It is a great opportunity to be your own boss. You are the only employee of your own business and can work anytime, day or night.
- You are free to choose your own working hours or clients or even the payment rate.
- It's an opportunity to work on different kinds of projects which gives you exposure to a variety of works and cultures.

So, if you are interested in freelance front-end developmen, then you should look into the steps in detail.

## Steps to Become a Successful Freelance Front-End Developer

### 1) Learn the Related Languages and Focus on Your Skills

The first step will be to learn the core primary coding languages which include HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. The more you are aware of the different languages, the better you will become at troubleshooting. Do not just be a random or an average developer, but be an expert developer. There are other languages that you can learn (Ruby on Rails, Swift, and Node.js are recently the trending ones).

And when you work regularly on a particular programming language, then off-course you become an expert, and then word-of-mouth becomes enough to get you more business. Although acquiring skills is a time-taking process, it will definitely prepare a solid foundation which will make the rest of the process quite easy. So, at first, spend your maximum amount of time learning and practicing new skills, honing them, and being on the top of all the trending languages.

### 2) Develop a Portfolio

Now, once you have learned and practiced enough to work on some projects, then it's time to show your work. For this, you need to develop a portfolio that is strong enough to highlight your skills and refer easily to any of your clients. While learning the skills, you might have developed different websites, and you must have worked for a few clients as well. These are good places to start gathering your work to present it in the best possible manner. During the process of building the website, you get a chance to demonstrate and improve your skillset.

The portfolio works as a shop window so ensure that the contents, videos, and links are all on point. You can also post a blog stating your experience, which will make you more visible among your prospects.

### 3) Develop Yourself as a Brand

Marketing is the link that creates a liaison between you and your customer. And this is one thing that you can make as creative as you want and will help set you apart from the competition. In today’s age, there are several marketing options available, some with certain costs and some without costs, so you need to decide based on your budget. One can use social media channels and leverage them to earn benefits for getting the business leads.

Make a strong profile on LinkedIn that will inform your network about your work, knowledge, and certification — this will brief your prospect customers about your overall work and performance. On Instagram, one can use hashtags to tag the relevant industries that can become leads for the developer. Develop your YouTube channel and grow your audience by pushing for subscribers.

Register yourself on online platforms like Upwork and Fiverr and mention your rates there. These platforms help you meet with potential clients.

All of these activities will definitely help you develop yourself as a strong and reliable brand.

### 4) Manage Your Profile

You need to be more organized when you are working for yourself. Manage the number of clients you are working for, the name of clients, what their projects are about, what their deadline is, what the respective fees of all the projects individually are, and many more details. If you are not organized, it is possible that you might create a mess that will definitely hamper your performance. There are several software programs available online to manage your work, including Google Drive, Trello, FreshBooks, Controller, Hubstaff, BillingsPro, etc.

### 5) Be a Part of the Related Community

Even the experienced and expert developers want the support of the community to learn more, as learning has no end. So, once you have acquired the basic knowledge, join the community over on Facebook, Quora, LinkedIn, or any other platform where related users keep on posting about their experiences, bug fixation issues, new discoveries in projects, or suggestions in the forum. By joining these groups, you will be aware of what is going on around the world in that particular field. You can also get constructive feedback and support from these groups and communities.

### 6) Start as Freelance Front-End Developer

Finally, start taking big projects, be strong enough to face the clients and their queries, and then accordingly help them with the answers. Try to be as genuine as possible, take enough time to understand the project, and then give a realistic timeline in which you will be able to submit your work. Also, be clear in your payment terms and post-sale services.

## Conclusion

Being a freelancer is not an easy task as you are not associated with any firm or big company, so you need to put in all your efforts to develop your name as a brand. Develop your reputation as a hard worker, smart performer, reliable and trustworthy developer, and programmer. If you follow the above-mentioned steps in sequence, then you will definitely get the desired results.
