> ## Excerpt
> First dates can be nerve-wracking. You're trying to leave a good impression and get to know someone, so what should be a fun, new experience often turns into an awkward interview. Instead of sticking to boring small talk, try mixing in some interesting topics that spark real conversations. These conversation starters can help you connect, relax, and get to know each other in a natural way. Here they are!

## More for You

1. What Is Your Favorite Thing That You Bought Under $100?
2. What Beliefs Did You Change in the Last Few Years?
3. What Tiny Habit Improved Your Life?
4. If You Looked Back at This Date When You're 80, What Advice Would You Give Yourself?
5. What's an Embarrassing Opinion You Feel Strongly About?
6. When Do You Feel Most You?
7. If You Could Get a 6-Month Paid Leave, What Would You Do?
8. What Failure Actually Made Your Life Better?
9. What's a Personal Rule You Follow that Most People Don't?
10. What Do You Wish People Asked You About More Often?
