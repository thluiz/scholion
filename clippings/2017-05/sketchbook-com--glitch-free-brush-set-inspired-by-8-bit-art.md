### Download the set

The [Glitch Brush Set](http://www.sketchbook.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2016/12/Glitch-Brush-Set.skbrushes) is a collection of intentionally malformed textures that are extremely playful. They are especially good for drawing landscapes that have an artificial or mirage-like look to them, like this image of a ruined, apocalyptic landscape:

[![glitch-artwork-1](https://i1.wp.com/www.sketchbook.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2016/11/glitch-artwork-1.png?resize=800%2C577)](https://i1.wp.com/www.sketchbook.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2016/11/glitch-artwork-1.png)

### Pixels, glitches, flickers, and freezes

Each individual brush pays homage to retro 8-bit video games and takes its name from various types of infamous screen glitches. Glitch features a mix of misplaced buggy squares and static textures that allow you to capture radical rhythmic repetition by adding pixels, glitches, flickers, and freezes to your brush strokes.

![glitch-sample](https://i1.wp.com/www.sketchbook.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2016/11/Glitch-Sample.jpg?resize=800%2C578)

### Blend them for a more streamlined look

Despite the choppy origin of this glitchy brush set’s inspiration, the blendable brush options for each unique buggy brush makes it possible to create not only flat two-dimentional illustrations, but also realistic realms using free-flowing brushstrokes.

[![glitch-artwork-2](https://i2.wp.com/www.sketchbook.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2016/11/glitch-artwork-2.png?resize=800%2C577)](https://i2.wp.com/www.sketchbook.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2016/11/glitch-artwork-2.png)
