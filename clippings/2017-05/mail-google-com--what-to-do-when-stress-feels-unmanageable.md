"Stress is a systems problem."

That sounds like a Zen koan, but it's also true.

When we're feeling overwhelmed or out of control, it's often because we're facing an amorphous mass of tasks and responsibilities, with no idea where to start or how to make progress.

But when we have a system - a clear path forward - we know where to apply our efforts. Your project might take a long time to accomplish, and it may not be easy, but it's manageable.

I'm pretty productive, mostly because I'm good at focusing on one task at a time.

But I'd be even better if I had real *systems* in place.

Some people just see the world that way. They intuitively grasp the sage business advice that if you have to repeat a task - ever! - then it's worth investing five minutes to move toward automating or standardizing it.

My friend Jenny Blake is one of those systems thinkers. We've both written two books (her latest is the career reinvention guide *[Pivot](https://el2.convertkit-mail2.com/c/e5ukd9gega7hp8l4r/3ydpyg/aHR0cDovL2Ftem4udG8vMnJ0TXM1RA==)*), so by this point, I have a lot of marketing tips rolling around in my head.

That's nice, but in the meantime, Jenny actually put her knowledge on paper - a Google spreadsheet, to be precise - and created a free, massive [15-tab book marketing checklist](https://el2.convertkit-mail2.com/c/e5ukd9gega7hp8l4r/8ku2d3w/aHR0cHM6Ly9kb2NzLmdvb2dsZS5jb20vc3ByZWFkc2hlZXRzL2QvMUJSUmtuTGs0bF96X2FHTjFmc0d1NE5JUUtOSXM4M0RYQTFPYnk1M0VxTU0vdGVtcGxhdGUvcHJldmlldz91c3A9ZHJpdmVfd2ViIw==) to help other aspiring bestsellers.

She does that for *everything*.

A couple of months ago, Jenny was experiencing a 'good problem to have.' Her speaking business around *Pivot* was taking off, so she was traveling all the time, and she'd just moved in with her boyfriend (mazels!).

But she was so busy, she felt other aspects of her life slipping through the cracks.

In the spirit of creating what we most need, Jenny decided to double down on systems and delegation. She realized she needed to get more strategic about what she should and shouldn't be spending her time on (perhaps you can relate).

She became ruthless about outsourcing, delegation, and learning to say no strategically. And - of course - she captured it all down.
