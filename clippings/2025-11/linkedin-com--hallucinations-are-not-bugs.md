Hallucinations are NOT bugs.  
  
When asked specific document backed facts GPT-4 hallucinates up to 30% of the references. Simpler factual prompts? Still ~5% wrong.  
  
Current technology is not going to solve this: Hallucinations are the product of how large language models actually work. Not mistakes. Not glitches. Just math doing what math does.  
  
This isn’t a “garbage in, garbage out” issue (though that too exists). It’s deeper: a structural trade-off between generalisation and precision. Between sounding right and being right.
