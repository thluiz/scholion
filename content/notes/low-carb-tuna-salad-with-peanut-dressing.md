This recipe frames canned tuna as a convenient, high-protein pantry staple that benefits from a different dressing. Instead of mayonnaise, it uses a sesame-peanut dressing with avocado oil, peanut butter, lemon juice, tamari, and a little maple syrup.

It serves two and combines tuna with salad greens, Napa cabbage, cucumber, red onion, green onions, peanuts, and sesame seeds. The instructions say to marinate the tuna with part of the dressing, then toss everything together and divide it between two plates. The recipe also notes that it works well if the salad and dressing are packed in separate airtight containers.
