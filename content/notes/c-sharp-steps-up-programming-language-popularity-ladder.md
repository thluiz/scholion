Microsoft's C# rose one place year over year in the June 2020 TIOBE Index, moving to fifth. The index ranks languages by search engine results and related measures of developer activity.

## Reading notes

- C# moved up to fifth place in the June 2020 TIOBE Index, one step above its June 2019 position.
- C remained at the top, followed by Java, Python, and C++.
- The TIOBE Index uses search engine results, skilled engineers worldwide, courses, and third-party vendors to estimate where the most code is written.
- C# has been rising in popularity since an early 2018 low, after a peak in 2012.
- Microsoft presented new C# features during the previous month's Build conference.
