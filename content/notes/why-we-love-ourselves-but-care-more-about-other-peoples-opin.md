Marcus Aurelius says people love themselves more than anyone else, yet often value other people’s opinions more than their own. The post explains this as a common human tendency and uses it to argue for more confidence in one’s own judgment.

## Reading notes

- People put their own survival, well-being, and preferences first, even when they are generous or selfless.
- In matters of opinion, people often look to others first and let outside judgment shape how they see themselves.
- Repeated criticism can make someone accept a negative view of themselves, even when they do not truly believe it.
- If everyone around you dislikes something you enjoyed, you may start doubting your own response.
- The takeaway is to value your own opinion of yourself and what you like, and to notice when other people’s views start to overpower it.
- The piece also says to be guided by facts when they are presented well, while still keeping pride in a personal perspective that may differ from others.
