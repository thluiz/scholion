The post presents tower defense as an accessible genre for people who want to start making games. It says the format works well on smartphones and tablets because players choose defenses, place them on the map, and manage resources while enemies cross the area.

It also points to examples like Plants vs. Zombies and Pirates Love Daisies, then discusses tools for building similar games, including EaselJS, TweenJS, SoundJS, PreloadJS, CraftyJS, ImpactJS, and the ScriptTD engine. It closes by stressing gameplay balance, the role of level designers, and the ease of distributing and selling games through app stores.
