The post presents schoolwork as something predictable and says students can prepare in advance by using Evernote templates. It groups templates for note-taking, planning, and extracurricular organization, and also points to tools for collecting research, PDFs, and audio or photo captures.

## Reading notes

- School tasks are described as repeatable, including notes, lectures, papers, exams, and pop quizzes.
- Cornell notes are recommended for breaking course material into small parts and starting each page with the day’s essential question.
- The Class Notes template is meant for notes, questions, homework, and to-do lists from each class session.
- Reminders can be attached to class notes so they prompt reading and follow-up.
- Notes can be shared with other students, who can open them even without an Evernote account.
- Conference Notes are presented for lectures at academic or professional conferences and for following speaker slides.
- Evernote’s audio recording feature and in-app camera can capture lectures, slides, whiteboard notes, and handwritten notes.
- Web Clipper can gather online research material and save the complete source URL.
- PDF files can be imported or scanned, then annotated with notes, highlights, and arrows.
- Monthly, weekly, and daily planning templates are offered to manage school schedules.
- Club and team templates cover meeting notes, agendas, projects, goals, budgets, marketing plans, and brainstorming.
- To use templates, the post says to create a Templates notebook, copy templates into the working notebook, edit the copy, and share notebooks with classmates if needed.
