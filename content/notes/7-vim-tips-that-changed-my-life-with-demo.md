The author says these are quick Vim tips learned from others while using Vim full time at work. The post also recommends starting with basic learning resources and treating Vim as something you improve in small steps, not something you must master all at once.

## Reading notes

- Use `S` to start typing on a new line at the correct indentation without tabbing first.
- Use `CTRL+w =` to make split windows the same size again.
- Use `%` with the cursor on a bracket or brace to jump to its matching pair.
- Use `>>` and `<<` to indent or unindent one line.
- Use `>` and `<` on selected lines to indent or unindent multiple lines.
- Use `=G` from the top of the file to fix indentation in the whole file.
- Use `:tabnew` to open a new tab, `gt` and `gT` to move between tabs, and `:tabo` to close the other tabs.
- Use `CTRL+o` to jump back to a previous cursor position, including a file you were just editing.
