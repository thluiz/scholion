The survey says quality matters more than speed for most respondents, with 70 percent willing to delay the product roadmap to avoid a critical error affecting users. It also says production issues remain common, pressure to move fast is a major challenge, and troubleshooting takes a noticeable share of work time.

## Reading notes

- 70 percent of respondents say quality is paramount and they would rather delay the product roadmap than risk a critical error affecting users.
- 53 percent say they encounter critical or customer-impacting issues in production at least once a month.
- A quarter of participants say more than 40 percent of critical production issues are first reported by end users or customers rather than internal mechanisms.
- Continuous Integration and Continuous Delivery are among the top three investment areas.
- 45 percent say pressure to move fast is one of their top software quality challenges.
- 59 percent say they release new code or features from bi-weekly to multiple times a day.
- Two out of three respondents spend at least one day a week troubleshooting code issues, and 30 percent spend two days to a full week.
- The top adoption plans for 2020 are static analysis and dynamic code analysis, behind DevOps and microservices or containers.
