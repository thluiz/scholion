Christian Heilmann says free services should stop punishing users. He argues that people should not be told to accept spying, battery drain, or lost data just because an app is free, and that companies need models that respect privacy and transparency.

## Reading notes

- Free services can sell users as products, which should make the wider audience more willing to pay for privacy.
- Collusion can help spread awareness about how free applications use people’s data.
- Young users often share everything, create new identities, and leave third-party data full of fake information.
- Mozilla Persona is presented as a way to avoid sharing everything and to reduce provider control over user data.
- Heilmann does not want to kill free apps, only to stop users from being punished for using them.
- He supports paid tiers that give more data access and transparency, including the ability to block data use.
- He says ad-supported apps often make users suffer because the payment system is harder than showing ads.
- He thinks ad display as a viable monetisation strategy should be phased out.
