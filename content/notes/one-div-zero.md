The page presents a parody creed about yak shaving in programming. It treats the yak as the developer’s constant companion and frames shaving it as an absorbing task that displaces productive work. The text says the programmer and the yak both recognize that coding output and computation do not matter here as much as the yak’s hair, which keeps demanding attention.

It also portrays the programmer as studying the yak in detail and becoming entangled with it, much like with the previous yak. By the end, the programmer swears that both of them are what blocks productivity and delays release, and that this continues until the code ships and nothing remains but hair.

## Reading notes

- The text is written as a mock oath centered on yak shaving.
- The yak is described as personally attached to the speaker and central to their work life.
- The speaker says the yak becomes hairy without intervention, while the speaker becomes productive without the yak.
- Shaving is presented as a recurring task that must be repeated before the hair returns.
- The page says that, in this situation, the focus is not code, processing, or efficiency, but the yak’s hair.
- The speaker claims they will learn the yak completely, including its features, weaknesses, and strengths.
- The relationship is described as close enough that the speaker and the yak become intertwined.
- The closing lines identify the speaker and the yak as the obstacle preventing productivity and delaying release.
