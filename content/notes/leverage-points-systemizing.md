**00:15:59** — Leverage points decide what's worth systemizing

Brooks borrows the concept of leverage points from systems-thinking literature to push back on the urge to systematize everything. The right question isn't "what could I turn into a system?" but "where would a system actually change the before/after state in a meaningful way?" He links this to shiny-object syndrome — the show's running theme — extended from apps to systems themselves, where chasing every systemizable corner of life becomes its own form of distraction. Leverage points let you pick the one or two places where a system pays back disproportionately.
