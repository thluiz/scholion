Joe Navarro says the shoulders are an underused part of body language. He treats them as a visible clue to strength, hierarchy, joy, depression, and confidence, and says they often confirm or complicate what people say aloud.

## Reading notes

- shoulders are prominent and can reveal health, emotion, and communication cues
- wide muscular shoulders are linked to strength, virility, health, and vitality
- shoulders can signal dominance, hierarchy, leadership, and respect
- dancing uses shoulder movement to communicate sensuality and joy
- slumped, heavy shoulders can signal depression and lack of movement over time
- a quick shoulder shrug can reinforce a verbal answer like “I really don’t know”
- a slight shoulder rise can show uncertainty or lack of confidence rather than deception
- observing shoulders can help clinicians or interviewers ask better follow-up questions
- standing straight with shoulders back shapes how others see you
