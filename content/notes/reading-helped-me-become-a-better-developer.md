Reading has helped the author level up as a developer. She points to documentation, newsletters, and articles as the three sources she reads most often, and says they each support learning in different ways.

## Reading notes

- Documentation is useful because it is updated as technology changes, often has a solid getting started section, and can even lead to a first open source pull request.
- Reading documentation can help build a stronger grasp of a topic and can save time compared with relying on the first Stack Overflow answer that works.
- Newsletters bring relevant resources directly to the reader, help keep someone up to date on new technologies, and can reduce imposter syndrome by making new tools feel less overwhelming.
- Articles are helpful after documentation feels difficult, because they often explain concepts one step further and focus on the why behind them.
- The author says she enjoys reading on her commute and that it gets her excited to start the day and work at her computer.
