Leo Babauta reflects on the end of the 2010s and on what Zen Habits became over the decade. He says the years brought growth in the blog and his work, but also loss, deeper practice, and a stronger sense of purpose.

## Reading notes

- Things did not unfold as expected, and he treats that lack of control as something to trust and meet with curiosity.
- Any area of life can be deepened, including mindfulness, relationships, purpose, health, and self-relationship.
- Self-trust and self-compassion are two central shifts he sees as roots of many people’s struggles.
- Working with people in person felt powerful and became a part of his work through retreats, workshops, and one-on-one coaching.
- Focus is a limited resource that shapes how energy, time, and money are used, and more intentional focus leads to more meaningful work.
- He came to see purpose as a deep motivation connected to love for other people and the wish to help with their struggles.
- Relationships need active care, and he regrets not always spending enough quality time with people he lost.
- A vegan diet and lifestyle feel meaningful to him, and he describes them as not difficult and at least as healthy as other lifestyles.
- Training with discomfort and uncertainty is hard but rewarding, and it includes practices such as the Goruck Challenge, ultramarathon, and meditation.
- The deaths of his father, father-in-law, and friend Scott were devastating, but grieving them opened his heart in new ways.
