- In Forest, starting a 30‑minute timer grows a tree if you stay in the app; leaving early makes the tree die, tying focus to visible progress.

- The app provides thousands of predefined connections that users can select to streamline common actions.
- Examples of IFTTT automations include creating a Google Calendar event from an Evernote reminder and saving Google Docs to Dropbox.
- Other IFTTT recipes route photos directly to services like Facebook or Flickr without manual uploads.
- The text also highlights IFTTT uses that improve convenience, such as turning lights on at sunset, emailing about next‑day rain, or starting music when you wake.

- In Productive, users can define habits and routines, schedule them across days, weeks, and months, and set reminders to stay on track.

- Completing tasks in Productive creates visible chains of finished habits, which the author says encourages sticking with routines.

- The text notes that even non‑hourly workers can benefit from tracking time to understand how they spend their day and plan it more effectively.
