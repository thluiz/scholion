Basecamp offers automatic check-ins that ask a team a chosen question on a fixed schedule. The user sets the question, the people to ask, and how often it runs, and Basecamp sends notifications and collects the answers.

The page says check-ins can be daily, weekly, or less frequent, depending on the need. It also says answers can start a discussion, and follow-ups stay directly under the original answer so they are easy to find later.
