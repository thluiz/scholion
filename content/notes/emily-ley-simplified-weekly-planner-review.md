The review describes the Emily Ley Simplified Weekly Planner as a colorful hardcover planner with tabs, sewn binding, monthly and weekly spreads, notes pages, yearly prep, a bucket list, and one sheet of stickers. It also notes the gift box packaging, the rainbow cover, and the price of $50 USD plus shipping.

## Reading notes

- Hardcover planner with sewn binding, tabs, colorful inside pages, lined notes pages, yearly prep, bucket list, and one sheet of functional stickers
- Monthly calendar starts on Sunday while the weekly spread starts on Monday
- Monthly boxes are split diagonally, and the weekly spread includes pre-printed Sunday prep tasks
- The planner has a gift box, one ribbon bookmark, and no back pocket folder
- The reviewer likes the bright rainbow cover and coordinated colors
- The reviewer says the planner is expensive and international shipping is expensive
- The pages show ghosting from the pre-printed ink, so the paper quality disappointed the reviewer
- The tabs are laminated extensions of the pages and are protected by the hardcover, but they are not sturdy
- The reviewer prefers the Emily Ley and At A Glance collaboration planner over the original Simplified Planner
- The sticker book has good color printing, but the sheets come loose easily and the reviewer would not buy it again
