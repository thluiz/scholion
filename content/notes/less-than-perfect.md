The post says the pressure to make every new piece better than the last can block publishing when attention is limited. It uses Gary Vaynerchuk’s early video blog, a Reddit answer, and a pilot podcast as examples of work that started badly, improved through repetition, and still found an audience.

It argues for keeping channels where quality expectations are low, so rough drafts can exist and later be refined. When standards get too high in one place, the answer is to find another place to start again.
