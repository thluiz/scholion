## Reading notes

- RethinkX released a report called “Rethinking Climate Change” about cutting emissions through disruptions in energy, transportation, and food.
- The report says the world already has the technologies needed to reduce more than 90% of greenhouse gas emissions within 15 years.
- It argues that the right societal choices can keep global warming well below 2 degrees Celsius.
- The largest energy-sector opportunities are solar PVs, wind power, and lithium-ion storage batteries.
- In transportation, the report points to electric vehicles and autonomous EVs, with transportation-as-a-service and the end of internal combustion engines and private vehicle ownership.
- In food, it highlights precision fermentation and cellular agriculture as technologies that can replace meat and animal-based products.
- The report says these technologies become even more effective when feedback loops between the three sectors are considered.
- It says disruptive technologies can help close wealth and technology gaps by scaling electricity access to billions of people without electricity today.
- One scenario, “Get Serious,” projects over 60% lower GHG emissions by 2030 and 100% before 2035.
- Another, “Be Sensible,” says 90% GHG reductions by 2035 and net zero by 2040 are possible by removing deployment barriers and supporting core technologies.
- The “Get Stuck” scenario warns that delays, fossil fuel subsidies, utility monopolies, and support for livestock could push temperatures above 2 degrees Celsius.
- The report includes a framework for judging the mitigation-readiness of climate solution technologies.
