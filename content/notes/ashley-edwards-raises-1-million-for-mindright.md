Ashley Edwards founded MindRight Health to provide trauma-informed, culturally responsive mental-health coaching by text message for communities of color. The company says it serves young people with daily support, and it raised more than $1 million in venture funding less than a year after launch.

The article says the funding makes Edwards one of only 35 Black female founders in the U.S. to reach that level and the first in New Jersey. It also notes support from investors including Esther Dyson, Newark Venture Partners, Telosity, Acumen America, and Impact America Fund, along with a partnership with RWJBarnabas Health.
