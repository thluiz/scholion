Consumer Reports removed its recommended badge from Microsoft’s entire Surface lineup after finding the hardware significantly less reliable than most other brands. In a survey of 90,000 tablet and laptop owners, about 25 percent of Surface users reported problems by the end of the second year, including freezing, unexpected shutdowns, and touchscreen response issues.

Microsoft rejected the conclusion and said the findings do not accurately reflect Surface owners’ experiences or the improvements made in each Surface generation. The survey period ended before the Surface Laptop and updated Surface Pro were released, so the result likely reflected earlier devices such as the Surface Pro 4 and Surface Book.
