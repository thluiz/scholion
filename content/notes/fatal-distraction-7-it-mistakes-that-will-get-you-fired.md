The piece collects seven true stories in which IT workers were fired after errors or misconduct that damaged systems, exposed private information, or undermined trust. It frames job loss as the outcome of avoidable mistakes and weak judgment.

## Reading notes

- Weak or broken backups can turn a security incident into a major business loss, and the person responsible for backups may lose the job the next day.
- Reading the boss’s email and other executives’ messages without permission can lead to immediate termination.
- Trying to hide an error after a storage failure makes the outcome worse, and the one person who told the truth kept the job.
- Using company systems or media for porn, or running adult sites on company servers, can get an admin fired on the spot.
- Refusing to document network changes can leave others unable to recover from an outage and can cost the engineer the job.
- A disaster-recovery plan that has never been tested may fail when an outage happens, and the CTO can be dismissed after the delay and damage.
- Telling a senior manager that his “vision” is only a maintenance plan gets the employee fired, though in the story that also leads him to start his own business.
