**00:29:13** — Custom GPT runs Tim's weekly review

Tim describes his current weekly review system: a custom ChatGPT he built about a year and a half ago, holding 15–20 review questions, run in voice mode for around twenty minutes every week. Instead of a static template, the AI conducts the review like an interviewer, opening with prompts like "what was the headline this past week?" to force a zoom-out view. The setup turns the abstract idea of a weekly review into something that physically reaches out to him on schedule, making it harder to skip and easier to think out loud through.
