Leo Babauta says online reading has reduced his book reading, but a few small habits have helped him read books more again. He points to repeated reading triggers, treating reading as something enjoyable, choosing books that sound appealing, always carrying a book, and closing devices while reading.

## Reading notes

- Build reading into several daily triggers, such as after stretching and meditating, during meals, and before bed.
- Read small amounts at each trigger, since a few pages per session can add up to a full book over time.
- Treat reading as a break and a pleasure, not as a chore to rush through.
- Stop reading books that bore you after giving them a chance.
- Carry a book everywhere so waiting time can become reading time.
- Put phones and laptops away, or in another room, so notifications do not interrupt reading.
