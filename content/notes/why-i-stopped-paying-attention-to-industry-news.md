The writer says he stopped reading industry news after noticing it felt like overconsumption. Cutting it out made him calmer, less anxious, and more focused on the work and people he can actually control.

## Reading notes

- He compares the experiment to cutting added sugar, because both changes were meant to show what life felt like without something he had been consuming automatically.
- He used to check Hacker News, Reddit, and Twitter for tech news many times a day.
- He stopped because he felt too absorbed, too anxious, and too concerned about what he should know.
- In the first weeks, he still encountered tech news on Twitter and had trouble avoiding it.
- He muted friends for 30 days when they tweeted about tech so his stream would contain less of that content.
- After a few months, he felt better mentally and physically, with more calm and more time.
- He says he focused more on what he could control, like his product, company, and personal work, instead of the next hot startup or the next “Basecamp killer.”
- A year later, he still does not read industry news, but he sometimes hears about important things later in conversations that are more useful and less sensationalized.
