Meta suspended an Instagram account called the “University of Melbourne Gooning Club” after the university reported it for alleged infringement of its trademark and intellectual property rights. The account used the handle “unimelbgooning,” posted satirical memes about “gooning,” and used an edited university logo.

The university said it had recently become aware of the account through routine social media monitoring and acted quickly to have it removed. It said the account raised significant concerns, that it prioritizes the safety and wellbeing of its community, and that it had no evidence the account was run by staff or students.
