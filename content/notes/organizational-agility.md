Agile is presented as a way to learn faster through frequent feedback, not as a practice for IT or engineering alone. The text says the values and principles need to spread across the organization, including HR, customer service, marketing, finance, and even the kitchen, or technical teams will not truly be agile.

## Reading notes

- Agile is about learning faster through frequent and rapid feedback.
- Limiting Agile to IT or engineering misses its purpose.
- The values and principles need to be propagated, understood, and shared across the organization.
- Salesforce is cited as an example of applying Agile principles to HR and recruitment.
- Organizational agility combines clear vision, top-down trust, and cross-functional, self-managing ownership.
- Teams should choose their own useful metrics to know when work is done and decide what to do next.
- The approach accepts ambiguity and can look chaotic to managers.
- A leader’s role is to assemble a strong team, explain the vision clearly, and give people what they need to succeed.
