Kevin Rose describes fasting as an emotional process that starts with hunger and ends with a kind of surrender, as the body adapts and mental clarity returns. He treats cold exposure as a way to resist constant comfort and to recreate some of the conditions and challenges of earlier life.

He says Tim Ferriss helped introduce him to Stoicism, along with quotes in *Ego is the Enemy*. He practices surrender daily, sees Stoicism as a prompt for deep work and personal development, and admires the Japanese respect for duty and craft.

## Reading notes

- Fasting is presented as a multi-day practice that moves from willpower to surrender and then to renewed mental clarity.
- Cold showers, ice baths, and other forms of cold exposure are framed as ways to challenge constant comfort and imitate ancestral conditions.
- Tim Ferriss’s example and *Ego is the Enemy* helped him start reading Stoic philosophy.
- He practices surrender daily and favors Alan Watts’s image of trusting oneself to the water.
- He thinks Stoicism fits technologists because it can lead from curiosity into deeper learning about the self.
- His interest in Eastern philosophy is tied to Japan, tea, and a respect for detailed, patient work.
- He admires artisans who are devoted to the craft itself rather than money or fame.
