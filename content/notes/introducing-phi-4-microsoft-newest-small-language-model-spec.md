Microsoft introduces Phi-4 as a 14B-parameter small language model that handles complex reasoning, including math, alongside conventional language processing. It is presented as the latest model in the Phi family and is available on Azure AI Foundry and Hugging Face.

## Reading notes

- Phi-4 is described as a state-of-the-art small language model with 14B parameters.
- The model is said to excel at complex reasoning, especially in math.
- Microsoft presents Phi-4 as the newest member of the Phi family.
- The post says Phi-4 outperforms comparable and larger models on math-related reasoning.
- The gains are attributed to high-quality synthetic datasets, curation of high-quality organic data, and post-training innovations.
- The post says Phi-4 pushes the frontier of size versus quality.
- It is reported to outperform much larger models, including Gemini Pro 1.5, on math competition problems.
- Microsoft points readers to the technical paper on arXiv for more benchmarks.
- The post says Microsoft’s responsible AI capabilities are available to customers building with Phi models.
- Azure AI Foundry is described as providing tools to measure, mitigate, and manage AI risks across development.
- Azure AI evaluations in AI Foundry support iterative assessment of quality and safety with built-in and custom metrics.
- Azure AI Content Safety features listed include prompt shields, protected material detection, and groundedness detection.
- The post says developers can use these capabilities as content filters through a single API and monitor applications in production for quality, safety, adversarial prompt attacks, and data integrity.
