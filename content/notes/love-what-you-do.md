The author says long-term staying power comes from building skill and managing energy, not from following passion alone. Success reinforces itself, but only if the work is sustainable and the rest of life is protected.

He says sacrificing happiness for a company usually leads to burnout, worse work, narrower options, and resentment. He describes taking all vacation time offered, being available when working, and protecting family time, creative time, and travel with loved ones as part of the same long-term discipline.
