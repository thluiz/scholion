**00:08:48** — You Don't Have to Read Every Page

Jonathan Cottrell shares a liberating insight about reading: you don't have to read every last page of a book for it to be valuable. Management books in particular don't need to be read like narratives the way we were taught in school — they can be used more like references. You can put a book down and come back to read different portions at different times, especially as new management challenges arise that make specific chapters suddenly relevant.
