The post describes the Blowhard Jamboree as an anti-pattern where a tech leader pushes AI or AR because they are fashionable, not because they are mature or proven for the problem at hand. It shows how this can drain resources, delay the current product, and leave the team exposed if the new technology does not work.

## Reading notes

- A CTO interrupts a solar app demo to push AI for automatic panel placement and an ARKit app for roof previews, then asks for the whole team to build a proof of concept quickly.
- The pattern is identified as a tech leader taking big risks with a cool but untested new technology.
- The symptoms in the meeting are the promotion of sexy new technologies, the fact that they are not mature, and the frivolous use of resources.
- The project can go off the rails if machine learning cannot produce useful solar designs or if ARKit cannot render the installation reliably.
- The root causes are insecurity, lack of team feedback, and unchallenged assumptions about whether AI and AR help the business.
- The fix is for the CTO to co-create the architecture with the architect, let the team challenge assumptions, identify risks, keep the regular app on schedule, and keep a fallback plan in case the new technology fails.
