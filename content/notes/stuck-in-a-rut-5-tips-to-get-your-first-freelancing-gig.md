The post argues that freelancing is harder to enter than many people expect, and that quitting a day job to freelance full-time can leave someone short of money. It recommends starting on the side and building up income before making the jump.

## Reading notes

- Choose two or three skills that are marketable and develop them more carefully, instead of trying to do everything.
- Present yourself to the market honestly about your abilities and limits, knowing what your own story is and what your own selling point is.
- Send proposals without fear of rejection and keep trying until one is accepted.
- Project confidence, without confusing that with arrogance, because clients judge what a person projects before knowing the work.
- Talk to many people and use word-of-mouth referrals, since references remain one of the best ways to get work.
