The post says writing matters for software engineers because it helps teams reach decisions, keeps a single source of truth, and saves time by replacing repeated meetings. It also says writing well is not a talent but a skill that improves with practice.

## Reading notes

- Writing documents helps engineers align on decisions and avoid long debates.
- A one-page summary can turn weeks of discussion into a short meeting.
- A well-written document can serve as the team's single source of truth.
- Writing can reduce the need for multiple meetings when a decision can be explained clearly on paper.
- The framework for better writing is Identify, Iterate, and Invite.
- Identify the audience before drafting, since engineers, product managers, executives, and mixed audiences need different levels of detail.
- Define the core message so the audience remembers one main takeaway.
- Keep the writing coherent by reinforcing the message across sections.
- Proofread with the audience in mind to check clarity and flow.
