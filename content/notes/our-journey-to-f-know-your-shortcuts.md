The author, a long-time C# developer and heavy ReSharper/Rider user, says F# required retraining his fingers for shortcuts he was not used to. He highlights two shortcuts that became useful in that workflow.

## Reading notes

- Error Description, mapped to Ctrl+F1 or Alt+T, is used to show a function signature when calling a function, faster than hovering for a tooltip.
- The author notes that the visualization of Error Description is weak and that larger signatures are hard to read, while hoping Rider tooltips improve in a future version.
- In F#, Go to definition, mapped to Ctrl+Shift+F11, takes the user directly from a value to its declaring type, which is more useful than F12 when types are inferred.
- The author says this shortcut works the same in C#, but many peers know F12 and not Ctrl+Shift+F11.
