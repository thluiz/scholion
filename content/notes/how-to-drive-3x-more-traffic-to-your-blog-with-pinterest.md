The post argues that Pinterest is a useful traffic source for bloggers, even outside food, fashion, or DIY niches. It points to business accounts, rich pins, and analytics as starting points, then focuses on pin copy, timing, image design, group boards, and Social Warfare Pro as ways to increase clicks and reach.

## Reading notes

- Pinterest can be useful for bloggers and businesses, not only for recipe, DIY, or fashion content.
- A business account gives access to analytics after website verification and allows applications for Rich Pins.
- Article pins can promote blog posts with a logo, larger headline, and a link back to the site.
- Pin descriptions should be helpful, detailed, interesting, and actionable, with concise SEO-friendly keywords.
- The post recommends pinning at times when the audience is active and being consistent without over-pinning.
- Pinterest favors vertical images, especially with a 2:3 or 4:5 ratio, and images should be high quality, simple, and visually strong.
- Adding text to images can make pins clearer and more clickable.
- Group boards can expand reach, and the post suggests finding them, joining them, or creating them.
- Social Warfare Pro can help by adding Pinterest buttons, hover Save buttons, and post-specific Pinterest images and descriptions.
