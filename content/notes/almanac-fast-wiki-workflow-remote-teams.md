Almanac is presented as a fast wiki and workflow tool for distributed teams to consolidate documents into a single, organized source of truth. The page emphasizes time savings, fewer meetings, and reduced tool sprawl for teams dissatisfied with existing tools like Confluence, Notion, and Asana. It highlights features to reduce information anxiety, streamline approvals, and keep teams aligned on important knowledge and tasks.

The product centers on a unified workspace where docs, tasks, and reviews live together. Capabilities include powerful search within document bodies, version control for handbooks, deprecation reviews to keep content current, and structured collaboration through Layers, approvals, and read receipts. Almanac also supports building public-facing microsites, integrates with tools such as Jira, GitHub, Figma, Google Workspace, Slack, and more, and offers security features like SOC2/HIPAA compliance, SSO/SAML, and granular permissions.
