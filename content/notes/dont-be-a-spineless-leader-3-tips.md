The post argues that leaders fail when they say yes to every request, agree with every pushback, or make decisions without the right information. It says this creates distrust and frustration, and that leadership requires clarity, courage, and the willingness to handle disagreement.

## Reading notes

- Leaders should not try to please everyone, because that usually upsets everyone over time.
- If there is pressure to decide quickly, it is better to ask for help or take time to think through the request.
- When the leader is not the decision maker, the decision should be delegated to the right person or discussed with them.
- A leader’s job is to align upper management, the team, and partner teams so the work runs smoothly.
- With the team, the leader should shield them from external noise but still explain real concerns when needed.
- With upper management, the leader should speak up about aggressive deadlines and provide missing visibility.
- With partner teams, the leader should hold them accountable when promises slip and raise risks that could harm the team.
- When disagreeing, the leader should ask clarifying questions, state the disagreement clearly, share the concern, and suggest an alternative.
- Conflicts should be handled with patience, focusing on the problem rather than the people involved.
