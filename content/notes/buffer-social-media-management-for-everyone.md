Buffer presents itself as a social media workspace that works across the platforms people post to and connects with other tools. It groups its product around publishing, content creation, community replies, and insights, and adds collaboration, a mobile app, and an AI assistant.

## Reading notes

- It schedules content for platforms including Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, Threads, Bluesky, YouTube Shorts, Pinterest, Google Business, Mastodon, X, and Substack.
- It says users can create, organize, and repurpose content for different channels, with an AI Assistant available when needed.
- It offers a dashboard for triaging and responding to comments across channels.
- It says its insights help users learn what works and how to improve, whether through basic analytics or deeper reporting.
- It includes collaboration tools for managing, editing, and approving posts from a team.
- It offers a mobile app for managing social media accounts from anywhere.
- It says the AI assistant can brainstorm ideas, rewrite content, and craft platform-specific posts.
- It describes an MCP integration that lets users connect Buffer to Claude or ChatGPT, ask for performance data, plan content from what is working, and send content to the queue.
- It says customer support is human and available worldwide.
- It says Buffer is an open company that shares finances, team salaries, and other key metrics openly.
