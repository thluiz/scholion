The piece argues that fan mail can be effective if it stays brief, names a specific work, and explains why it mattered. It also says the message should not ask for anything and should not be followed up on if there is no reply.

## Reading notes

- Keep the email to four to six sentences so it feels less intimidating and is easier to answer.
- Say exactly what work you liked and why it connected with you.
- Do not ask for anything, since that makes the note feel more like a networking email.
- Do not keep following up; a reply is only a bonus.
- A short, specific fan email can open a natural dialogue without trying to force one.
