Writing is presented as a major part of leadership work because emails, presentations, articles, and talks all start with writing. The author says technical leaders do not need formal writing training to improve and recommends five books that can help them write to encourage and educate.

## Reading notes

- Writing clear and persuasive text is essential for effective leadership, and writing is part of the job every day.
- Writing Tools: 50 Essential Strategies for Every Writer is described as a practical starting point, with short chapters, clear rules, examples, and workshop exercises.
- The Sense of Style is recommended for its focus on classic style, clear and direct language, and an updated look at grammar and parts of speech.
- On Writing Well is presented as a long-standing nonfiction guide with advice on tone, style, and different nonfiction forms.
- Everybody Writes stands out for its guidance on structure, style, brand journalism, and writing for different channels like blogs and tweets.
- Draft No. 4 is described as an essay collection about the writing process, story progression, structure, and reference frames.
- The article closes by saying that reading about writing supports the advice to read a lot and write a lot, and that these books can help readers influence and inspire through writing.
