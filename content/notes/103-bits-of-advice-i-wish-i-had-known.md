## Reading notes

- The list is presented as advice he wishes he had known when young, added to over several years.
- Right timing is usually now, and small actions repeated consistently matter more than occasional bursts.
- Treat people well, forgive, keep promises, pay what you owe, and be generous with teachers, workers, and those who can do nothing for you.
- Leadership should create more leaders, not followers, and criticism belongs in private while praise belongs in public.
- Communication matters in speaking, writing, and negotiation, including pausing, listening well, and choosing the right moment to ask for salary or help.
- Curiosity, detours, trying new things, and doing work that stretches you are presented as better paths than routine optimization.
- Save, back up data, keep useful things visible, notice hidden costs, and spend time on the long game because decade-long effort compounds.
- Personal growth is tied to uncomfortable conversations, becoming the kind of person you want to be, and staying astonished rather than settling into certainty.
