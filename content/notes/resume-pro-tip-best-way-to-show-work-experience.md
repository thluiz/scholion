The post says work experience bullets are stronger when they emphasize what was accomplished and include numbers. It recommends framing each bullet as “Accomplished X by implementing Y” and argues that measurable impact makes a resume more memorable to recruiters.

## Reading notes

- Compare bullets by how much impact they show, not by how much activity they describe.
- Use the format “Accomplished X by implementing Y.”
- Show numbers in every bullet when possible.
- If you do not have metrics, start measuring what your work changes.
- The post gives examples such as faster prototypes, better loading time, shorter deploy time, and faster data flow.
