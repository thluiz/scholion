## Reading notes

- Edison’s test for potential employees was filled with trivia that was considered largely irrelevant to work under him.
- The leaked questions became a topic of national discussion after publication in the *New York Times* in May 1921.
- Newspapers copied and parodied the test, and by May 13, 1921 a full set of 146 questions and answers had been published.
- The *Chicago Tribune* gave students at the University of Chicago a short version and reported low scores.
- Reporters also quizzed Albert Einstein and Edison’s son Theodore, both of whom did poorly on the test.
- The article says Edison created the test because he was frustrated with college-educated applicants who lacked the knowledge he wanted.
- The National Park Service notes that the test changed several times after it leaked with the answers.
