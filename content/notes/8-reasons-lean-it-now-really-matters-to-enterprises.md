Steve Bell presents Lean IT as a response to the scaling problems Agile faces in large organizations. The article says Lean methods push IT to focus on customers, manage uncertainty, treat innovation as part of daily work, and connect technology more closely to business improvement.

## Reading notes

- Technology can affect every product, service, and industry, but IT has to stop thinking only about its own assets and start asking what customers want.
- Innovation should be part of ordinary business work, not confined to R&D or development teams.
- Planning a year ahead is described as harmful to innovation, so governance and budgeting need balance with purpose-driven teams.
- Agile often breaks down in large enterprises because of dependencies, and Lean methods are presented as a way to improve scalability.
- ERP is framed as a major issue, and Bell argues it could fit a Lean enterprise if it becomes more Agile, reduces waste, and supports data-driven decisions.
- Analytics has to be developed intentionally, because strong analytics skills are rare and weak substitutes can hide poor decision-making.
- Lean programs need shared ownership across stakeholders, with attention to value streams from end to end.
- IT priorities should be ranked against long-term breakthrough goals, while “dark matter” work that consumes capacity but stays invisible should be removed.
