The article defines a superpower as the contribution a person brings to work, or what they do better than anyone else. It says using that strength helps the team and can make work more satisfying. The piece also argues that teams work better when people understand one another’s strengths and use them together.

## Reading notes

- Superpowers are described as individual strengths people bring to their work, not as job titles or fixed roles.
- The article says team building should focus on combinations of strengths rather than hiring for the same kinds of pedigree.

- The text says a shared language about strengths helps teammates notice how colleagues can contribute and how their work can be acknowledged.
- Each strength has a shadow side, and the article says this is important to recognize in order to work well with a team.
- Empathy can slow decision-making when it leads someone to weigh every side for too long.
- Systems thinking can become a weakness when attention to process holds things up.
