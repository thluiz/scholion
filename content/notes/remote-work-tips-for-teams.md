Remote work changes how teams coordinate, so the post recommends aligning on a common toolset and using integrations to make meetings, chat, calendar status, and call setup easier. It also argues for video-first communication, more intentional check-ins, an all-team async channel, and daily status updates to preserve visibility and reduce isolation.

## Reading notes

- Teams working remotely should agree on the same tools for video, chat, document sharing, and possibly even the IDE.
- Integrations can reduce friction, such as linking Zoom with Google Calendar or Slack, and syncing calendar status to chat presence.
- Video calls help with turn-taking and screen sharing, and everyone should be able to share their screen when needed.
- Remote work removes casual contact, so teams need to be more intentional about unplanned conversations and check-ins.
- An all-team channel can limit overuse of DMs, support more real-time feedback, reduce email volume, and add some social energy.
- A daily status bot can increase communication and visibility, helping people see overlapping work and join calls when useful.
- Remote workers should stay in touch with friends during the workday, even when the conversation is not about work.
- The post ends by saying proactive teams can adapt to remote work and stay productive while keeping people safe and healthy.
