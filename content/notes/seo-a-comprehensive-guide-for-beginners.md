The post presents SEO as a way to help people find what they are already looking for, not just as a source of free traffic. It says the basics can be learned quickly, then points to keyword research, long-tail terms, content optimization, code, site structure, and links as the main areas to work on.

## Reading notes

- SEO is about matching website content to what people are trying to find, so traffic only matters if it can convert.
- Keyword research should look for high search volume, low competition, and relevance to the site.
- Long-tail keywords can add up to most of a site’s traffic.
- Content should be written for people first, while still helping search engine bots understand it.
- Titles, keywords, links, quality, and freshness all matter for content optimization.
- Search engine bots read code too, so title tags, meta descriptions, headings, sitemaps, domain names, URL structure, site structure, and alt tags should be optimized.
- Links are described as one of the most important ranking factors.
- Effective links come from related sites, use strong anchor text, and are more useful when they appear in content.
- The post warns against spammy links, sitewide links, excessive exact-match anchor text, reciprocal links, and paid text links that get caught.
- The closing advice is to find what people want, build a site that gives it to them, and then optimize so search engines can help people find it.
