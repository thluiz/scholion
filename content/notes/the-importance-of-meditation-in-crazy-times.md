Meditation is presented as especially useful during periods of change, disruption, uncertainty, fear, and anxiety. The text says that usual coping habits like distraction, procrastination, control, quitting, complaining, and worrying do not work well in this kind of crisis.

## Reading notes

- Meditation is described as a medicine for uncertainty and anxiety, though not as magic.
- It helps people notice anxious thoughts and the feelings that arise with them.
- It can interrupt thought cycles by shifting attention to bodily sensations.
- It can calm people enough to see that, in the present moment, things are not dangerous.
- It can bring calm focus when someone needs to get things done.
- A meditation habit is made by choosing a regular time, setting reminders, starting with 2 minutes, paying attention to the breath, and thanking yourself when the timer ends.
- The text also invites readers to join the Zen Habits Meditation Room on Zoom.
- It mentions a Daily Meditation Challenge in April as a way to build a daily meditation habit.
