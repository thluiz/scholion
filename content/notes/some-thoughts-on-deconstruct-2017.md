The post says Deconstruct is a technology-agnostic conference carefully curated by Gary Bernhardt, with a single track, no sponsors, and speakers chosen for high-impact talks. The author finds the lineup inspiring and notes that the conference felt safer than many tech spaces, but not inclusive or comfortable enough.

She describes feeling out of place because many attendees were men, few women or people of color were present, and people often assumed she was a bootcamp student. She highlights Jenn Schiffer, Suz Hinton, David Nolen, Justin Searls, and Michael Bernstein, and ends by asking attendees to bring someone who does not look like them next year.
