The post argues that productivity advice loses its effect if notifications keep interrupting attention. It recommends disabling notifications on phones, tablets, and computers, keeping only the few that are truly needed, and treating email as an asynchronous channel.

- A proposta central é cortar todas as notificações em todos os dispositivos.
