Microsoft has published the Build 2020 agenda on the Build website after opening registration the week before. The schedule shows the event starting at 8am Pacific, with keynote sessions and repeated blocks for people in different time zones.

## Reading notes

- The event begins with "Empowering every developer" from Satya Nadella, followed by the Imagine Cup, a session with Scott Hanselman, and a session with Scott Guthrie.
- The May 19 schedule also includes "Building the tools for modern work" with Rajesh Jha, Digital Breakouts with live Q&A, and a Social Hour.
- The day two schedule repeats some sessions, including Azure for every developer, The future of tech, and Power Platform for developers, to fit different time zones.
- The rest of the event will be breakout sessions, but that schedule is not listed yet.
- The schedule is not yet listed in the Microsoft Events app, and it is unclear whether Microsoft will publish it there.
