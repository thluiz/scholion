The article argues that genuinely successful people are hard to spot because many people claim titles and status without real experience. It says the difference shows in behavior, especially in how people work, lead, and present themselves.

## Reading notes

- They run real companies with real products, customers, and management experience.
- They love the work itself and talk about the company rather than their status.
- They lead in their own way instead of copying a template.
- They know what they do not know and stay humble.
- They use common sense and reject hype, quick fixes, and miracle cures.
- They are never fully satisfied with what they have already achieved.
- They are usually not highly visible and are not motivated by fame.
- They are not trying to sell you something.
- They do not self-promote because their work speaks for itself.
- They do not preach or claim to have the key to success or happiness.
