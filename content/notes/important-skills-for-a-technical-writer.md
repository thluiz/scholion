The post says a technical writer needs technological knowledge, clear writing, problem-solving, cross-functional teamwork, communication with subject matter experts, flexibility during product changes, and the ability to present complex ideas graphically. It also says writing improves over time and that a better version of an existing document matters.

## Reading notes

- Research technology before writing, and keep up with enough of it to give effective and meaningful information.
- Write clearly and concisely, and use tools like Grammarly or help from the SME when needed.
- Treat problem solving and troubleshooting as basic skills for software documentation.
- Stay in touch with cross-functional teams so deadlines, product releases, and product facts stay current.
- Communicate with SMEs to support a more complete research document.
- Be flexible with overtime and last-minute changes during product release work.
- Present complex ideas graphically when possible, since it adds value even if it is not required.
- Accept that writing is never perfect and that improving an existing document is part of the work.
