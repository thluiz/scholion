David Seah’s task nagger is an arm-mounted index card scabbard for immediate to-do items and small bits of physical data. It is meant to stay visible while you are on the move and out of the way while you work.

## Reading notes

- It combines an archery arm guard, a clear vinyl pocket protector, Super Sticky Post-It notes, and index cards.
- Seah describes it as a mini-desktop for immediate to-do items and a place to keep physical data handy.
- It can be strapped to the inside or outside of the forearm.
- David says he put it together for about 7 bucks.
- The article says it may get puzzled looks at the office, but could help keep attention on what needs to get done today.
