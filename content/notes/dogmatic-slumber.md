The comic frames Kant’s “dogmatic slumber” as a response to Hume. It says that, after reading Hume’s skepticism about causation and morality, Kant turned from trying to reconcile empiricism and rationalism toward explaining morality and the structure of conscious experience.

## Reading notes

- The strip addresses the idea that Hume would have “woken” Kant from his dogmatic slumber.

- After encountering Hume’s skepticism about causation and morality, Kant would have been led to seek a rational foundation for morality.
