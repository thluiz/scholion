The article argues that the job market for software engineers is shifting fast. Hiring as it exists now is expected to collapse, while fewer jobs will remain because AI will make software work cheaper and more accessible for smaller businesses. The author says the useful response is to build broad systems thinking, understand the real problem, and avoid fixating on a single solution.
