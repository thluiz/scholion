The comic lists several philosophical attempts to define what makes humans distinct. It moves from Plato’s “featherless biped” and Aristotle’s “rational animal” to Descartes, Schopenhauer, Nietzsche, Sartre, Wittgenstein, and Derrida, each framed as a different answer to the same question.

- Sartre trata a liberdade como traço essencial do humano.
- Wittgenstein aponta a linguagem como a característica mais singular e definidora.
