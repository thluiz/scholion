Patti Smith’s advice centers on doing the work you feel called to do, even without broad acceptance. She also stresses keeping a clean name, avoiding compromise, and protecting your work, while accepting that life will include both perfect moments and rough spots.

## Reading notes

- An artist, or any person, should know what they want and pursue it.
- A writer or artist should keep doing the work because it is a calling.
- William S. Burroughs’s advice to Smith was to build a good name, keep it clean, avoid compromises, and focus on good work and right choices.
- If you build a good name, that name can become its own currency.
- Smith links artistry with seeing what others could not.
- She describes life as a roller coaster with perfect moments and rough spots.
