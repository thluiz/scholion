**00:07:16** — Five Systemic Perspectives as Analytical Lenses

Jackson introduces the five systemic perspectives that form the core analytical toolkit of critical systems thinking: mechanistic, interrelationships, organismic, purposeful, and societal/environmental. Each perspective functions as a distinct lens that stretches a leader's imagination and reveals different aspects of a problem situation. These perspectives are described as partial but proven tools — ways of engaging with natural and social systems that have demonstrated value over time. Understanding the differences between them gives practitioners five complementary framings for any challenge they face.
