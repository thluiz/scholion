The page argues that doubts about money, skill, boredom, attention, and material costs are reasons to rethink quitting art, not to stop. It frames each problem as something artists can work through by continuing practice and changing how they work.

## Reading notes

- The page says many artists feel self-doubt and wonder whether making art is worth it.
- It presents Gabriel Nkweti Lafitte as someone who combines a coffee shop job with art and follows his artistic conscience.
- It says self-doubt often comes from trying to draw or paint subjects you do not really understand yet.
- It advises going back to basics, studying one part at a time, and practicing anatomy in small steps.
- It says boredom can come from distraction and suggests changing methods, materials, or subject matter.
- It says art should stay enjoyable, because people can tell when the artist is not engaged.
- It says artists should not expect attention to come on its own and should keep promoting their work.
- It recommends replying to people, promoting other artists, posting regularly, and showing work in progress.
- It says art does not have to be expensive because cheaper alternatives exist.
