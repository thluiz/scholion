The author set out to make YouTube his 2020 goal and aimed to create 365 videos, ideally one each day. He found that the work was larger than expected, but daily practice helped him learn recording, editing, thumbnails, equipment, and YouTube Studio.

He says he did not focus much on subscribers or views at first. Instead, he kept making videos, learned that valuable quality content attracts viewers, and refined his goals toward design and development videos. He also noticed that some non-educational videos performed better, but chose to concentrate on topics he enjoyed and on videos that were useful, well planned, and reviewed before publishing.

- Started YouTube as a 2020 goal and aimed for 365 videos in the year.

- Learned OBS, Adobe Premiere, Adobe Photoshop, camera, microphone, greenscreen, and YouTube Studio through practice.

- Plans to keep making videos and cares more about content and value than subscriber counts or views.
