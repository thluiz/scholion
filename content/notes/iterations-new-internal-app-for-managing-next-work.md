37signals built Iterations to answer what the team should work on next and who will do it. The app gathers feature and bug requests in one place, lets people discuss them, and helps form teams around the ideas they want to work on.

## Reading notes

- It centralizes ideas that were previously scattered across heads, Backpack, Campfire, and private lists.
- It accepts quick, not fully formed ideas and lets a team build on them.
- It helps expose small projects that fit short windows of downtime.
- Team members can propose ideas, discuss them, +1 the ones they support, and show interest in working on an iteration.
- It shows who proposed an idea, who supports it, and who wants to work on it, along with room for details, implementation, and scope.
- It also shows which projects are not viable and which ideas can be dismissed.
- People can align themselves with ideas they are excited about, and teams can form more organically around that interest.
- New teams can review earlier interest in projects to find common ground.
- The app keeps a history of proposed iterations, which is useful when an iteration ends without a launch or when an exploration is not pursued further.
- The core of the app was designed in-person during a company meetup, then built in about a week.
- It has been used since early September and later gained integration with Backpack Journal and Campfire rooms.
- The result is a more scoped view of what people are working on, what they want to work on, and what matters.
