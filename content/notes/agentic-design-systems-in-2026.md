Brad Frost presents DS+AI as a way to connect generative AI with the structure of a design system. He says this pairing can help teams move faster in collaboration sessions while keeping generated UI aligned with established standards.

## Reading notes

- He describes a discussion with Storybook about the combination of design systems and AI, and notes Storybook’s new MCP tool for generating UI from component libraries.
- He says coded prototypes help avoid unproductive back-and-forth in design reviews, and that AI plus a design system can create more collaborative workflows.
- He says non-technical teammates can “mouth code” a feature during collaboration sessions and watch it take shape using production-grade design system foundations.
- He distinguishes DS+AI from vibe coding by saying the AI is deliberately constrained to the organization’s high-quality design system materials.
