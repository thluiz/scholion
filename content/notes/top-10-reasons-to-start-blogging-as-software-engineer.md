The post presents blogging as a useful practice for software engineers because it helps them track their progress, share solutions with the community, and write down ideas before they are lost. It also says blogging can make problem solving easier, improve communication, and help people keep others updated on what they are doing.

It adds that writing posts can be enjoyable, help engineers meet others, learn from feedback, display skills, and establish expertise. The disclaimers stress that people should not start a blog mainly to make money and that not writing blog posts does not make someone any less of a software developer.

## Reading notes

- Blogging lets the author look back at earlier posts and see progress over time, which helps reduce imposter syndrome.
- Writing about a solved problem is presented as a way to give back to the community.
- Putting ideas on the page helps preserve them, organize them, and sometimes solve the problem being written about.
- Enjoying the process matters because it makes blogging easier to keep doing.
- Blog posts can lead to contact with other engineers who read, reply, and exchange ideas.
- Feedback from readers can improve both technical work and writing.
- Blogging can show skills over time and support interview situations.
- Writing for a broad audience helps software engineers explain ideas in simpler language.
- Repeated writing on a topic can make others see the author as an expert.
- A blog can be used to keep colleagues and others up to date by sharing a link instead of sending repeated emails.
