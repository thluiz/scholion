The post explains how the author grew a developer mailing list to over 1,000 signups in about six months. He says the results came from repeating simple promotional habits across blog posts, video tutorials, Reddit, and side projects.

## Reading notes

- Ask for signups on every blog post, since the posts are free technical content and can include a quick promotion.
- Publish regularly, because each post can bring search traffic and create chances for people to join the list.
- Write about what you learned, even if the topic is small, so the posts stay useful.
- Blog about topics that have been covered before, because a personal angle can still help readers.
- Crosspost to Dev.to and use canonical_url so search engines still treat the personal blog as the original source.
- Post to Reddit for traffic, while recognizing that the community can be harsh and negative.
- Send email about once a week, which matches the pace of new content and does not seem to drive many unsubscribes.
- Make video tutorials from blog topics and add mailing list links in the video notes.
- Ask for signups on side projects, such as a JavaScript quiz, and include links to the mailing list and YouTube channel.
