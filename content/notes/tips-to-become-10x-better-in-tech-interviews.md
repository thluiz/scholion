The newsletter says stronger interview results come less from chasing perfect readiness and more from building leverage ahead of time. It recommends using relationships instead of cold messages, treating each interview as a learning loop, and preparing stories and examples that can adapt to different companies and questions.

## Reading notes

- Build a network that can vouch for you before you need help, and keep adding value through work, advice, and support.
- Do not wait to feel fully prepared; line up multiple interviews, use earlier ones for practice, and learn from each failure.
- In behavioral interviews, collect 3-4 flexible stories, use network insight about company culture, and adapt examples when needed.
- For LeetCode-heavy processes, try to eliminate those rounds, offset them with more system design, or frame problems through a small set of patterns.
- In system design interviews, steer toward familiar areas, say when you do not know something, and use real-world examples and storytelling to answer clearly.
