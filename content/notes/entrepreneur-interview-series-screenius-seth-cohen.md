Seth Cohen describes Screenius as an iPad app for personalized video discovery, like a Pandora for video. He says it learns a user’s tastes from choices and actions, and that the product is moving toward social video by learning friends’ tastes too.

The interview also traces Cohen’s path from film and television into programming, software engineering, product management, and entrepreneurship. He says his biggest startup challenge is clarity of purpose, his biggest mistake was not doing enough rapid prototyping and customer validation early, and he believes the right team matters more than the right market or product.
