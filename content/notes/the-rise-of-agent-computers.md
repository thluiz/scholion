The post says the PC era is expanding into an agent era. AI agents can research, write, summarize, analyze, plan, and execute, and they are described as persistent collaborators rather than chatbots. The new device category, called an Agent Computer, is meant to run agents continuously so they can handle apps and tasks for the user.

It presents local, always-on agentic compute as useful for professionals, creators, developers, startups, and small businesses that want control over data, affordable AI, and hardware suited to parallel workloads. AMD positions its Ryzen AI Max systems, including the Ryzen AI Max+ 395, as well suited for this role, and the post closes with a warning that autonomous agents can behave unpredictably and should be used with caution.

- The PC is described as the machine you use, while the Agent Computer is the machine that works for you.
- AI agents are said to move across tasks, tools, and information continuously and at scale.
