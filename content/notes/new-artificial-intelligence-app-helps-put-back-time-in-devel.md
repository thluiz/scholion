Trevor AI is a virtual personal assistant app that uses artificial intelligence to help developers manage time and scheduling. It is available in the App Store, and Android development will begin after funding is secured. The app is free, syncs with a calendar, and uses natural language plus integrations to suggest schedules and track tasks.

## Reading notes

- The app is called Trevor AI and is available today in the App Store.
- Android development will begin once the app secures funding.
- Founder George Petrov started the project after bringing the idea to the Founder Institute in Sofia, Bulgaria, in December 2015.
- Trevor AI focuses on time, using natural language and integrations to help people plan their time.
- The app is meant to help individuals, especially developers, understand their time with analytics.
- Users agree to sync a calendar, and the app uses artificial intelligence to understand tasks and make suggestions based on lifestyle.
- Trevor keeps calendars and reminders in sync, and users can schedule tasks into their calendar with drag and drop.
- It can understand tasks such as “Exercise twice a week” and “Learn to code.”
- Developers are described as primary users because they can manage tasks in time and get more accurate timelines and time tracking.
- The team plans to open an API for developers after building a graph of users’ schedules, which Petrov says will take at least a year.
- JIRA and Trello integrations are planned for the next month.
- Petrov says integrations are important because they help users find their most important tasks and focus their energy and time on those tasks.
