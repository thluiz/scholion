The article treats “Why should we hire you?” as a question about fit, character, and how well a candidate can present themselves. It says interviewers use the answer to judge cultural fit, interest in the company, and whether the candidate connects their strengths to what the company needs.

It recommends preparing before the interview by clarifying what you want in a job, checking the job ad, researching the company, comparing your profile with theirs, and writing an honest story about the match. It also says to listen closely during the interview and use new details, such as training programs, community engagement, or future plans, as concrete reasons to hire you.

## Reading notes

- Treat the question as a check on fit, character, and self-presentation.
- Prepare before the interview by naming what you want from a job and comparing it with the role.
- Research the company’s background, values, reviews, and public image.
- Write down how you and the company match so you can answer with a clear story.
- Take notes during the interview and use new details in your answer.
- Use your answer to show multiple reasons to hire you, your ambition, and your interest in contributing.
