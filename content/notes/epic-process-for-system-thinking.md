**00:05:53** — The EPIC Process: A Four-Stage Methodology

The conversation turns to Jackson's EPIC methodology — Explore, Produce, Intervene, Check — a sequential framework for deploying systems methodologies in practice. Jackson is careful to frame it as an ideal type that draws on the best of the systems tradition, acknowledging that real-world applications will always face resource and political constraints that prevent full realization. The methodology is presented not as a rigid prescription but as a principled guide shaped by decades of systems practice.
