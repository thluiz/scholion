The article says developers should handle testing, with QA supporting the team through automated acceptance tests, corner cases, and exploratory testing. It then introduces the testing pyramid and places unit tests at the highest quantity because they are fast and cover small parts of the system.

## Reading notes

- Testing is best done by developers.
- QA is part of the team and can create automated acceptance tests.
- QA catches corner cases and does exploratory testing to identify system behaviors.
- The testing pyramid groups tests by quantity, from most common to least common.
- Unit tests check the smallest parts of the system, such as functions and classes.
- Unit tests should be close to 100% coverage because they run fast and can be used in larger numbers.
