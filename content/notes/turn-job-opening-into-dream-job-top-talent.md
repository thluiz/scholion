The article argues that most companies waste money on inefficiencies instead of investing in strong development teams, even though top developers can deliver order‑of‑magnitude more organizational value. Rather than copying typical perks, companies should differentiate with remote‑first culture, flexible hours, thoughtful compensation, serious mentorship, and realistic interviews that focus on real work. Pairing senior and junior developers, offering generous learning support, and structuring benefits around freedom and impact can turn a generic job opening into a dream role for top tech talent.
