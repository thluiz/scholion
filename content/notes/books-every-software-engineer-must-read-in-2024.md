The page uses the Lindy effect to argue that technologies and ideas with longer use are likelier to last, then applies that idea to software development. It says frameworks come and go, while fundamentals such as algorithms, clean code, design patterns, architecture, testing, distributed systems, and SQL stay useful across tools and languages. It then recommends a long list of books grouped around those areas, plus a few titles on leadership, teamwork, motivation, focus, and habits.
