The article argues that remote developers need discipline because they work without supervision. It recommends over-communicating, keeping messages moving, and investing in equipment and internet that make coding, testing, and calls easier.

It also says remote work benefits from a better home workspace, contact with other people, and self-care routines such as breaks, exercise, walks, and healthier food.

## Reading notes

- Remote work demands discipline because workers do everything themselves without being watched.
- Developers should keep people updated, stay available for messages, and check replies often.
- A fast computer helps with loading, opening windows, building code, reloading code, and running tests.
- A second monitor can help developers view more things side by side.
- Headphones, a webcam, comfortable keyboards and mice, and fast reliable internet are useful for remote work.
- Communication tools can also be used to socialize, and walking with neighbors is another way to avoid isolation.
- Remote workers can improve their own workspace with a better chair, a desk that fits their needs, and tools that reduce noise.
- Self-care includes taking breaks, keeping a fitness schedule, walking, and eating healthily.
