## Reading notes

- art (n.) is recorded in early Middle English as skill gained through learning or practice, from Old French art and Latin ars.
- In Middle English, it often meant scholarship and learning, especially the liberal arts.
- Later senses include human workmanship, a system of rules and traditions for performance, cunning, and creative arts.
- art (adj.) refers to something produced with conscious artistry, with art film and art rock given as later examples.
- art (v.) is the second-person singular present of be, from Old English eart, and became archaic in the 1800s.
