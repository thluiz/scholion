The post gathers suggestions for making remote work easier by separating work from home life, keeping a routine, and setting up a workspace that supports focus and comfort. It also adds habits for staying active, managing stress, and keeping in touch with coworkers and friends.

## Reading notes

- Set clear start and end times, and let teammates know them so breaks and logging off are easier to keep.
- Keep a routine similar to an office day, including waking up at the usual time, having breakfast, exercising, showering, and starting work on schedule.
- Wear regular clothes, or gym clothes if that feels easier, to make the line between home and work clearer.
- Use a large water bottle if you forget to refill your drink often.
- Cook simple meals or batch-cook for the week so lunch breaks are not spent cooking.
- Choose boring or healthy snacks, keep them out of reach, and take only a small portion at a time.
- Take short naps of 20 to 30 minutes to feel more rested and focused.
- Make time for exercise during the day, such as walking, running, yoga, HIIT, or indoor cycling.
- Use apps and programs such as Down Dog, C25K, Yoga With Adriene, Zwift, and Strava for workouts and tracking.
- Practice gratitude and compassion to care for your mind as well as your body.
- Step away from news if it increases anxiety, and use meditation apps to refocus when thoughts spiral.
- Tell coworkers when you will be away, when you are available, and when you will start or end work.
- Schedule online social time with coworkers and stay in touch with friends through calls, messages, and video chats.
- Find online activities to do with friends, including games and shared watching or messaging during shows.
- Use a desk or kitchen table instead of bed or sofa to avoid mixing sleep and work.
- Consider a monitor or laptop stand to improve screen height and reduce neck and back strain.
- Add an external mouse and keyboard for more comfortable typing, and a vertical mouse to help avoid repetitive strain injury.
- Use Sidecar on Mac with an iPad if that setup helps extend the desktop.
- Buy comfortable headphones with a microphone and noise cancelling so outside noise does not interrupt work.
- Keep the camera off when needed, since video is optional and can depend on comfort, space, or the situation.
