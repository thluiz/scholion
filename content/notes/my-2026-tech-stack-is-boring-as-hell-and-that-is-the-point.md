The author says he spent years chasing new tools and now prefers simplicity. In 2026, he rejects the hype cycle, the complexity of microservices, and stacks built to impress rather than to ship software.

He favors a boring monolith with one repo, one deploy, and one database. The stack he describes uses a single VPS, SQLite or Postgres, a standard REST API, and React without extra libraries. He says users care about features and working buttons, not the stack, and that boring tools save time, reduce hidden costs, and make it easier to finish products.
