**00:28:49** — IC vs Management: A Longer Career Than You Think

Larson identifies a perverse assumption in Silicon Valley: engineers default to thinking their career spans only 10-15 years, making the IC-vs-management decision feel permanent and high-stakes. Referencing Matt Klein (creator of Envoy at Lyft), who has a long career as an individual contributor by choice, Larson suggests the decision is less binary than it appears when you think in terms of a 30-40 year career rather than a decade.
