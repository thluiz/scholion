The article argues that soft skills can decide a JavaScript interview as much as technical knowledge. It recommends projecting confidence through appropriate clothing, eye contact, smiling, preparation, and a concise way of speaking. It also says interviewers often test communication, self-knowledge, problem solving, enthusiasm for the company, and whether you can move on from past jobs without resentment.

## Reading notes

- Dress for the role you are applying for, not overly formal and not as if you are on vacation.
- Make eye contact, smile, and show that you are glad to be there.
- Prepare for common interview questions and keep answers clear and short.
- For questions about yourself, have a personal brand, a mission, and a brief story that sets you apart.
- If you do not know an answer, say “I don’t know” instead of guessing or stumbling.
- For problem questions, explain the problem, the root cause, and the solution as simply as possible.
- If asked why you want the job, show real interest in the company, the people, the tech, or the challenge.
- If asked why you left a previous job, keep the answer short and avoid bad-mouthing anyone.
- Stay calm if the interview turns into a surprise challenge.
- Handle rejection gracefully, because it can lead to future opportunities.
- The article treats empathy as the most important soft skill in software development.
