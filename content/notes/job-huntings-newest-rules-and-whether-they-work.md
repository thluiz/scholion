Career experts from LinkedIn and Indeed review several job-hunting tactics that applicants use to stand out in a slow hiring market. They say some approaches can help visibility, but others are more myth than advantage, and the quality of the application still matters.

## Reading notes

- Applying early can increase visibility, and applying within 10 minutes of a LinkedIn alert may raise the chance of hearing back by up to four times.
- Applying early in the morning does not have clear data behind it; one expert sees business hours as a better bet, while another says there is no magic hour.
- Applying through a company website is often not a real advantage because many employers use integrated hiring systems that put website and platform applications in the same pipeline.
- Keeping a résumé focused on recent, relevant experience is advised, but this does not mean cutting off an entire career.
- Matching résumé and cover-letter keywords to the job description is presented as one of the strongest tactics because ATS systems scan for those terms.
- Interview preparation is described as easy to overlook, even though it is the stage where candidates have the most chance to stand out.
