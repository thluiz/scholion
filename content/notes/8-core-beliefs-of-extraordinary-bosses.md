The article says the best managers share eight core beliefs that change how they understand work, the company, and the team. It contrasts average bosses with extraordinary ones and presents management as service, not control.

## Reading notes

- Business should be understood as an ecosystem, not as a battlefield between companies, departments, and groups.
- A company is a community of people with hopes and dreams, not a machine with employees as cogs.
- Management should provide direction and resources, then let teams make decisions and act on their own.
- Employees should be treated as peers, with excellence expected at every level.
- Motivation should come from vision and a shared future, not from fear of punishment.
- Change should be treated as part of growth and accepted as necessary for success.
- Technology should free people to be creative and build better relationships, not just tighten control.
- Work should be enjoyable, and managers should place people in jobs that can make them happy.
