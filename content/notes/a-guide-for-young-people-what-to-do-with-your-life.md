The page says young people cannot figure out the future, because life changes, opportunities change, and many careers did not exist before. It recommends focusing on what is useful now, such as making things, learning skills, making friends, and practicing for whatever may come.

- The author answers a 15-year-old who feels pressure to choose a future career.
- The future cannot be planned with certainty, because life and opportunities change.

- Getting comfortable with uncertainty matters because important projects often require acting without knowing how they will turn out.
- Distraction and procrastination are treated as ways of avoiding discomfort.
- Meditation and blogging are presented as ways to understand how the mind works.

- Saving an emergency fund and investing in an index fund are recommended after earning money.

- Trustworthiness is built by showing up on time, doing good work, being honest, admitting mistakes, and meeting deadlines.
- If young people prepare in these ways, they are more ready to seize or create opportunities.
