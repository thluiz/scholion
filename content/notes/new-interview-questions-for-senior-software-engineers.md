Scott Hanselman collects questions meant to probe how experienced developers think about design, patterns, trade-offs, testing, maintainability, and learning. He says the list is not a guide to interviewing or a trivia quiz, but a shared pool of topics for people with years of software experience.

He also stresses that the best interview is not a rote checklist. Several comments push toward design exercises, code review, portfolio discussion, and questions about communication, judgment, and how a candidate approaches real problems.
