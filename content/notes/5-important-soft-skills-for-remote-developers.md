The article says soft skills are personal attributes that make people more effective at work, and argues that they can matter more than technical skills when someone wants to become indispensable. For remote developers, the piece stresses that these skills still matter and can shape hiring decisions.

## Reading notes

- communication skills matter more when colleagues and clients are not met in person, because non-verbal cues are reduced
- communication here means speaking and writing clearly, confidently, and precisely, including in presentations and video conferences
- developers need to explain technical matters to non-technical people, which makes clear communication especially valuable
- empathy helps people understand what others really mean, see situations from their perspective, and feel heard
- empathy can reduce conflict and make someone more approachable
- adaptability shows up in willingness to learn new technologies and accept feedback without taking it personally
- perseverance matters because programming can be slow and bugs can delay delivery
- perseverance is linked to getting things done and finishing work better and faster
- teamwork matters for remote developers because they still belong to a project team and a wider community
- being approachable, helpful, respectful, and patient supports collaboration
- the article recommends three books on soft skills, personal development, and emotional intelligence: How to Win Friends and Influence People, Mindset, and Working With Emotional Intelligence
