Bill Gates says artificial intelligence should be treated as a long-term concern. He argues that machines may first take on many jobs in ways that are useful if managed well, but that stronger intelligence could later become a problem.

He places that warning alongside Elon Musk and Stephen Hawking, and notes that work on systems like Cortana and Microsoft's Personal Agent shows how machine intelligence is already entering consumer products. He also tells a Reddit user that learning to program is still useful.
