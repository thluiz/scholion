Weil: "SORRY, I CAN'T NOW, DE BEAUVOIR, I'M OFF TO WORK AT THE AUTO FACTORY." Camus: "YOU WORK AT A FACTORY NOW? I THOUGHT YOU TAUGHT PHILOSOPHY." Weil: "YES, CAMUS, SIX DAYS A WEEK." Beauvoir: "WOW, SHE REALLY LIVES BY HER IDEALS, DOESN'T SHE?" Camus: "INDEED, SHE IS REALLY THE ONLY GREAT SPIRIT OF OUR TIMES." A silent panel. Sartre: "SUPER ANNOYING THOUGH, RIGHT?" Beauvoir: "YES! I'M SO GLAD YOU SAID THAT." Camus: "DOES SHE HAVE TO BE SO AUTHENTIC ALL THE TIME?" Sartre: "EXACTLY, WE HAVE TO BE AUTHENTIC ABOUT EATING CHEESE NOW? COME ON. THERE'S A LIMIT." Camus: "PRECISELY. MORE WINE?" Sartre: "PLEASE."](simone-weil-vs-the-existentialists.png)
