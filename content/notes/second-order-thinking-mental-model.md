Second-order thinking is presented as a way to make better decisions by looking past the immediate result and considering what happens next. The author uses the change from Rails Meeting to Backend Meetings as an example of a decision that worked at first but created longer-term problems.

The page suggests pausing before acting, checking whether a decision is reversible, asking “And then what?” several times, and using the 10-10-10 rule to compare consequences over different time spans. It closes by saying the goal is to think about long-term impact rather than only immediate outcomes.
