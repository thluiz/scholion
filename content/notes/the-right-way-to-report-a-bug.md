The page says that at Zerocracy bug reports are paid only when a project architect considers them good enough, and that this judgment is subjective and non-disputable. It then gives recommendations aimed at making a report more likely to be paid, especially by shaping how it is presented.

## Reading notes

- Stay calm and keep reporting bugs even when some are not accepted or paid.
- Present the bug as serious, even if it is minor, so the priority and severity do not work against you.
- Frame the problem as something that blocks you or a group of users from using the class.
- Ask why a report was not paid, but do not act offended if payment does not come.
- Make the report look substantial, detailed, formatted, and supported by links.
- Offer to investigate further and provide more details if needed.
- Show concern for the project, the users, the market, and the mission instead of appearing motivated only by money.
- Combine small cosmetic bugs into one report when that gives a better chance of acceptance.
