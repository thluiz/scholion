Babauta says fatherhood has been both terrifying and the most rewarding part of his life. He frames a father’s role as keeping a child loved and alive while accepting that the child is not something to shape at will.

## Reading notes

- The main duty is to love the child and be there for them.
- Keeping a child alive is the baseline; the harder part is helping them feel loved.
- A father’s example matters more than his words.
- Hugs and empathy are more important than punishment when a child behaves badly.
- Trusting children helps them learn to trust themselves and accept failure.
- Parents cannot control who a child becomes and should let them express themselves and make choices.
- Shared activities like reading, talking, walking, playing, and making things together are part of fatherhood.
- Fatherhood means being there until the child no longer needs you, and then again when they do.
