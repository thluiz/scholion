Mentat is presented as a job-search service for people who want a new job but have not organized themselves to apply, or are too busy to do it. The company takes over LinkedIn and resume optimization, writes cover letters, and has an advisor submit applications by hand.

It also assigns a real advisor for phone, chat, and email support, says it uses machine learning to match candidates with jobs and advisors, and charges $249 for a package that includes a money-back guarantee for at least one interview. It also offers a free tier similar to a traditional job board.

## Reading notes

- The service focuses on people who want to change jobs but need help taking action.
- Mentat handles the application work, including cover letters and manual submissions.
- Each user gets a dedicated advisor with industry expertise.
- The company says machine learning helps match job seekers with jobs and advisors.
- The paid package costs $249 and guarantees at least one interview or a refund.
- A free tier lets users search and apply for jobs like a standard job site.
- Mentat is also trying to partner with educational institutions so schools can buy the product for students.
