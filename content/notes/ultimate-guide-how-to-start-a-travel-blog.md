## Reading notes

- Travel and tourism generate large revenue, and the page frames travel blogging as a way to share trips and eventually earn income.
- Postach.io is presented as a blogging platform powered by Evernote, with SEO-friendly themes designed to help pages get indexed and found.
- The setup starts by creating an account, connecting an Evernote notebook, signing up for Evernote if needed, and authorizing Postach.io access.
- A first post is created in Evernote, with a clickable title, useful content, images, a Published tag, and a manual sync if needed.
- The site should then be checked in Postach.io to confirm the post appears correctly before customizing the design.
- The site name should be catchy and broad enough to cover future travel destinations, not too narrow to one region.
- A custom domain is recommended for a more professional presence and better traffic potential, and it is part of paid plans.
- The site description should tell search engines and visitors who you are, what you cover, and how you approach travel blogging.
- Visuals can be personalized with a logo and cover image, using personal photos or free design and image resources.
- Themes can be changed without losing posts or images, and the page ends by encouraging regular publishing and future monetization through ads, mentions, and affiliations.
