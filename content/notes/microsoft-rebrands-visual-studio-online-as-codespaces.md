Microsoft is renaming Visual Studio Online to Visual Studio Codespaces and says the change is meant to make clear the product is more than an editor in the browser. The new tagline says Codespaces are the most productive place to code, and the new name will appear in the coming weeks and months.

Microsoft is also cutting Codespaces prices starting May 19. The lower pricing includes Basic, Standard, and Premium instance types, while self-hosted environments can be registered and connected for free from VS Code or the browser editor.
