## Reading notes

- Princeton Artist Brush Co. is based in Princeton, New Jersey, and the review brings together five synthetic sets for watercolor, plus a few other brushes.
- The text highlights the brand’s variety of sizes and shapes and says the brushes seem well made.
- Heritage Series 4050 is presented as Princeton’s flagship, made with golden taklon fibers, with a large belly, fine point, and good capacity.
- Elite Series 4850 is described as synthetic Kolinsky sable, with a fine point, strong water and pigment retention, and a much lower price than natural Kolinsky.
- Neptune Series 4750 is a very soft faux squirrel, with great water and pigment retention, but little spring and little precision for detail.
- Velvetouch Series 3950 is a luxury synthetic with a velvety handle, very fine point, and good spring.
- Summit Series 6850 uses white synthetic hair, was developed for fluid acrylics, and also works for watercolor.
- The review praises stickers that peel off cleanly, translucent handles on some flats and angled brushes, and the company website’s appearance.
