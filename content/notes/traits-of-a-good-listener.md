The page argues that listening matters as much as speaking, and that good listening involves more than hearing words or staying quiet. It points to four traits that make a listener better: encouraging someone to keep talking, asking for clarification, avoiding judgment, and disagreeing without sounding hostile.

## Reading notes

- Good listeners encourage people to go into more detail and connect their thoughts on their own.
- Good listeners ask questions that get at the source of frustrations, concerns, and excitement.
- Good listeners help move vague comments toward clearer underlying issues.
- Good listeners do not moralize or make the speaker feel judged.
- Good listeners can disagree without turning the exchange into criticism or disrespect.
- These habits are difficult and take practice, and the author says he is still working on them.
