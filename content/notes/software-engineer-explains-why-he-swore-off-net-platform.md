Ryan Bennett says Microsoft .NET has become too cumbersome and costly for his work. He points to licensing costs, weak attention to user experience, and ASP.NET features that lag behind other platforms.

He argues that free and open web platforms make it easier to develop, deploy, and market test products. He also says the biggest web applications use non-Microsoft technology, and he chooses those tools for his own projects.
