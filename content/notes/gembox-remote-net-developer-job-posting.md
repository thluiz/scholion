GemBox develops fast, easy-to-use .NET and Java components focused on file formats, with well-documented APIs used by thousands of companies worldwide. The company operates fully remotely with a small, mostly Europe-based team that works in an agile, Kanban-style process, favors documentation over chat, and aims for asynchronous communication.

The role is for an experienced C# developer who will primarily develop, upgrade, and fix GemBox APIs so they remain fast and simple to use. The position includes writing documentation, creating API examples, writing unit tests, and performing code reviews. The company offers a salary range of 45,000–63,000 USD per year, remote work from anywhere with flexible hours, coworking expenses and work-related education covered, paid public holidays based on location, 24 days of vacation, and various paid leaves including sick and parental leave. Candidates begin with a screening test, followed by a video interview and potential job offer.
