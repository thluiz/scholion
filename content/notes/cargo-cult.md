The post uses Richard Feynman’s 1974 remarks to describe cargo cult science as a case where the outward form of scientific work is preserved while the essential part is missing. It then extends that idea to divided public debate, where people believe they are on the right path and dismiss others as wrong.

- The text links this image to the division of the world into factions, in which each side believes it is right and sees the others as wrong.
