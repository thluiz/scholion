Obsidian is described as a personal knowledge base and note-taking app built on Markdown and plain text. The post presents it as a file-based tool without lock-in or a database, with notes that can be linked into a network of ideas.

It emphasizes simplicity, portability, and customization through plugins, themes, and scripts. The author says it supports commonplace-book style note-making, helps connect ideas, and stays out of the way so users can keep their notes local and leave the format easily.
