Big tech companies in Silicon Valley are described as having shifted from perk-rich, open workplaces into larger bureaucracies with more layoffs, stricter office rules, and less tolerance for employee dissent. The article frames this as a sharp change from the culture that once made Google, Apple, Meta, and Netflix dream employers.

It links the shift to the 2022–23 round of mass layoffs, the rise of generative AI, and executives’ claims that some jobs are becoming redundant. Workers describe lower trust, weaker benefits, and a stronger sense of fear, while some leave big tech for AI startups or say the industry now needs unions.

- Google’s early culture included strong perks, transparency, and a playful atmosphere that made it feel like a dream workplace.
