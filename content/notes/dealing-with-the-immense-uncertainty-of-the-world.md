Babauta argues that widespread fear and uncertainty can damage sleep, health, decision-making, relationships, productivity, and happiness. He recommends not avoiding these feelings, but meeting them directly and using them as material for practice.

## Reading notes

- A constant atmosphere of fear and stress can lead to anxiety, worse sleep and health, depression, bad decisions, panic, confusion, damaged relationships, lower productivity, and less happiness.
- The first step is to notice the uncertainty and stress that are already present instead of running on autopilot.
- Give fear and uncertainty space in awareness rather than trying to force them away.
- Treat the situation as a chance to practice mindfulness with fear and uncertainty, since they will keep arising in life.
- Welcome the feelings with unconditional friendliness instead of relating to them as enemies.
- Open awareness to the wider, changing, spacious quality of the present moment.
- Recognize that many other people are experiencing similar fear and uncertainty, and let that become a basis for connection, compassion, and love.
