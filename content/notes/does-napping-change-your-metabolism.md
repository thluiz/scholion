The article answers that an afternoon nap does not significantly change metabolism. Sleep does slow heart rate, breathing, digestion, and metabolism for a while, but the effect does not last after waking, and regular activity and exercise are what really shift metabolism.

It also says naps can help more through stress relief than through metabolism itself. A well-timed nap may lower cortisol and reduce the hunger that can come with exhaustion, and it may be more helpful than skipping rest if the alternative is less activity or worse habits.
