The post says Basecamp uses six-week budgets for most major product work, not as deadlines but as a way to keep scope flexible and quality high. The budget pushes the team to write less software and make trade-offs instead of drifting into late, bloated work.

It also says good constraints hurt when they work. Features and settings that seem important often get cut, but the discipline of shipping on a budget has proved worth the sacrifice. Basecamp has changed cycle lengths at times, but the author presents budgeted shipping as a key reason a small team can release so much for so long.
