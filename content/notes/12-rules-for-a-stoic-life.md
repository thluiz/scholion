The post uses Jordan Peterson’s 12 Rules for Life as a prompt and offers a Stoic version of life rules. It frames the list as an attempt, not a definitive answer, and keeps the emphasis on self-responsibility, kindness, restraint, and practical discipline.

## Reading notes

- Live each day as if it were a gift after having died and been resuscitated.
- Treat every person you meet as an opportunity for kindness.
- Read to become better, not to look knowledgeable.
- Forgive others, but do not ask for forgiveness for yourself.
- Hold as few opinions as possible.
- Use a person like Cato as a standard for difficult choices.
- Keep spending habits disciplined and stay close to poverty.
- Consider the worst-case scenario.
- Keep track of what other people have taught you and thank them often.
- Get up as early as you can every day.
- Take cold showers.
- Do only what is right and say only what is true.
