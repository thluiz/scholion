Task Boxes is a way to organize task lists for people who think visually. It turns a list into a set of boxes, with the most important task in the biggest box and the remaining tasks in smaller boxes by importance.

The method is meant to be easy to make on a note card or Post-It note, and it can be used for daily, weekly, or monthly lists.

## Reading notes

- Task Boxes organizes tasks through a visual hierarchy.
- A simple list may be hard to follow for a visual person.
- The most important task goes in the biggest box.
- Other tasks go in the other boxes in order of importance.
- A Task Box can be made by drawing a few lines on a note card or Post-It note.
- The method can be used for daily, weekly, and monthly lists.
