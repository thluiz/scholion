The article argues that remote work is not effortless. It asks for more focus, more self-control, and clearer habits so people can prove they can work well from home without burning out.

## Reading notes

- Keep your camera on during meetings so people can see each other and connect faces to names.
- Hold daily team check-ins with action items, especially if the team worries about productivity.
- Keep a rigid work schedule, such as a 9:00AM to 5:00PM day, and stop working when the workday ends.
- Set up a dedicated workspace at home with as few distractions as possible, instead of working in front of the TV or around family.
- Take breaks during the day, such as stretching, walking, or getting water, to avoid staying at the computer too long.
- Use breaks and a fixed schedule to keep your sanity and stay within the work hours you set for yourself.
