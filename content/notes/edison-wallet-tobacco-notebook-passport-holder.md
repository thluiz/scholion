The Edison Wallet is a leather wallet inspired by American inventor Thomas Edison, evoking the idea of a compact place to record ideas, notes, and drawings. It is designed to hold a notebook on one side and a passport on the other, with multiple card pockets, a pen slot, and room for receipts or extra cards. The wallet is made from Horween Dublin horse leather, has waxed and burnished edges, and comes with a Field Notes notebook and a Loyal Stricklin stylus pen.
