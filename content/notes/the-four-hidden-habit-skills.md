The post argues that missing a habit is not proof of failure or a lack of discipline. It treats habit formation as a learned skillset and says progress comes from practice, adjustment, and repeated attempts.

## Reading notes

- Committing to start means moving past mental resistance and making a real decision to begin now, even with a small habit change.
- Focusing on starting means doing the habit itself instead of getting stuck in procrastination, distraction, or excuses.
- Noticing rationalizations means seeing the mind’s excuses in action and answering them with counterarguments.
- Constant readjustment means changing the plan when something breaks down, instead of treating the missed habit as total failure.
- The post says these skills can be practiced and mastered over time, and that doing so can unlock many achievements.
