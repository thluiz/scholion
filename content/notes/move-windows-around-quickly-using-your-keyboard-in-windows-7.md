Windows 7 includes keyboard support for moving and arranging application windows. The article says Win+arrow shortcuts can dock windows side by side, maximize and minimize them, and send them to another monitor.

## Reading notes

- Win+Left arrow snaps the active window to the left half of the screen.
- Win+Right arrow snaps the active window to the right half of the screen.
- Win+Up arrow maximizes the active window.
- Win+Down arrow minimizes the window, or restores it if it is already maximized.
- On multiple monitors, Win+Shift+Left arrow moves the window to the monitor on the left.
- On multiple monitors, Win+Shift+Right arrow moves the window to the monitor on the right.
- The article says these shortcuts are useful for comparing documents side by side and for keeping a browser open while writing in another window.
