Leo Babauta argues that the Weekly Review can fit into under an hour, or even 30 minutes, if daily inbox processing and next-action review are already part of the routine. He says the time savings come from clearing the inbox first, reducing distractions, and moving through the review without interruption.

## Reading notes

- Daily inbox processing and daily review of next-action lists make the weekly review shorter, even if the habit has been neglected.
- Before the review, the inbox, including email, should be down to zero.
- A clear desk helps reduce distractions during the review.
- Email, blog readers, games, and other unnecessary distractions should be closed, and others should be told not to interrupt for an hour.
- A printed checklist for the weekly review and for the mind dump should be ready.
- The review should be done in focused mode, without letting distractions break concentration.
- When it is finished, Babauta recommends rewarding yourself.
- He links to a Weekly Review checklist from Davidco or MineZone wiki and to a Mind Sweep triggers list from 43 Folders.
