The post says Microsoft certification exams could be taken from home or another private place during COVID-19, when testing centers were closed. It focuses on practical preparation: making sure the machine, room, and test-taker are ready for a proctored online exam.

## Reading notes

- Exam times are available throughout the day, including late at night, because proctors are placed across time zones.
- Before the exam, run the system test and check the webcam, internet connection, speakers, and microphone.
- Use a wired connection if possible, especially for labs in Azure or O365 portals.
- A larger screen can help, but multiple monitors are not allowed.
- Tell other people in your space that the exam cannot be interrupted.
- Keep the room clear of posters, books within reach, electronic displays, sound-making devices, virtual assistants, smart watches, wireless speakers, notepads, paper, post-its, writing tools, mirrors, and whiteboards.
- Keep pets out of the testing area, including automatic feeders that make noise or play recordings.
- Do not eat, drink, or take breaks during the exam, except for a clear unmarked bottle of water.
- Do not touch your face during the exam.
- Have a phone ready for check-in, then put it out of reach before the exam starts.
- Have a government-issued ID ready, and submit four photos of the exam area.
- During the exam, close all applications except proctorcam, wait for the proctor, and use the chat if you need to contact them.
- A virtual whiteboard may be available in proctorcam and can be used to work out problems.
- The post ends by pointing readers to the testing session protocol and says the online format may continue to be useful after normal conditions return.
