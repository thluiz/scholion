The post argues that three signals should make someone think about leaving a job: respect, learning opportunity, and payment. It treats lack of respect, blocked learning, and pay far below market price as reasons to look for a new role, while allowing for exceptional cases when a company is going through a bad time.

## Reading notes

- Lack of personal respect in a company is a reason to look for a new job.
- A company should also respect the work itself; ignoring standards and good practices is a warning sign.
- If a job does not let someone learn regularly, it is worth considering a move.
- Pay far below market value is another reason to look for an upgrade, unless the company is in a difficult period.
