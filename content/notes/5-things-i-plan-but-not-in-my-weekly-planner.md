The author uses a weekly planner, but not for everything. Budgeting, routine chores, travel, project planning, and meal planning all get handled with other tools because they are easier to manage there.

## Reading notes

- Budgeting is done in Excel, after paper methods felt too limited, too cluttered, or too time-consuming.
- Day-to-day routine tasks are handled with phone reminders, and repeated habits are not added to the planner.
- Travel planning is spread across Excel tabs, with a master to-do list used to pull tasks into the weekly planner.
- Project planning starts on scrap paper, then moves into whichever planner layout fits the project, including printables, Excel, Kanban Flow, and other systems.
- Meal planning is done with dry erase tools such as laminated printables or a small weekly whiteboard.
