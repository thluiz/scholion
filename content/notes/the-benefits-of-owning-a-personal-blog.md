The post argues that a personal blog helps developers share solutions, build a reputation, learn new technologies, and introduce themselves to a community. It also says a blog can help create opportunities to grow a network and keep learning by writing.

## Reading notes

- A personal blog lets developers turn bug fixes and solutions into articles that help other developers.
- Blogging can strengthen a developer’s brand and reputation in the community.
- Writing about new technologies and concepts can help the writer learn them more deeply.
- Consistent helpful content can make someone known as a teacher in a field and help them connect with others.

- It suggests using a blog on your own domain, or platforms like Medium, Dev.to, or Hashnode.
