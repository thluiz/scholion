Toptal presents its remote-work playbook as a practical guide for organizations adapting to distributed work. It frames remote success around the People function and describes a system built to recruit globally, onboard new hires digitally, support learning and management, and keep structures and goals visible across the company.

## Reading notes

- Toptal says the pandemic accelerated remote work and made distributed operations a practical reality for companies of all sizes.
- The playbook shares lessons from Toptal’s fully distributed workforce in technology, productivity, culture, security, tools, and common problems.
- The People Framework covers recruitment, onboarding, learning and development, performance management, career development, and organizational structure.
- Remote recruiting expands the talent pool globally, but still requires language requirements, time zone constraints, and strategic job posting.
- Job descriptions should explain the role, expectations, team context, and who the candidate will work with.
- Remote interviews use Zoom, move faster than in-person processes, and include work assessments plus interviewer training.
- Cultural fit receives special attention, with a final interview round focused on Toptal’s values and working style.
- Onboarding uses grouped start dates, Zoom introductions, a peer mentor called a TopPal, Slack, and digital training tools.
- Learning and development rely on Udemy and Lessonly, with customized training paths for specific skills and for managers.
- Performance management depends on written goals, self-assessments, peer and manager reviews, frequent feedback, and action items stored in Top Team.
- Career development includes mentorship and public career maps that show role outlines and promotion paths.
- Top Team centralizes organizational structure, member profiles, OKRs, and geographic maps to support ownership, accountability, and coordination.
