**00:24:19** — Black Box Thinking: airlines vs healthcare loops

Brooks brings in Matthew Syed's Black Box Thinking to scale the feedback-loop argument from personal habit to whole industries. The book contrasts aviation, where every incident is investigated and folded back into procedures, with healthcare, where mistakes too often die in silence. The result is one industry that is statistically extraordinarily safe and another that struggles to learn at the same rate. The takeaway: feedback-loop tightness isn't just productivity advice — it's the same mechanism that separates safe from dangerous systems at the institutional level.
