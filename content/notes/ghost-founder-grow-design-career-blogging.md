Blogging is presented as the path that helped the author build a design career. He says a personal blog brought traffic, contacts, and credibility, while guest posts expanded his reach and brought both income and client inquiries.

- Work for clients created material for more posts, which led to more inquiries from companies like Nokia, Microsoft, and EasyJet.
