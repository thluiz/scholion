- Digital solution architects usually draft high-level architecture diagrams to support technology solution design.
- One benefit is better understanding, since the diagram gives an overview of available systems and interactions and helps assess the impact of changes.
- Another benefit is improved communication and collaboration, because it aligns implementation plans across projects and stakeholders and reduces communication gaps.
