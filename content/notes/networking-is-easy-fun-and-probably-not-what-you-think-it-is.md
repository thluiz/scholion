Networking, according to the page, is not mainly about meet-ups, pizza, or LinkedIn adds. It is something that often happens through ordinary life, and it becomes useful when a person is curious, kind, generous, and open to chances that appear along the way.

The author illustrates this with two stories that led to a dream job, then adds a privilege note and a final point for introverts: social contact can be tiring, but it is a skill that can be trained.
