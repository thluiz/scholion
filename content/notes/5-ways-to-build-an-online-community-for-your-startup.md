The text says that building an online community can create demand for the product and generate long-term results, unlike marketing campaigns. For startups, the path involves choosing the right platform, talking to the audience, offering ways to socialize, and tracking the data.

## Reading notes

- Select the most suitable platform to reach customers, taking part in the discussions that are already happening about the product, such as on Facebook, and keeping the page updated.
- Answer questions, add material that supports the effectiveness of the products and services, and, when there is a result on one platform, gradually expand to others.
- Engage the audience from the beginning, listening to customer needs, responding to comments, and acting in forums and discussion groups.
- Offer ways for users to connect with each other, such as a newsletter, social media icons, shareable links, and a presence on social bookmarking sites.
- Prioritize quality in everything, from the product to the service, track market changes, and cultivate customers who defend the products.
- Quantify the results to understand the direction of the business, assess the return on investment, and observe how a community built over time attracts new visitors.
