This template creates monthly calendars for printing directly to 3 x 5 and 4 x 6 index cards. It is an Adobe PDF document, and Adobe Acrobat Reader is required to view and print it.

The page offers settings for the start day, language, month, paper size, bottom lines font, event styles, padding between lines, previous month events, next month events, and whether to use black and white colors only. It also lets the user include holidays, include a schedule, and remember selections.
