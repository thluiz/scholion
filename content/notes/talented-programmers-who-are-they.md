The post defines programmer talent as an innate need to structure things. A talented programmer reacts strongly to ambiguity, inconsistency, chaos, irrationality, and lack of logic, while a mediocre one can keep working with messy code and shrug it off.

It argues that common interview advice looks for people who can work well in existing teams and handle bad code, not for talent. The author says his own Java interview test looks for intolerance to chaos, and that most projects and managers do not actually need those qualities.
