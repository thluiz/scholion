Babauta says he felt exhausted after many video calls, then found that leaning back and allowing space made him feel more at ease. He applies that shift to daily tasks by releasing tension, giving each thing full attention, and keeping a restful attitude.

## Reading notes

- Video calls initially left him exhausted.

- Leaning back feels more at ease for him.
- He describes the core practice as replacing grasping and tension with relaxation.

- He says most people hold tension much of the time.
- A more easeful attitude can make the day feel more restful and leave him feeling refreshed.
- For long calls, messages, and tasks, he recommends one thing at a time with full attention and deliberate care.
