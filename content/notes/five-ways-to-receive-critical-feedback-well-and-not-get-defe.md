The article argues that a leader’s response to negative feedback sets the tone for the whole company. If a leader dismisses criticism or reacts defensively, people are less likely to speak honestly again. The page presents five ways to respond better and make honest feedback feel welcome.

## Reading notes

- Make empathy the main goal when receiving feedback, so the first task is to understand what the other person is saying.
- Pause for 3–4 seconds after hearing criticism to avoid an immediate reactive reply.
- Bring a notebook to a one-on-one meeting and write down what is being said as a sign of attention.
- Assume positive intent so defensiveness does not grow from the idea that the other person is trying to “get” you.
- Talk less, and if needed, say you will think about the feedback and reply later.
- The article says choosing even one of these tactics is a positive step toward an open and honest company culture.
