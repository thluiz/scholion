Anchor announces a feature that converts video files from virtual hangouts into podcast-ready audio, helping people stay connected while physically apart. Users can upload .mp4 or .mov recordings from tools like Zoom, Google Meet, Skype, FaceTime, Instagram Live, and Twitch, then edit and publish them as podcast episodes with Anchor’s built-in tools and distribution.

The article explains that once a video file is uploaded on the web, Anchor automatically generates an audio segment that appears in the episode builder and library for trimming, splitting, and adding enhancements like background music, interludes, sound effects, and Voice Messages. Anchor provides free, unlimited hosting and distributes published shows to platforms including Spotify and Apple Podcasts. The text also notes that YouTube links can’t be uploaded directly, but owned raw video files can be used. It closes with concrete examples of everyday video calls that could become podcasts, from family conversations and virtual watch parties to work meetings and themed hangouts.
