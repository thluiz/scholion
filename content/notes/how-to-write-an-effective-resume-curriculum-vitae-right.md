The post treats a resume as the first document a potential employer sees and says it should present relevant experience and education clearly. It also says hiring is not only about technical skill, because attitude, personal zeal, and eagerness matter, and that people can be chosen for their willingness to learn and train well.

## Fichamento

- A resume is a summary of relevant job experience and education used to secure a new job.
- It is often the first thing a potential employer sees, so it carries a lot of importance.
- An effective resume should show the simplest information about yourself and the technical skillsets that pin-point what you are best capable of executing.
- It should be precise, to the point, and no-nonsense.
- The writer prefers resumes to stay under 3 pages, with 5 pages as the maximum.
- Spelling mistakes and grammatical errors should be avoided.
- The resume should not describe what you want to do for the organization in vague company-growth language.
- It should not list every technical term you have heard of, but focus on your core competency and the area you want to pursue.
- It should not try to present you as both a good developer and a designer at the same time, because that creates confusion about what you want to do.
