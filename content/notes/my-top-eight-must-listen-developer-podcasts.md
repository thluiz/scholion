Ben Halpern says he listens to podcasts to keep up with trends and learn about things he did not know he did not know. He lists eight shows he hears regularly and says the order reflects his preferences and bias. He also notes that the list is not exhaustive and points to Dev.to’s discovery tool, /pod, for more shows.

## Reading notes

- Software Engineering Daily offers five interviews a week, with strong research and broad coverage of software development, especially lower-level stack topics.
- The Changelog features weekly interviews with open source figures and is described as consistently valuable.
- Code Newbie focuses on helping new programmers make the leap, while remaining useful for experienced readers too.
- Shop Talk Show covers front-end development with varied formats, practical advice, and opinions.
- Ruby Rogues is a panel show on Ruby topics and the community side of development.
- JavaScript Jabber has a similar panel format but centers on JavaScript and the different personalities of its hosts and guests.
- The Bike Shed focuses on Ruby, Rails, and ongoing Rails development, with tech discussion and banter.
- Hanselminutes brings in strong guests and explores niche programming topics, with a substantial backlog.
