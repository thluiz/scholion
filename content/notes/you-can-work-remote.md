The post says remote work can be effective, but it requires discipline, focus, time management, and resilience to distractions. It treats communication as the central skill, especially in distributed teams that need early sharing, repeated updates, and clear next steps.

It also recommends keeping a predictable digital presence, sharing calendars, staying reachable, and using tools like team chat, hangouts, and a work-in-progress board. It values a home office, occasional time away from home, time to meet teammates in person, and a morning routine that helps keep the workday consistent.

## Reading notes

- Remote work depends on self-discipline, focus, time management, and resistance to distraction.
- Communication is the core skill in distributed teams, with early sharing and repeated updates reducing misunderstandings.
- A predictable digital presence helps others know when to reach you.
- Shared calendars, mobile chat, team hangouts, and a work-in-progress board support remote coordination.
- A dedicated home office helps create a place for focused work.
- Leaving the house sometimes and meeting teammates in person can support collaboration and longer-term thinking.
- A morning routine, including gym, breakfast, and dressing for the day, helps maintain consistency.
- The post says remote work still fails when someone is dishonest, disrespectful, lazy, or unwilling to communicate daily.
