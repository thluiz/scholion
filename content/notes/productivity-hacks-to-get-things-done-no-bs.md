The post collects straightforward productivity tips for dealing with procrastination and getting through work more efficiently. It emphasizes batching routine tasks, keeping a workspace clean and comfortable, doing very short tasks right away, and prioritizing important work over urgent work.

It also recommends breaking large tasks into smaller pieces, using music to help focus, identifying the times of day when you are most productive, templating repetitive work, applying the 80/20 rule, and taking care of health.

## Reading notes

- Batch similar mundane tasks together to work more efficiently, such as calls, emails, and bills.
- Keep your workspace clean and organized, and change anything that makes it unpleasant to use.
- If a task takes two minutes or less, do it immediately.
- Prioritize important tasks before urgent ones, because urgent tasks keep coming up.
- Break large or complex tasks into smaller chunks to create small wins during the day.
- Use music, such as Chopin or lofi, to help focus your energy while working.
- Figure out when you are most productive during the day and protect that time from meetings or interruptions.
- Use templates for repetitive parts of life and work to reduce decision fatigue.
- Apply the 80/20 rule by focusing on the small share of work that produces most results.
- Eat healthy, live healthy, and drink well.
