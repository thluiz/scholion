IT leaders in the article say career progress in technology depends on more than programming languages. They emphasize keeping up with change, understanding users and business needs, and finding purpose in the work.

## Reading notes

- Diarmuid Gill says IT professionals should never stop learning because technologies change quickly and their shelf life is getting shorter.
- Gill also says people on the management path still need to stay current with new tech so they do not fall out of step.
- He says ambitious IT professionals should learn clients’ needs and talk directly with users or internal contacts such as sales or business development.
- Malcolm Lowe says IT professionals should connect their work to business outcomes and user needs.
- Lowe recommends speaking with the people who will use the service and doing research instead of working in isolation.
- He says modern tools make it easier to create something quickly, get instant feedback, and work iteratively.
- Lowe advises starting small, identifying the minimum viable product, and adding new requirements step by step.
- Ian Cohen says modern IT professionals have broader opportunities than before and should grab their chances.
- Cohen says technology is now fundamental to society and that the most important thing is to have a passion and a purpose.
