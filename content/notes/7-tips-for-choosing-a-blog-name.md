The post argues that a blog name should be chosen with long-term use in mind, because it becomes part of a blog’s identity and first impression. It recommends thinking through your topics, checking domain availability early, and choosing a name you will still want later.

## Reading notes

- Think carefully about what you want to write about, and choose a name that fits the topics you expect to cover.
- If you may write about several different subjects, pick a broader name that leaves room for the brand to grow.
- Choose a unique name, since a distinctive blog name does not immediately limit future posts.
- Check whether the domain is available before settling on a name, especially if you want to avoid a blogspot-style address.
- Ask trusted friends, family, or blogging groups for ideas or honest reactions when you are stuck.
- Use keyword tools and thesaurus searches to find related words and new combinations.
- Look at competitors’ blog names to see what works and what does not in your niche.
- If the blog centers on you and your interests, using your own name can be a good option.
- The main goal is to choose a name you genuinely like, because changing domains later is difficult.
