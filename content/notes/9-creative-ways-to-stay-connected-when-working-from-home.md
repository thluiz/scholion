The article argues that remote teams can reduce isolation by recreating everyday office interactions in virtual form. It recommends structured social time, more video, shared routines, and small informal moments that help coworkers stay visible to one another.

## Reading notes

- Reimagine commute time as a chance to schedule coffee chats with different coworkers by phone, video, or text.
- Use a shared time management method, such as Pomodoro, with a coworker to make the workday feel more collaborative.
- Make video chats standard, because phone calls and Slack leave out nonverbal cues.
- Try working silently on video together so coworkers can feel more like they are in the same space.
- Create shared lunches by choosing a simple meal, preparing it separately, and eating it together later.
- Pair team members as visibility buddies so they can call attention to each other’s accomplishments in Slack.
- Treat pets and children as part of remote work life and use those moments to know coworkers better, while respecting boundaries.
- Make meetings more engaging with exercises like Six Thinking Hats, “Yes and,” or “First and Worst.”
- Keep casual contact alive by taking coworkers along for ordinary moments and using a Slack channel for informal chatter.
