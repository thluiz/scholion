The post says the software engineering job market is in a bad place, but experienced engineers with a maverick attitude can still create extraordinary leverage. The main example is using automation to turn formulaic work into productive output, even when some steps used to require human input.

It applies this to marketing a startup through daily sprite packs on itch.io. The author describes precomputing themes, using a startup service to generate the packs, customizing each page, and automating much of the process with ChatGPT. They also automated the creation of showcase videos with thematic music and narration, and say tools like Cursor with o3 MAX can turn a well-defined problem into an automation script in minutes.
