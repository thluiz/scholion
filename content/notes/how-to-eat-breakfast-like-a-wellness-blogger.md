The article says wellness bloggers make breakfast work by planning ahead, even when mornings are rushed. With no time, they prep chia pudding, overnight oats, or homemade raw bars. With a few minutes, they use smoothie pods or preloaded blender jars. With more time, they make toast, avocado toast, baked eggs, frittatas, omelets, or full brunch spreads. If nothing else works, they fall back on Starbucks orders like espresso over ice, fruit salad, oatmeal, or a protein pack.

## Reading notes

- Perfect-looking breakfasts are usually partly prepared in advance.
- Chia pudding can be mixed at night with coconut milk, spices, almond butter, and berries, then left in the fridge.
- Overnight oats can be assembled in five minutes and taken to work.
- Homemade raw bars can be made in a batch from dates, nuts, and dried fruit.
- Mason jars make breakfast more attractive for photos and are easy to carry.
- Smoothies can be sped up by pre-packaging ingredients in zipper bags or loading the blender jar the night before.
- Purple smoothie colors can be kept vivid by combining foods with similar shades.
- Quick smoothie bowls can be topped with fruit and nuts arranged in lines, circles, or patterns.
- For toast, the suggested formula is 1/4 protein, 1/4 grain, and 1/2 produce.
- Avocado toast can be finished with cayenne pepper and sometimes a fried egg.
- A more elaborate breakfast can be built around baked eggs, frittatas, or omelets, with side dishes prepared ahead of time.
- Vegan brunch can include dairy-free pancakes or donuts, roasted vegetables or stir-fry, and fresh fruit salad.
- At Starbucks, the emergency order is espresso over ice with dairy-free milk, plus fruit salad or oatmeal with cinnamon.
- The Protein Pack is mentioned as a common backup choice.
- The article also gives Instagram advice about lighting, table settings, props, and hashtags.
