The author says he was skeptical of AI development tools because the demos felt overhyped and the “AI will replace developers” framing seemed unrealistic. A conversation with Adam Wathan and Brian Casel changed that view.

He highlights that much of programming is grunt work, which AI handles well. He also says AI matters more in the hands of experienced developers, because it is easy to trust it when it is confidently wrong if you do not already know the work. The post also notes business risks for Tailwind Plus if people rely on markdown docs instead of visiting the site, and it closes with open questions about the effects on junior developers and how teams will adapt.
