A commonplace book is a notebook or other resource for recording ideas, facts, quotes, and personal responses from reading, study, and daily life. The post argues that keeping one preserves your thoughts and learning for later use, whether that is next week or years from now.

## Reading notes

- It gives you a written record of thoughts and learning that you can use later.
- Writing something down makes it more likely that you will remember it, even if you never look back at the entry.
- Taking notes helps you focus on one idea at a time and can make other people’s ideas feel more like your own.
- Writing while reading makes you less likely to fall asleep.
- It shows children that learning and education continue in adult life.
- The physical act of writing is described as good cognitive exercise for keeping the brain sharp.
- A commonplace book may also become useful to children or other descendants later on.
