The post invites people to join March of Robots, a sketch challenge on Instagram created by Dacosta, also known as @chocolatesoop. Its goal is to celebrate robots and make drawing more fun. The challenge emphasizes making a personal commitment, even if that means drawing only one robot a week, and posting the results with the suggested hashtags.

## Reading notes

- Autodesk SketchBook says it has taken part in drawing challenges before, like Inktober, because repeated challenges help fight complacency and procrastination.
- March of Robots is presented as a sketch challenge hosted on Instagram and created by Dacosta, a self-taught artist in Vancouver.
- The challenge values getting drawings done more than drawing every day.
- Participants are asked to post their drawings with #marchofrobots and #marchofrobots2017.
- Drawings made with the app can also be tagged with #autodesksketchbook.
- Dacosta is giving away four Wacom Intuos Art Creative Pen and Touch Tablets at the end of March.
- To be considered for the drawing, a person needs to post at least four new drawings during the month.
