## Reading notes

- Leadership is treated as an intangible but real form of creation, like art, because it involves building high-performing teams.

- The article says leadership principles are learned through practice, mentorship, theory, and experience.

- The third principle, "Know Your People and Look Out for Their Welfare," focuses on understanding team members’ backgrounds, strengths, weaknesses, personal situations, and well-being.

- Howard Schultz is used as an example of employee-focused leadership through health benefits, stock options, and support during crises.
