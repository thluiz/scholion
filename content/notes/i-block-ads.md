The author says he grew up liking advertisements and now blocks them because the modern web is overcrowded with them. After macOS Sequoia broke AdGuard in Safari because of a conflict with iCloud Private Relay, he noticed how unusable browsing becomes without an ad blocker.

He says he blocks ads both in the browser and at the DNS level, and that this combination works well enough for now. He also recommends some tools for ad blocking and mentions that the FBI has suggested using ad blockers to avoid scams and impersonation through search ads.

- He works around the problem by turning off Private Relay in Safari and also at the system level, while waiting for a fix.
