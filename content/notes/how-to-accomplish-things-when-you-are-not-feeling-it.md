When motivation is low, the article recommends making work easier to begin rather than waiting to feel ready. It favors a weekly paper calendar, one doable item per day, and small wins that can lead into larger tasks.

## Reading notes

- Use a weekly paper calendar to move tasks out of your head and onto paper, with at least one item each day that you can actually finish.
- Expect that inspiration will return at some point, and organize the week with that in mind.
- Start with the least demanding task on the list, because checking off one item can help you move to the next.
- If a project feels too large, break it into smaller steps such as gathering color palettes, reviewing data, or looking for design inspiration.
- Do not try to finish a project immediately; begin by putting something on the page and continue later.
- If work still feels blocked, do a household task like folding laundry and return to the desk afterward.
