The page lays out eight predictions for 2026 in AI. It says generative UI will speed up, personal agents will move to the edge, smart homes will understand context, and AI labs will rely on complex harnesses to prove agent performance.

It also says engineering will shift toward review and evaluation, social media will become more human-centered, AI-generated content will be treated as the default unless signed as human, and biometric proof of personhood will become a social login for platforms.

## Reading notes

- Generative UI is expected to grow as lower latency and better performance let code-generation applications create interfaces on the fly for specific tasks and preferences.
- Personal agents are expected to move to edge-first devices, powered by small language models running locally on specialized hardware.
- Smart home assistants are expected to understand context and intention and to integrate naturally with personal assistants like Gemini App or ChatGPT.
- AI labs are expected to use complex harnesses instead of saturated benchmarks to show that agents can reliably complete multi-day workstreams.
- Engineering is expected to shift further toward reviewing, evaluating, conceptualizing, and aligning technical work with business goals.
- Social media is expected to slow down as AI-generated content creates a premium market for human-made posts.
- Posts and images are expected to be treated as AI by default unless they carry cryptographic human-signature metadata such as C2PA.
- Social platforms are expected to split into a verified-human web that requires biometric verification to reduce agent spam.
