Mike Vardy keeps a simple weekday routine built around waking up, washing his face, dressing, making coffee and a smoothie, then spending about 30 minutes on reading before checking the calendar and starting his three main tasks. He avoids making the routine more complicated so he can keep doing it.

## Reading notes

- He wakes around 8:00am on weekdays, and at 7:00am on Fridays because of his wife’s work schedule.
- He starts with water on his face, then gets dressed and makes AeroPress coffee and a smoothie.
- His reading workflow uses Reeder, Flipboard, and an Instapaper queue, and he does it in his office chair with the door open.
- He checks the calendar after reading, then either heads upstairs to say goodbye to his wife and kids or, on Saturdays, spends time with the family.
- On workdays, he follows “My 3 Absolutes,” written on paper the night before and placed face down on his MacBook Pro.
- He has used this routine for about three years, with minor tweaks such as Launch Center Pro prompts for reading.
- He has added a vitamin D pill during grey months in Victoria and made the NutriBullet part of the routine.
- He goes to bed around 1:00am and wants at least four full sleep cycles.
- His evening routine includes writing down the next day’s three absolutes, preparing NutriBullet shakes, journaling in Day One, and meditating with Muse or Calm.
- He uses Sleep Cycle as an alarm and says he never hits snooze.
- His usual breakfast is the smoothie, sometimes with steel cut oatmeal on weekends.
- He works out around midday, not in the morning, so he has energy later in the day.
- He does not meditate in the morning and sees morning reading as a mild form of reflection and preparation.
- He does not answer email first thing; email comes later, often after at least one absolute is done.
- He uses Productive to keep the routine active and an alarm to start his evening routine.
- He usually checks his phone before 11:00am, but not first thing.
- He does administrative tasks early, writes online from 9:00 to 10:30am on weekdays, and handles email later in the morning.
- His first drink is decaffeinated coffee, and he saves caffeine for CogniTea around 1:00pm.
- He designed the routine to fit his wife’s schedule and says they rarely disrupt each other’s routines.
- He may sleep in a little on weekends, keeps Saturday as Family Day, and uses Sunday as Planning Day.
- He says the routine can travel with him because it stays simple.
- If he misses part of the routine, he feels off and less in sync, and he sees routines as the framework for effectiveness and productivity.
