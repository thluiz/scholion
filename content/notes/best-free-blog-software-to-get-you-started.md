The page argues that blogging software should be chosen with future growth in mind. It says ease of use matters, but so do flexibility, room to expand, search visibility, and the ability to develop a blog beyond the first setup.

## Reading notes

- Choose a platform that is easy to work with, flexible, and lets you create content the way you want.
- Think about the long-term plan, because an established audience and URL can make switching later difficult.
- WordPress offers broad growth potential, many themes, free plugins, SEO support, but it can be harder for beginners and requires separate hosting and security setup.
- Postach.io is simple to set up, works especially well with Evernote, includes hosting, offers customizable themes, and has a free plan with the Postach.io domain in the URL.
- Blogger is easy to set up, fully free, connected to Google features, and allows up to 100 blogs per account, but it has basic themes, limited features, and few updates.
- Medium is easy to join and focuses on writing inside an existing community, but it offers little design control, makes audience portability harder, and limits monetization.
- Wix is free, uses drag-and-drop editing, and offers many templates, but free plans are limited, include Wix branding and ads, and restrict e-commerce features.
- The page closes by advising readers to look beyond fast free setups and check what each platform can support as a blog grows.
