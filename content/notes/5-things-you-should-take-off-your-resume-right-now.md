The article argues that resumes should reflect current hiring expectations and avoid sections that add little value. It recommends removing outdated items, keeping formatting simple, and focusing on relevant skills and accomplishments.

## Reading notes

- Leave off a career objective and use a summary of qualifications instead.
- Skip unrelated awards, hobbies, and interests unless they are relevant to the role.
- Avoid too much formatting, since applicant tracking systems and recruiters may have trouble reading it.
- Do not rely on task lists alone; show accomplishments and results instead.
- Omit basic skills like Windows, Microsoft Office, and email, and highlight more specialized abilities.
- Update the resume regularly so it stays current and reflects resume trends.
