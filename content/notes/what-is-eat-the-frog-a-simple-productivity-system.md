Eat the Frog is a simple productivity method built around doing the most important task first, especially the one you are most likely to postpone. The article says this helps people beat procrastination, focus on high-impact work, and start the day with momentum.

It also argues that the method works because it supports deep work, keeps you from reacting to email and messages first, uses your best hours for demanding tasks, and stays easy to follow even when other systems feel too complex.

## Reading notes

- Identify the day’s Most Important Task, especially the one that feels difficult or easy to delay.
- Keep the frog manageable, ideally something you can finish in 1 to 4 hours or in half a day.
- If the task is too large, break it into smaller concrete steps and make the first step the frog.
- Do not plan frogs too far ahead, because future scheduling is hard to predict accurately.
- Prepare the frog the night before so the next morning starts with clear focus.
- Do the frog first thing, before email, meetings, browsing, or other distractions.
- Apply the method at the time of day when you feel most energized and focused.
- In Todoist, the setup uses a frog label, a Today’s Frog filter, and a start view that surfaces the frog first.
