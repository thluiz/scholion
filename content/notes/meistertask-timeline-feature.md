MeisterTask presents Timeline as a project overview for users who wanted a Gantt-like view. It shows tasks together in one calendar view, lets users change dates directly, and helps teams coordinate work across a project.

## Reading notes

- Timeline is compared to a Gantt chart and displays start and end dates, assignee, and workflow location through color coding.
- The view is presented as useful for project overview when Kanban boards are not enough for a broader project perspective.
- It is also framed as a tool for resource management because it can be filtered by assignee, tag, watcher, due date, and status.
- The article says Timeline helps identify linked tasks, deadlines that cannot be missed, and time frames that may need adjustment to prevent bottlenecks.
- It also supports transparency and communication by showing teammates’ planned work without opening each task individually.
- Users can add tasks by dragging them in, scheduling dates, double-clicking the Timeline, or creating new tasks there.
- Task end dates are described as independent from due dates, and users can assign tasks, sort the view, filter it, go fullscreen, and zoom in or out.
