Torvalds says AI is interesting and may change the world, but the hype cycle around it makes him want to ignore it for now. He says the industry is in a bad position because the marketing outweighs the reality, and he expects clearer evidence in five years, when real workloads will show what GenAI is actually used for.

## Reading notes

- He describes the AI sector as 90 percent marketing and 10 percent reality.
- He says ChatGPT makes good demonstrations and is used in areas like graphic design.
- He links his view to a general dislike of the hype cycle, which he says also surrounded crypto.
- The article adds that large sums have gone into AI start-ups and hyperscaler spending, while revenue from AI services remains much lower.
- It also notes that Google and Microsoft are still trying to prove GenAI productivity benefits, and that return on investment is uncertain.
