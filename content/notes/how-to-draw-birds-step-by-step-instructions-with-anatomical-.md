- Waterfowl have flattened bodies, filtering beaks, wings for takeoff from water, and webbed feet.
- Owls have rounded bodies, flattened faces, large forward eyes, silent wings, and zygodactyl feet.
- Parrots have big round heads, hooked beaks, long wings, and zygodactyl feet.
- Ostrich-like birds do not fly, have long legs and necks, reduced wings, and unusual foot arrangements.
