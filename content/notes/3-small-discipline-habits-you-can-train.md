The post says discipline improves through training, not constant self-control. It recommends repeating small, effective habits and using the feeling of learning something new as motivation.

## Reading notes

- The main advice is to practice discipline as a skill, not to expect perfection without repeated, deliberate practice.
- One habit is to tell yourself what you are going to do next, so you focus on one immediate task instead of a long to-do list.
- Another habit is to ask what the smallest step is, then make the task easy enough to start, even if that means only a few minutes.
- A third habit is to ask what is stopping you from focusing on that step, then remove distractions or get help and accountability.
- The author describes this as a training session: repeat the process two more times, take short breaks, then rest for 20 minutes as a reward.
- Doing several training sessions a day is presented as a way to get good at discipline quickly, until the cost of doing anything feels very small.
