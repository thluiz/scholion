The post groups common non-technical frontend interview questions into product, Agile, and soft-skills topics. It suggests answering with concrete experiences, thinking ahead about follow-up questions, and showing how you work with teammates and learn new things.

## Reading notes

- Product questions focus on the relationship with the Product team, favorite apps, and how you would change an app to increase engagement or improve user experience.
- Agile questions ask whether you have worked in Agile, what kind of framework the team used, what you like or dislike about the workflow, and how many meetings per week you prefer.
- Soft-skill questions cover disagreements with coworkers, miscommunication, missed tickets, learning style, and how you keep up with changing technology.
- The author suggests talking about positive experiences with Product, choosing an app you know well, and preparing practical answers about engagement, UX, and team workflow.
- For learning and technology updates, the author mentions reading, doing, sharing with others, Twitter, and podcasts such as Ladybug, Front End Happy Hour, and Syntax.
