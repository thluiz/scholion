James Gurney presents a behind-the-scenes build for a reusable gizmo that makes the last 20 seconds of a YouTube video more interesting and encourages viewers to click end-screen links. He says the device is cheap, easy to build, and customizable to the style of a channel.

## Reading notes

- The end screen can offer links to other videos, playlists, websites, or the Subscribe button in the last 20 seconds of a YouTube video.
- The video shows how to make a reusable gizmo for that end screen segment.
- The panels flip into position before the link options appear.
- Mousetrap springs power the motion.
- The build uses mousetraps, 1 x 3 pine boards, screw eyes, Magic Sculpt epoxy clay, and Gorilla glue.
