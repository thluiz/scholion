The piece recommends questions that reveal values, recent changes, and small turning points, in place of first-date small talk.

## Fichamento

- Ask about a cheap purchase that mattered. It reveals taste more than budget does.
- Beliefs someone dropped in the last few years say more about them than beliefs they've always held.
- Small habits are framed as more effective than resolutions for improving a life.
- The "look back at 80" question forces perspective while keeping the answer grounded in one concrete memory.
- Embarrassing opinions and personal rules work as questions because they invite specificity.
- Failure gets reframed as useful, a source of learning.
