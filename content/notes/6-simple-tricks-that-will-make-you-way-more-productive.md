The article says productivity depends on using limited time more efficiently, not on working longer. It presents six habits that help you act on tasks faster, protect important work, and reduce interruptions.

## Reading notes

- Never leave tasks in a holding pattern; act on them, delegate them, or delete them when they first get your attention.
- Do the least pleasant item on your to-do list first so it does not hang over the rest of the day.
- Delete or delegate urgent small tasks when they get in the way of work that matters more.
- Say no to new commitments so you can honor existing ones and avoid stress, burnout, and depression.
- Check email on a schedule instead of treating it as a constant interruption, and use alerts only for important senders.
- Avoid multitasking because research cited in the article says it reduces attention, recall, and switching between jobs.
