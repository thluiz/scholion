The essay says it is gratifying to see companies built with Rails reach billion-dollar valuations, but dangerous to turn that into resentment about personal wealth. That grievance mindset, the author argues, poisons the open source spirit and makes others’ success feel like a loss.

He frames the real reward of open source as the work it enables for programmers and the economic activity it creates around Rails. He says he has enough, and that open source means allowing others to build freely while capturing far less than one creates.

## Reading notes

- Rails has helped create large value at companies like Shopify, GitHub, Gusto, Zendesk, Instacart, Procore, Doximity, and Coinbase.
- Looking at that success as a personal missed opportunity leads to resentment.
- Grievance about what creators “should” have captured is described as lethal to open source.
- The author says the real return from open source comes from happy programmers using Ruby to build successful web businesses.
- More success for Rails-based businesses means more work and more Ruby programmers.
- He describes “enough” as a form of wealth.
- Open source spirit means letting many users benefit without trying to extract too much back from them.
