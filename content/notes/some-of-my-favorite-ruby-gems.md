- Devise is presented as a flexible and easy authentication solution the author uses in every project.

- FriendlyID replaces numeric IDs in URLs with human-friendly strings for readability and SEO.
- MetaTags lets a Rails app insert default, dynamic, and social meta information with little effort.
- Impressionist tracks impressions or page views and is useful for tutorial or art-focused sites.
- Draftsman supports draft states and includes scopes and instance methods for publishing content.

- Unread manages unread records, mark-as-read actions, unread scopes, and uses one extra database table.
- Homebrew and Homebrew-cask are described as the author’s top pick for installing software.
- Pagy is favored for pagination because of its performance and ease of use.
- Sidekiq is recommended for background processing and sending large numbers of emails.
