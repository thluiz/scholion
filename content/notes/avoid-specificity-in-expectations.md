Our feelings come from the difference between expectations and reality, not from reality alone. When expectations are too specific, they narrow what we can see and make disappointment more likely.

In career planning, the post says that asking where you want to be in five years can hurt because it ties the future self to the present self’s imagined desires. Future interests, friends, ideas, and situations should be allowed to shape what comes next. Near-term expectations can help action, but they work better when they stay less specific.
