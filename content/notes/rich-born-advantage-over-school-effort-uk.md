- John Goldthorpe says younger generations face worse mobility prospects than their parents or grandparents, even with higher qualifications.

- Top university places remain highly selective and are often reserved for rich private school students.
- The expansion of tertiary education has produced an overqualified pool for graduate jobs without reducing inequality.
