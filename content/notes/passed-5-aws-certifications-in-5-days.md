## Reading notes

- Stephane Maarek’s Udemy courses are presented as the best option, and the author says the practice exams are needed, especially for professional exams.
- Passing an exam gives a 50% discount on the next one, and AWS Community Builders get one free exam per year.
- Nonnative English speakers can apply for 30 extra minutes before registering.
- Online exams can be rescheduled or canceled up to 24 hours before the appointment, with a limit of two reschedules after the original booking.
- The author prefers taking the exam in person, but he took his online because of flexible timing.
- Online exams require a quiet room and a stable computer setup, and he reports problems caused by background software and noise.
- Some network errors during the online exam setup are caused by access code problems rather than a real network issue.
- If exam slots are full, repeated logins and retries can reveal newly opened dates and times.
- He advises against spreading study over many months because details fade, especially for professional exams.
- For professional exams, he recommends answering quickly by scanning keywords, reading the question, imagining the answer, and checking only if time remains.
- Long questions should be skipped at first, marked for review, and revisited later if time allows.
- The hardest questions can feel like they come first because of time pressure, so the author advises staying calm.
- He divides the professional exam into chunks of questions and leaves 30 minutes for review, noting that 10 minutes are removed for registration.
- Passing certain professional exams automatically renews related associate certificates if they have not expired.
- Results usually arrive within 24 hours, though the portal may show them within a few hours.
- The closing point is that certifications help you understand AWS and prove knowledge, but hands-on experience is still necessary.
