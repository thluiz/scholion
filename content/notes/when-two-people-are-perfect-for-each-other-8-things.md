The page says that a strong match shows up in everyday habits, not just in big romantic moments. Comfort with silence, mutual support, easy conflict resolution, small acts of care, shared humor, aligned values, vulnerability, and time together that feels worthwhile all point to a relationship that feels secure and sustaining.

## Reading notes

- Silence feels peaceful, not awkward, because the relationship does not depend on constant talk or entertainment.
- Each person supports the other’s dreams and helps with goals instead of competing.
- Disagreements end with understanding, listening, and solutions that work for both people.
- Small gestures such as remembering a coffee order or checking in during the day show care.
- Shared humor and inside jokes keep the connection playful and resilient.
- Core values about family, money, integrity, and the future align naturally.
- Both people can be vulnerable without fear of judgment or having their feelings used against them.
- Time together feels worthwhile, even during ordinary activities, because both people genuinely enjoy each other.
