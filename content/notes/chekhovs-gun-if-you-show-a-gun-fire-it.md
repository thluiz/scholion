The author says he tends to suggest things that are ahead of current circumstances, while his co-founder brings the discussion back to first principles. That tension appears in their product planning for Valinor Earth, where the author wanted to demo tools that were not ready and the co-founder warned that this could raise expectations they could not satisfy.

The post then applies Chekhov’s Gun to product work. If a tool is shown, it should be ready to be used and should solve something the customer actually needs, otherwise it becomes a promise the team does not intend to keep.
