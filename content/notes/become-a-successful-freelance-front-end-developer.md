## Reading notes

- Build strong skills in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, and keep learning related languages and frameworks.
- Create a portfolio from websites, client work, and other examples that can show your abilities to prospects.
- Present yourself as a brand through marketing channels such as LinkedIn, Instagram, YouTube, and freelance platforms like Upwork and Fiverr.
- Keep client work organized by tracking names, projects, deadlines, fees, and other details with tools such as Google Drive, Trello, FreshBooks, Controller, and Hubstaff.
- Join communities on platforms like Facebook, Quora, and LinkedIn to learn from others, follow what is happening in the field, and get feedback.
- When starting freelance work, take time to understand each project, give realistic timelines, and be clear about payment terms and post-sale services.
