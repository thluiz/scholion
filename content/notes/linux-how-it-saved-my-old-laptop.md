The page describes a move away from Windows toward Linux because the author wanted more control, fewer preinstalled ads and bloatware, and less friction when setting up work. After a Windows laptop failed, he found Ubuntu easier to reinstall and keep stable, then later replaced it with EndeavourOS when he wanted a lighter system for older hardware.

## Reading notes

- Windows is described as acceptable for a broad audience, but not ideal for someone who wants to control everything on the computer and remove what he does not want.
- On earlier Windows systems, he regularly reinstalled to remove viruses, fix corrupt files, and regain speed.
- Windows 8 is presented as slower and worse received than Windows 7, while Windows 10 is described as smoother and quicker.
- When his Windows 10 laptop died, the setup process on the new laptop felt too long and got in the way of returning to work.
- He had already used Ubuntu in a virtual machine for work and chose to make it his main system on the new laptop.
- He created a Windows/Ubuntu dual boot, but removed Windows within six months.
- Ubuntu installation took about 20 minutes, and after that he could install what he needed from the command line and get back to work.
- He says Ubuntu stayed fast and stable for a year without needing reinstalling.
- He later tried to revive the old laptop, which had been unused for about two years, by booting from a USB drive.
- The Ubuntu installation restored the laptop to a working desktop environment and fixed the boot-drive connection.
- Because Ubuntu still felt too slow on the older hardware, he tried Fedora and then settled on EndeavourOS.
- He describes EndeavourOS as an Arch-based distribution that installs only the essentials, with a graphical installer, a modified XFCE desktop, a file manager, a browser, and a few other basics.
- He says the lack of bloatware makes it fast and smooth, and that the whole installation took 10 to 15 minutes.
- He concludes that the old laptop now feels simple, fast, and usable again, and that he wrote and edited the post on that machine.
