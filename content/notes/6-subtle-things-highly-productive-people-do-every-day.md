The article argues that being productive is less about working faster and more about shaping the conditions of work. It emphasizes mood, routines, planning, and the environment as the main levers for getting meaningful work done without becoming reactive or overworked.

## Reading notes

- Start the day calm, since mood affects focus, procrastination, and productivity.
- Avoid email in the morning because it pushes you into reacting to other people’s priorities.
- Before trying to make a task faster, ask whether it needs to be done at all.
- Focus comes from reducing distractions and changing the environment.
- Personal routines and systems matter more than relying on self-discipline alone.
- Set the one or two most important tasks the night before and write them down.
