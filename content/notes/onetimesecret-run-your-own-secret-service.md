Onetime Secret is presented as a privacy-first service for sending passwords, credentials, and other sensitive data through links that work only once. The page stresses that each secret is encrypted, stored by region, and removed after a single view, with no recovery or trace left behind.

It also says the service supports team workflows through a developer API, access controls, SSO, audit logs, and compliance goals such as SOC 2, GDPR, CCPA, and HIPAA. The page lists use cases for IT, HR, legal, finance, consultants, and agencies, and says data stays in the region chosen at creation.

## Reading notes

- Sends passwords, credentials, and sensitive text through links that open only once.
- Encrypts each secret with its own key before storage.
- Keeps decryption keys on the application server, not in the database.
- Deletes the secret permanently after a single view, with no copies or recovery.
- Lets users choose regional storage in the EU, US, and other regions.
- Says it supports SOC 2, GDPR, CCPA, and HIPAA workflows.
- Offers a developer API with language-specific SDKs.
- Includes custom branding, SSO/SAML, incoming secrets, and access controls.
- Describes team uses in IT, HR, legal, finance, consulting, and agency work.
- States that data stays in the chosen region with no cross-border replication or silent failover.
