The post argues that doubling revenue and profit is less about secret tactics than about doing what you already know, finding where revenue is leaking away, and dealing with the psychological blocks that keep you from acting. It also says growth requires clearer selling and better systems that tell you what to do next.

## Reading notes

- If you are actually trying to double the business, you can do it without outside help by focusing on what you already know and following through.
- One way to grow is to identify where revenue is being lost and stop ignoring opportunities when they appear.
- The post says the real blocker is often psychological, such as fear, overwhelm, procrastination, or avoidance.
- Examples include not hiring an assistant, not advertising, not upselling, or not fixing a sales page even when you know you should.
- A simple sales approach is to make it clear what you sell, make people aware of it, and give them a reason to buy.
- The post says many businesses only do the awareness part and fail to make the buying case.
- Better systems and processes are presented as essential because they remove guesswork and help you plan the next step after each action.
- The article says systems should map what happens after email signup, after a purchase, after ad clicks, and after a blog post.
