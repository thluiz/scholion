The article argues that reaching senior level requires increasing scope, impact, and ownership. One of the main ways to do that is to become a go-to person in a technical area or product domain, so others seek out your advice and treat you as a leader there.

It says the best expertise area sits at the overlap of three things: what you are good at, what you are passionate about, and what creates team or company impact. If you have all three, you should invest more in that area. If one is missing, the article suggests either building that skill, finding a way to create impact, or choosing a different area. It also says visibility matters, and gives examples like leading projects, answering questions in Slack, writing documentation, solving unowned problems, creating tools, running presentations, leading recurring meetings, and starting guilds or working groups.
