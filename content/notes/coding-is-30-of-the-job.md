- Understanding the business helps developers match solutions to company goals, customer needs, and the level of technical depth a project should have.

- Good communication matters for understanding requirements, working with teammates, giving feedback carefully, and handling criticism well.
- Decision making depends on business and domain knowledge, including when to write code and when not to, and when to choose one solution over another.
