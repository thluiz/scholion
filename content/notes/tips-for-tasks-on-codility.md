The post collects preparation tips for Codility tasks. It says the platform measures programming ability with a few timed problems that must meet running time and space constraints, and it advises practicing the demo, reviewing Big O, sorting, hash tables, and using a familiar language and IDE.

It also recommends reading the prompt carefully, identifying the problem type, drafting an initial solution that fits the constraints, writing the main logic before edge cases, and testing large values and boundary positions. Unit tests, a quiet workspace, and local tools are presented as ways to save time and avoid mistakes.

- Codility is described as a tool for measuring programming capabilities with three selected tasks under time and space constraints.
- The demo is presented as a way to get used to the platform and the time pressure.
- The author recommends refreshing Big O notation, simple sorting algorithms, hash tables, and hash functions.
