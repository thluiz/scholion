Brad Isaac describes Jerry Seinfeld’s advice as a way to pressure himself into writing every day. The method uses a yearly wall calendar, a red marker, and a visible chain of Xs that should not be broken.

The note argues that steady daily action matters more than occasional bursts. Isaac says he has applied the same technique to exercise, programming, network administration, websites, and business, because small daily improvements add up and skipping one day makes it easier to skip the next.
