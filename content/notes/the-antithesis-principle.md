The essay argues that some truths about human nature work in two directions. Outward, they suggest a tactic for influencing other people. Inward, they point to a default you should eliminate in yourself.

## Reading notes

- If content is entertaining, people learn better, but it is also worth becoming someone who can learn without needing entertainment as a prerequisite.
- Charisma helps a leader influence others, but wisdom also requires resisting other people’s charisma.
- If first impressions matter, it is also useful to avoid becoming the kind of person who fixes an impression in the first 7 seconds.
- A competent manager matters, but it is wiser to be able to do good work even under an average or absent manager.
- People resist the principle because they like consistency and do not question their default programming.
- Analogies are useful for explaining decisions, but not for making decisions.
