Holism over Reductionism: Classical Chinese thought rejects isolating parts; everything interconnects via qi, yin-yang, and wuxing (Five Phases: Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, Water), mirroring systems theory's emphasis on wholes.

Dynamic Balance: Taoist yin-yang cycles and Confucian harmony (e.g., datong 大同 ideal society) prefigure feedback and equilibrium in systems models.

Chinese systems thinking roots in holistic, interconnected views from classical philosophy, aligning perfectly with modern concepts like feedback loops and emergence. The characters: [繫/系 hai](/notes/etimologia-de-hai-xi-7e6b/) (to tie, to bind) and [統 tong](/notes/etimologia-de-tong-tong-7d71/) (the master thread drawn from the silkworm cocoon).
