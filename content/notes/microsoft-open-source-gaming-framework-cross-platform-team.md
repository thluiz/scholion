Microsoft is described as shifting toward openness and cross-platform support in gaming. A new team called “New Devices and Gaming” is tied to a job posting for a Software Development Engineer II, and the posting says the team will create a modern framework that is open source, lightweight, extensible and scalable across Windows Store, Windows Phone, iOS and Android.

The stated goal is to “win back our game developers from our competitors,” and the work is framed as enabling end-to-end gaming scenarios with internal teams and external partners. The article reads this as a move away from a locked-in Xbox model and as part of a broader Microsoft strategy built around services across platforms.
