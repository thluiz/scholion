The article says changes in salary should lead to a deliberate reset of spending habits. It treats both higher and lower income as moments to adjust expectations, protect financial stability, and keep spending aligned with the new situation.

## Reading notes

- When income rises, do not raise lifestyle expectations immediately; keep some room for guilt-free spending, but first strengthen future security.
- Build an emergency fund before spending extra money elsewhere, with three to six months of expenses as the target.
- Use automation to direct a percentage of income toward debt repayment and savings, especially for high-interest debts.
- When income falls, adjust your mindset to the new standard of living and keep spending in line with the lower income.
- Cut recurring expenses, reduce large discretionary costs, and avoid trying to slash every expense at once.
- Automate bills and savings so you can see what remains after fixed costs and keep saving in smaller amounts.
- If needed, sell unused belongings or rent out extra space to create temporary cash.
- Increase side income through extra jobs, online tasks, or freelancing to recover lost earnings.
