The post argues that remote work gets harder when work and home blur together. It recommends creating a routine that replaces the commute, taking breaks during the day, and reserving time for focused work.

## Reading notes

- Remote work can seem easy at first, but it becomes harder if it is not taken seriously.
- Replace the commute with a routine that marks the start and end of the workday.
- Use that transition time for something like the gym, coffee and a book, or meditation.
- Breaks are necessary because working from home makes it easy to keep grinding for hours.
- Breaks help organize the mind and support mental health.
- Examples of breaks include walking the dog, making a snack, exercising for 10 minutes, reading articles, and getting coffee.
- Set aside time for focused work when a task needs full attention.
- Schedule meetings around that block of time.
- Remove distractions, disable notifications, and get started on the task.
