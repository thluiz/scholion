The post introduces Virtual Coffee’s November 2023 community blogging challenge, inspired by National Novel Writing Month. Instead of each person writing 50,000 words, the community writes together, welcoming both experienced and new tech writers. The official focus topic is building a personal brand, with the goal of compiling a guide and related resources by the end of the month. Participants are encouraged to set a personal goal, share it and their posts with the community, and write about tech topics that interest them or that they are learning.
