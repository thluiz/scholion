The post introduces a free Fur Brush Set for SketchBook users and says it helps paint semi-realistic or realistic fur faster than traditional methods. It illustrates the set with a quick gray wolf study and says the same brushes can be used for different furry creatures.

## Reading notes

- The set has thirteen brushes for adding fur texture to animal drawings.
- The example starts with the wolf’s basic shape and colors, using a quick and messy base.
- Short Fur or Short Sparse Fur is used on the muzzle and face.
- Brush strokes should follow the natural direction of fur growth.
- Thick Fur Wisps is used in horizontal sweeps along the sides of the wolf.
- Longer brushes such as Wavy Fur and Straight Fur can fill out other fur textures.
- Rough Grainy Eraser is used to avoid harsh eraser lines.
- Fur Smudge Brush and Strand Brush add finishing wisps of fur.
- SketchBook Pro members can share and install weekly free brush sets in the desktop app.
- In SketchBook version 8, double-clicking the .skbrushes file installs it automatically.
