Microsoft Edge’s enterprise new tab page is meant to reduce the time workers spend searching across files, people, sites, and conversations. When signed in with an Azure Active Directory work account, it shows a personalized mix of Office documents, internal websites, company resources, and other Microsoft 365 content. The page also lets users switch between Office 365 content and Microsoft News.

The layout combines Microsoft Search in Bing, dynamic site tiles, recommended content, and recent files and sites. It uses the Office 365 graph to surface relevant documents and collaboration signals, while some tile data is computed locally on the device. The post also says the experience is designed to keep data within organizational boundaries and includes admin policies for content, tiles, and layout control.

## Reading notes

- The page is designed to help users find work information faster in Microsoft Edge.
- A work account in Azure Active Directory unlocks a personalized new tab page.
- Microsoft Search in Bing searches both the web and the intranet for people, documents, and internal sites.
- Dynamic site tiles show frequently used websites and can be partly configured by IT administrators.
- Recommended content uses Office 365 graph signals to surface files that are likely to matter now.
- Recent files and sites are grouped into Recent, Pinned, Shared with me, and Discover views.
- The enterprise new tab experience includes compliance-oriented M365 services and keeps data inside the organization’s boundaries.
