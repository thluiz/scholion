## Reading notes

- Vic Ramon’s tutorial is described as the most complete and up-to-date option listed, and the post says it is worth the effort to follow.
- Matt Lehner’s writeup explains how Ember concepts differ from Rails, especially Views and Controllers.
- Miguel Madero’s talk shows a gradual move from a traditional Rails and jQuery app to Ember.js, with two takeaways: start small by replacing one component, and think carefully about how to break up the domain model.
- The video is noted as slow to start, so the post tells readers to skip ahead to 6:30.
- Thoughtbot’s article on EmberJS with a separate Rails API offers a practical introduction based on a real example.
- From the Thoughtbot piece, the post pulls two takeaways: using a simple Rails app with ember-rails to build and serve the client app, and practical testing advice with Mocha, Chai, Konacha, and Teaspoon.
- EmberWatch.com is presented as a regularly updated collection of Ember.js articles and resources.
