The page argues that managers can feel a lack of motivation at work during troubling times, especially amid COVID-19, and that there is no single playbook for fixing it. Instead, it recommends small, practical moves that help a person lean back into the work in front of them.

## Reading notes

- Create a wedge of time and space that belongs only to you, away from email, Slack, news, and other demands.
- Admit that the weight feels heavier than usual and that you are operating with less fuel.
- Reconnect with the parts of the job that first made you want the role, including the people and the work itself.
- Look toward the customers or clients who benefit from the work, and use that connection to renew motivation.
- Step away from the work to gain perspective, reflect, and give something back to the team by doing so.
