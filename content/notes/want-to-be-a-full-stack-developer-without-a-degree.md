The article argues that a degree is not required to show you can work as a full-stack developer. Instead, it recommends building visible proof of your skills through projects, deployment, writing, experience, and a clear way to present everything together.

## Reading notes

- Build one large project that includes frontend, backend, and database, and make it strong enough to discuss in interviews.
- Put that project on GitHub and write an excellent README that explains what it does and which stack you used.
- Deploy something to the cloud so an employer can see that you understand how to use a cloud service.
- Write one or two blog posts to strengthen your knowledge and show communication skills and personality.
- Get work experience in any practical form you can, including side projects for other people.
- Show personality through projects, articles, social media, a colorful CV, or YouTube videos.
- Create a website that brings together your projects, articles, and social links.
- Keep a strong drive and determination, since the article says that is what helps everything else work.
