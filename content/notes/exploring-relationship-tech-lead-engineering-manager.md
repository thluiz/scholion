The article compares the EM/PM and EM/TL relationships and argues that the common “shared accountability, split responsibility” model works for EM and PM, but breaks down for EM and TL. For EM and TL, the author proposes the inverse: the Engineering Manager is solely accountable for the team’s technical direction, while the Tech Lead shares responsibility by extending the EM’s bandwidth on specific scopes.

This model is presented as more effective than a rigid split where the EM handles people and process and the TL owns technical decisions. The TL can help by proposing platform improvements, leading a project, or serving as the main technical interface across teams, while the EM remains the final decision-maker and keeps ownership of the technical agenda.

- The EM and TL consult each other on tradeoffs and prioritization, and the EM decides when there is no consensus.
