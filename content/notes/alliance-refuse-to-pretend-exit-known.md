**01:23:30** — A politics that takes the deadlock seriously (host outro)

Amari's closing reading in the outro. Stripped of the Gulag jokes and the Lee Kuan Yew admiration, what Žižek leaves the listener with is a single shape: a politics whose starting assumption is that the situation is genuinely deadlocked and that no existing tendency — liberal, left or right — has the resources to unlock it. The strange left/intelligent-conservative coalition is not a synthesis but a recognition: an alliance of those who refuse to pretend the exit is already known.
