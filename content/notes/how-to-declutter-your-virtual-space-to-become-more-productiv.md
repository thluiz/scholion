The article argues that decluttering digital spaces matters as much as organizing physical spaces, especially during the pandemic when people spend more time on devices. It says virtual clutter distracts people, slows them down, and reduces productivity.

## Reading notes

- Organize the computer desktop by deleting unneeded files, creating folders, and moving files you want to keep into Evernote.
- Clear the downloads folder by deleting unnecessary files, removing duplicates, and moving useful files into Evernote.
- Uninstall apps and programs that are not used, and keep only the shortcuts that matter most on the dock or taskbar.
- Delete emails you no longer need and forward ones you want to keep to Evernote so they can be found later.
- Unsubscribe from e-newsletters that no longer interest you, since they add clutter to the inbox and slow down searches.
- Empty the trash and recycling bin regularly, especially if it contains large files that take up storage.
- Unfollow social media accounts that do not add much value, especially if they are negative or uninteresting.
