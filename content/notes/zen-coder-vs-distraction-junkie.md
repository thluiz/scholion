The post contrasts a zen coder, who works in focused blocks and takes real breaks, with a distraction junkie coder, who opens email, news, and social media during short waits and loses focus. It frames this as a productivity and well-being issue for anyone working with computers.

The author says waiting time during compiling or similar operations should be used for a micro-break that rests the mind and body. Suggested actions include getting up, stretching, closing the eyes, breathing, and drinking water, while avoiding RSS, news, email, social media, and videos.
