This page is the main episode index for the "This Developer's Life" podcast, hosted by Rob Conery and Scott Hanselman. It lists episodes from 2010 through 2023, each centered on a theme that connects software developers' work with their personal experiences, beliefs and challenges.

## Reading notes

- The show is hosted by Rob Conery and Scott Hanselman and presents itself as developers sharing their lives.
- Episode 5.0.2 "Reboot" (Dec 25, 2023) frames life as a sequence of chapters, from childhood to adulthood and possibly parenthood.
- Episode 5.0.1 "Voice" (Nov 6, 2023) discusses what happens when someone's voice gains an audience and whether to provoke or create opportunities with it.
- Episode 4.0.1 "Faith" (Jun 24, 2015) explores how faith motivates people, including faith in oneself and one's abilities, and questions how to know if one is on the right path, through two stories.
- Episode 3.0.2 "Space" (Dec 10, 2013) asks why humans are not on Mars and have not returned to the moon, and features conversations with Paul Lutus, Dan Bricklin, Tamar Cohen, plus audio from Ray Bradbury.
- Episode 3.0.1 "Cancer" (Jan 31, 2013) mentions gratitude from the host and his wife for support after a cancer announcement and reflects on a difficult year.
- Episode 2.0.9 "Drama" (Sep 15, 2012) looks at the origins of drama, how people react to it, and how much drama can come from something as small as a semicolon, focusing on "The Great Semicolon Affair".
- Episode 2.0.8 "Learn" (Apr 9, 2012) questions how many "empty brain cells" people have, how capable they are of learning something entirely new while retaining knowledge, and features two developers who use their learning ability for fun and career success.
- Episode 2.0.7 "Dinosaurs" (Jan 4, 2012) challenges the idea that using old software and languages makes someone obsolete, as guests discuss Fortran, DataFlex and the Commodore 64 still doing useful work.
- Episode 2.0.6 "Play" (Nov 15, 2011) asks what developers do away from the screen, focusing on obsessions that occupy them on the way home, with David Heinemeier Hansson and Pete Brown as guests.
- Episode 2.0.5 "Typo" (Sep 19, 2011) focuses on typefaces, why some people care deeply about them, and explores online reading with a leading figure in typefaces and fonts.
- Episode 2.0.4 "Taste" (Aug 11, 2011) examines what taste and style are, with Scott and Rob admitting they do not know how to get them but recognizing their importance, and talking to a designer and a developer committed to spreading good taste.
- Episode 2.0.3 "Education" (Jul 8, 2011) has Scott and Rob discuss the value of a degree and talk with two developers whose passion helped them finish school and launch careers.
- Episode 2.0.2 "Pressure" (May 13, 2011) features the StackOverflow team recounting a day their database failed and how they responded under pressure.
- Episode 2.0.1 "Criticism" (Apr 22, 2011) covers stories from three developers about giving and receiving criticism and includes a conversation with a strong critic of the podcast itself.
- Episode 1.1.5 "Revolt" (Mar 22, 2011) is a special episode about the Egyptian revolution, featuring Remon Zakaria, who writes software by day and takes part in overthrowing the government at night, based on many recorded interviews.
- Episode 1.1.4 "Obsession" (Mar 3, 2011) presents Rob Sullivan describing how his obsessiveness helps and hinders his job, and Rory Blythe discussing his compulsion to keep learning, which he has fully embraced, in conversation with Scott.
- Episode 1.1.3 "Competition" (Feb 10, 2011) talks to people who are both competitors and programmers, asking whether coders are predisposed to competition, with stories from Jon Skeet, David Fowler, Aaron Jensen and Danielle Banks.
- Episode 1.1.2 "Drive" (Jan 28, 2011) has Scott and Rob talk to four entrepreneurs (Dave Nielsen, Tom Preston-Werner, Nate and Niki Kohari) about the risks they take and the outcomes.
- Episode 1.1.1 "Scars" (Jan 7, 2011) features Chad Myers, Chad Fowler and Doug Rohrer discussing scars from dead-end projects, and includes Sara Chipps's first contribution interviewing Scott Reynolds.
- Episode 1.1.0 "Disconnecting" (Dec 17, 2010) follows Scott going to Disneyland with his wife and two sons while the other host spends a week with his daughters while their mother is on a retreat in California.
- Episode 1.0.9 "Management" (Dec 3, 2010) talks with technical people who moved into management and what that transition means to them.
- Episode 1.0.8 "Motivation" (Nov 19, 2010) features two people with very different stories discussing what motivates them.
- Episode 1.0.7 "Audacity" (Nov 3, 2010) highlights three developers who accomplished bold, "audacious" actions.
- Episode 1.0.6 "Abstraction" (Oct 20, 2010) asks what a software developer needs to know, how many abstraction layers they must understand, and how many "geek trading cards" they should have, through interviews with three prominent developers.
- Episode 1.0.5 "Homerun" (Oct 11, 2010) includes a story where Rob talks to David Heinemeier Hansson about the inspiration for Ruby on Rails and how he guided the project.
- Episode 1.0.4 "Being Mean" (Oct 2, 2010) starts with Scott's story of being listed multiple times on a "what not to do to code" list at a previous company.
- Episode 1.0.2 "Fame and Notoriety" (Sep 2, 2010) features a conversation with John Sheehan, a developer active on Twitter who is building a presence in the open source community.
- Episode 1.0.1 "Getting Fired" (Aug 18, 2010) has the host talk with two developers and then add his own story at the end.
